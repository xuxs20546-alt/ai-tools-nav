Overview

DreamStudio is the official online Stable Diffusion generation platform launched by Stability AI, opened to the public in 2022 alongside the release of the Stable Diffusion open-source model. It lets users without a local GPU experience Stable Diffusion too: enter a prompt on the web page, adjust the parameters, and generate images. Unlike tools that chase a "dumbed-down" experience, DreamStudio keeps professional parameters such as steps, resolution, and seed — for many people it is the entry point for systematically learning prompts and diffusion model parameters for the first time. Stability AI has now shifted its product focus to platform.stability.ai and Stable Assistant, while DreamStudio’s account and credit system remain connected to the official platform.

Core Features

  • Text-to-image: enter a prompt to generate images, with support for positive and negative prompts — the most basic and core capability of Stable Diffusion.
  • Fine-grained parameter control: steps, CFG guidance scale, sampler, resolution, seed and more can all be adjusted manually, giving users familiar with the model stable, controllable output.
  • Image-to-image and variants: generate style variants from existing images, or use masks for local inpainting — ideal for reworking existing assets.
  • Model and version selection: choose different versions of the Stable Diffusion model to experience the differences in style and detail.
  • Credit-based billing: generation consumes credits, and different parameter settings cost different amounts; free credits are granted at registration, and you buy more when they run out — no long-term subscription required.

Hands-On Experience

After registering a Stability AI account, you receive initial free credits. Open DreamStudio, type your prompt into the input box, adjust the parameter panel on the right, and click generate to see the result. For beginners, the most important things to learn are prompt writing and the meaning of a few core parameters; for experienced users, precisely controlling the seed and step count makes results highly reproducible. Free credits are mainly for trying things out — before batch generation, it is wise to test parameters on a small sample to avoid wasting credits. A common workflow is to lock the seed and steps first to keep the style stable, then gradually adjust the prompt and observe the changes. This "reproducible" nature is exactly what most distinguishes SD-family tools from black-box generators.

Pricing Plans

DreamStudio uses a credit system: according to the official platform, 1 credit is roughly equal to $0.01 (on the API side), and registration grants 25 free credits; credits within DreamStudio are obtained through a monthly subscription. Generation cost depends on the parameters — early official materials show that generating one image at default settings costs about 0.2 credits. Pros and Cons

Pros: officially produced and consistent with the Stable Diffusion ecosystem; parameter freedom is among the best of online tools, ideal for learning how the model works; the flexible credit system keeps costs manageable for light users.

Cons: not beginner-friendly — you need to understand concepts like steps and CFG; free credits are scarce, and deeper exploration requires payment; since Stability AI shifted its focus, updates to DreamStudio’s interface and features have slowed down.

Who It’s For

  • Hobbyists who want to systematically learn Stable Diffusion
  • Creators who need fine control over generation parameters
  • Developers testing official models and APIs
  • Technical users interested in the open-source model ecosystem

FAQ

Q: How many free credits do you get at registration?

A: According to the official platform, registration grants 25 free credits for trying things out.

Q: How many credits does it take to generate one image?

A: It depends on the parameter settings; default settings consume fewer credits, while higher resolution and more steps consume more.

Q: Can generated images be used commercially?

A: The Stable Diffusion model itself is commercial-friendly; please refer to Stability AI’s license terms for details.

Q: What is the relationship between DreamStudio and Stable Diffusion?

A: Stable Diffusion is the model open-sourced by Stability AI, and DreamStudio is the official online platform that uses this model.

Q: Is DreamStudio still worth using for new users?

A: Stability AI has shifted its focus to the new official platform and Stable Assistant; DreamStudio still works normally but offers limited new features. Users who want a continuously updated experience can follow the new official platform.
