🧭 Tool Overview

MiniMax (Xiyu Technology) is an AI large-model company headquartered in Shanghai, founded in late 2021, and one of the so-called "Six Little Dragons" of China's AI large-model startups. The company develops its own multimodal large models and offers model APIs as well as consumer-grade products to the global market, the most well-known being Hailuo AI, which excels at video generation. On January 9, 2026, MiniMax was officially listed on the Main Board of the Hong Kong Stock Exchange (stock code 0100.HK), raising approximately HK$4.82 billion — reportedly setting a new global record for the IPO scale of an AI large-model company at the time, with its share price more than doubling on the first trading day.

The problem MiniMax solves is straightforward: it lets everyday users generate short videos with coherent visuals and natural camera movement from text or images, and lets developers integrate multimodal capabilities into their applications at relatively low cost. In September 2024, the company reported that its large models handled 3 billion daily interactions and processed 3 trillion tokens per day. Since then, its video products have consistently ranked near the top of global AI video tool charts, making it one of the most representative players in China's AI video space.

⚙️ Core Features

Video generation (Hailuo series): The core capability of Hailuo AI is text-to-video and image-to-video generation. On June 18, 2025, MiniMax released a new generation of Hailuo video models, adding native 1080p video creation and rolling it out simultaneously on the Hailuo video web app, mobile app, and open-platform API. Users can enter a Chinese description and the model generates a video clip with camera movement, subject motion, and scene changes.

First/last frame and instruction control: On the API side, the Hailuo models support first/last frame technology — users specify the starting and ending frames, and the model automatically fills in the dynamic transition in between, which suits creative scenarios that require precise control over how a shot begins and ends. The company emphasizes that the model can understand text instructions that go beyond what is shown in the image, delivering "what you write is what you shoot" — complex storyboard descriptions can be broken down and rendered in the footage.

Multimodal conversation and creation: The Hailuo AI app also offers text chat and image generation, powered by MiniMax's self-developed abab / MiniMax-01 series of large models, with strong Chinese understanding and generation quality as a key advantage. Developers can also call a range of model interfaces — chat, voice, video, and more — through the open platform.

Open source and ecosystem: MiniMax has released some of its large models in open-source form for community self-deployment and research; it also distributes model capabilities through multiple cloud platforms beyond Volcano Engine and overseas channels, serving content creators, marketing teams, and developers of apps going global.

In public feedback from the creator community, Hailuo video earns high marks for camera-movement coherence and subject consistency, and long Chinese prompts can be entered directly without translating them into English first — a real convenience for domestic users. Model capabilities are also iterated through version updates; for example, since September 2025, first/last frame generation has been available through the open-platform API, letting professional creators precisely control the starting and ending frames of a shot for finer-grained production. Overall, MiniMax follows a dual-track "consumer product + open platform" strategy: ordinary users get a polished product, and developers get a complete integration channel.

🖥️ Getting Started

To use Hailuo AI for the first time, visit the official website or sign up with your phone number to enter the main interface. The video creation entry is usually in the "Hailuo Video" section: write a description of the scene — subject, setting, lighting, camera movement — choose the aspect ratio and duration, then hit generate. Generation typically takes anywhere from tens of seconds to several minutes, after which you can preview and download the result directly.

Other common workflows include: browsing finished works and prompts from other users in the "Inspiration" or community sections and applying them with one tap; editing prompts and regenerating results you are not satisfied with; or using first/last frame to lock the opening and closing shots. For users who need volume, you can create an API Key on the open platform, call the video generation interface per the official documentation, and pipe the results into your own workflow. Note that specs like 1080p and longer durations usually map to higher membership tiers or API costs; the free quota only covers basic generation.

For teams, the open platform provides usage statistics, balance management, and call logs to help control costs; individual users can manage past generations in "Creation History" and favorite or archive good results. If this is your first time with AI video, start with the free tier and simple static scene descriptions to learn how the model outputs, then gradually try complex instructions like camera moves, transitions, and character actions — this makes it easier to understand the relationship between prompts and the final footage.

💰 Pricing Plans

Hailuo AI's consumer side is subscription-based: the free tier offers a daily basic quota, and paid memberships come in multiple tiers, each with different limits on resolution, duration, and generation count. After the June 2025 membership price adjustment, the top-tier "Ultimate" plan costs ¥10,788 per year, which sparked discussions about pricing being too high; the company responded that this is already the industry floor. On the developer side, billing is based on actual API usage, and the company has long promoted its video generation API pricing as among the lowest in the industry.

👍 Pros and Cons

Pros: Video generation quality and Chinese semantic understanding rank in the first tier among comparable products; a complete product line covering chat, image, and video; aggressive API pricing that is developer-friendly; and the company is publicly listed, so long-term product maintenance is assured.

Cons: The top-tier membership costs nearly ¥10,000 a year, making heavy personal use expensive; generated results have an element of randomness, and complex scenes often require multiple re-rolls; and some advanced specs and features are limited to higher-priced tiers, so free users can only experience a narrow range.

🎯 Who It's For

Short-video creators and self-media operators who need to produce visual assets quickly; advertising and marketing teams using AI video for creative previsualization and batch testing; film, TV, and animation professionals doing concept validation and storyboard previews in pre-production; and developers and product teams that need to integrate video generation into apps or websites.

❓ FAQ

Is Hailuo AI free? There is a free tier with a daily quota — you can experience basic generation without paying; higher-quality specs require membership or API payment.

Can generated content be used commercially? Commercial licensing and copyright ownership are subject to the terms of service on the official website, and may vary by tier.

How is the video generation API priced? Billed by tokens and generation specs; the company has published video model pricing on the order of ¥0.015 per 1,000 tokens — check the open platform for real-time quotes.

How long a video can be generated at once? Duration specs vary by model version and membership tier; refer to the in-product options.

Does Hailuo AI have a mobile app? Yes — web and mobile apps are available, with creation history synced across devices, so you can generate and browse anytime, anywhere.
