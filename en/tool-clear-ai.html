Tool Overview

ClearMetal was originally an independent AI supply chain visibility startup founded by Adam Compain in San Francisco in 2014. Its core technology used machine learning to analyze ocean freight logistics data, helping companies track cargo locations in real time, predict arrival times, and identify supply chain bottlenecks. In May 2021, global supply chain visibility platform project44 acquired ClearMetal, integrating its AI prediction engine into project44's "Movement" intelligent platform. ClearMetal no longer operates as an independent brand, but its core predictive analytics capabilities are now deeply embedded in project44's product portfolio, becoming a key technology asset in project44's AI-driven supply chain decision intelligence. project44 positions itself as a "supply chain decision intelligence platform," serving global companies including Amazon, Lenovo, and Panasonic.

Core Features

Real-time cargo tracking and visualization: after integrating ClearMetal's data processing engine, project44 can connect to real-time data from global ocean carriers, ports, and railways, displaying the exact location and status of every shipment on a map. The system supports multimodal visibility — whether cargo is in ocean, rail, or truck transport, it can all be viewed in a single dashboard.

AI arrival time prediction (ETA): this is ClearMetal's core technology asset — by analyzing historical voyage data, port congestion, weather patterns, and carrier operating behavior, the AI predicts actual cargo arrival times with significantly higher accuracy than carriers' official ETAs. For manufacturers requiring precise production scheduling and inventory management, accurate arrival forecasts directly impact production line schedules and warehousing costs.

Anomaly detection and automatic alerts: when the system detects potential delays (e.g., a vessel deviating from its planned route, port congestion warnings, customs clearance anomalies), it automatically sends alert notifications to relevant parties, along with estimated delay times and recommended response actions.

Supply chain performance analytics: project44 provides carrier performance scores — quantitative ratings of carriers and logistics providers based on historical on-time rates, damage rates, and response speed, helping companies optimize carrier selection.

Getting Started Experience

project44 is a B2B SaaS platform for enterprise users; the process typically starts with a sales consultation. After enterprise customers discuss their needs with project44's sales team, they receive a customized demo and trial environment. During formal deployment, project44's implementation team helps customers with data integration — connecting their ERP/TMS systems to the platform, configuring the cargo types and routes to track, and setting up alert rules and report templates. In daily use, logistics managers log into the project44 console and first see a global supply chain overview dashboard — shipment statistics categorized by status (normal/attention/delayed), a list of this week's key anomaly events, and supply chain health indicators. Clicking any shipment drills into its full journey, ETA prediction, and all historical events. The platform has a moderate learning curve, typically requiring 1-2 weeks of system training.

Pricing

project44 uses enterprise-level custom pricing, and no specific prices are published on its website. Pricing is typically based on the shipment volume tracked, the number of transport modes connected (ocean/air/ground), and the combination of feature modules needed. Industry estimates put the base annual fee in the tens of thousands to hundreds of thousands of US dollars. After acquiring ClearMetal in 2021, project44's valuation exceeded US$2.4 billion, positioning it as a leading platform in the global supply chain visibility space.

The prices above are industry estimates, not official published pricing. Actual prices are subject to project44's contract quote.

Strengths and Limitations

Strengths: project44 leads the industry in multimodal visibility coverage — the number of carrier, port, and railway data sources it connects to is among the richest of comparable platforms. After integrating ClearMetal's AI prediction engine, ETA forecast accuracy has gained market recognition. For large enterprises' supply chain digital transformation, the ROI is clear — accurate arrival forecasts can significantly reduce safety stock costs and production line downtime risk.

Limitations: the price barrier is extremely high, creating significant cost pressure for small and mid-sized import/export businesses. Deployment cycles are long — from contract signing to full rollout typically takes weeks to months. The platform's complexity means it requires a dedicated logistics team to manage and operate, and it is not suitable for companies without dedicated logistics analysts. In the Chinese market, project44's localization coverage (such as real-time data integration with Chinese carriers and ports) may fall short of domestic solutions.

FAQ

Q: What is the current relationship between ClearMetal and project44?
A: ClearMetal was acquired by project44 in 2021, and its AI prediction engine has been integrated into the project44 platform. ClearMetal is no longer sold as a standalone product.

Q: Is project44 suitable for small businesses?
A: project44's target customers are mainly mid-to-large enterprises with annual logistics spending of several million US dollars or more. Small businesses may be better served by usage-based tools such as Flexport or Freightos.

Q: Does project44's data cover Chinese ports?
A: project44 covers major ports worldwide, but the depth and update frequency of real-time data integration for Chinese ports may differ from local solutions (such as YunQuNa).

Q: Does using project44 require installing hardware?
A: No. project44 obtains data through API integration with carriers, ports, and railway systems — no hardware installation is required on the customer side.
