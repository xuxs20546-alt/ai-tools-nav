One-line verdict: DeepBrain AI is a veteran player in the digital human video space. AI Studios is easy to get started with and covers many languages, making it a good fit for teams that need bulk presenter videos — just estimate your video minutes before picking a plan.

Tool Overview

DeepBrain AI is an AI company headquartered in South Korea. Its core product, AI Studios, is an AI digital human video creation platform for businesses and individuals. Users only need to enter a text script and choose an avatar to generate presenter videos — no on-camera talent, recording equipment, or editing experience required. The platform supports avatars and voices in multiple languages, and offers pre-built video templates, text-to-speech (TTS), and 3D avatars. It is widely used for corporate training, marketing videos, news broadcasting, and e-commerce live selling. For teams that need continuous video content without relying on human presenters, it is one of the representative products in the digital human video space today.

Key Features

  • Text-to-avatar video: The core capability is text-driven presenter video generation. Pick an avatar, enter a script, set the speaking speed and pauses, and the system automatically generates a broadcast-style video with lip-synced mouth movements.
  • Multilingual support: Offers digital human voices in multiple languages and accents, making it easy to create multilingual content for different markets. Global teams can produce multilingual versions through a single workflow. In practice, switching languages only requires changing the voice option in the project settings — lip sync and intonation adjust automatically, with no need to re-record or re-edit.
  • Text-to-speech (TTS): Even without visuals, you can use the platform's voice synthesis to generate narration for video voiceovers, audio content, and more.
  • Pre-built video templates: Includes templates for news broadcasting, product introductions, training explainers, and more. Apply a template, swap in your copy, and you're ready to render — ideal for batch production.
  • 3D avatars: Beyond standard 2D avatars, the platform offers 3D digital human options, suitable for presentations that need a stronger sense of immersion.

Getting Started

After registering at aistudios.com, you enter the workspace, which is organized around "New Project": choose a template or a blank project, pick an avatar, enter or paste your copy in the script editor, adjust speed, pauses, and background, then click generate. The system first renders a preview; once you're satisfied, export the final video. For first-time users, starting with a template is recommended, as it helps you quickly understand how script segments relate to lip sync. The whole workflow is browser-based and requires no video editing experience — you can usually finish your first video quite quickly, though the exact time depends on the render queue. If you're unsure about your script, refer to the structure of the built-in templates and split your copy into short "opening — key points — closing" segments; this usually sounds more natural than long blocks of text. The platform also lets you rewrite existing company content into presenter scripts, making it easy to convert legacy content into video.

The official pricing page shows the Personal plan starting at about $24/month and the Team plan at about $55/month; earlier third-party sources listed an entry plan at around $30/month including about 10 minutes of video. Pros and Limitations

Pros: Avatars look fairly natural and lip sync is mature; multilingual support is friendly for teams creating content for global markets; the template-based workflow is well suited to batch output of presenter videos; and no human talent is needed, significantly reducing the time and cost of video production. For content channels that need a consistent on-screen presence, digital humans also keep the look and presenting style consistent — something live-action filming finds hard to match.

Limitations: Subscription pricing is not cheap, and heavy usage costs add up quickly; advanced features such as custom avatars and personal cloning require extra payment; generated videos carry a platform watermark on some plans, and removing it requires an upgrade. In addition, avatar gestures and facial expressions are still less expressive than real people, so emotionally intense content should be used with care.
