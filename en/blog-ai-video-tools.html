AI Video Generation Tools Guide: The Magic from Text to Video

2025-04-28 · 8 min read · 🎬

AI Video Generation: The Magic from Text to Moving Images Is Happening

2024-2025 marks the "iPhone moment" for AI video generation. A year ago, AI-generated videos still had a distinct "dreamlike" quality—objects morphing, physics violations, temporal jumps. Now, top AI video models can generate a full minute of coherent video with convincing character movements, lighting changes, and physics. Sora's release ignited the global imagination for "text-to-video," while domestic tools like Kling AI and Jimeng AI are catching up and surpassing at remarkable speed.

This article provides an in-depth review of today's most powerful AI video generation tools, comparing them across four dimensions: technical capability, cost, Chinese language support, and ideal use cases.

Sora — The Industry-Defining Benchmark

When OpenAI's Sora debuted in early 2024, it shocked the entire tech world. A demo video of "a woman walking through Tokyo streets" showcased AI's unprecedented understanding of the physical world—natural lighting variations, the physical sensation of clothing movement, character consistency across different angles. This was the turning point where AI video generation moved from "collage" to "understanding."

Core Capabilities

Sora's most critical technical breakthrough is its world model—it doesn't simply "stitch pixels together" but builds a deep understanding of 3D space, physical laws, and temporal continuity. This allows Sora to far exceed competitors in:

  • Physical consistency: Objects don't appear or disappear out of nowhere, lighting changes follow physical principles, characters maintain consistent appearance after turning around.
  • Long-duration coherence: Sora can generate videos up to 1 minute long—the longest of any current AI video tool. Most competitors remain at 5-10 seconds.
  • Camera language: Sora's simulation of camera movement—push, pull, pan, tilt, depth of field changes, wide-to-close-up transitions—reaches an astonishing level.

Access and Usage

By late 2024, Sora was integrated into ChatGPT Plus and Pro subscriptions. Plus users ($20/month) can generate 720P video, Pro users ($200/month) can generate 1080P video without watermarks. Generation speed ranges from 1-3 minutes depending on video length and complexity.

Limitations

Sora's understanding of Chinese prompts is limited (English prompts yield best results). Additionally, while Sora has strong physical understanding of common scenes, it still produces implausible results with complex interactions (multi-person combat, fine tool manipulation). There's also a practical issue—Plus users have limited generation quotas, making high-frequency use costly.

Kling AI (可灵AI) — The Leader in Chinese AI Video

Kling AI, launched by Kuaishou, is the most comprehensively capable Chinese AI video generation tool. In the Chinese video space, it even outperforms Sora across several key dimensions:

Differentiating Advantages

  • Native understanding of Chinese scenes: Kling's generation quality for Chinese urban landscapes, indoor environments, clothing styles, and everyday scenes far exceeds overseas tools. It won't produce strange "foreigners' imagination of China" visuals when generating Chinese-style videos.
  • 1080P native output: Kling supports 1080P resolution video generation, meeting short-video platform publishing requirements.
  • Motion control: This is Kling's most underrated feature—in image-to-video mode, you can specify the movement direction, speed, and trajectory of specific objects in the frame. This gives creators fine-grained control far beyond "input text, wait for result."
  • Image-to-video: Upload a static image and Kling can add dynamic effects—clouds drifting, water flowing, characters making expressions—generating naturally smooth video.

Pricing

Kling offers generous free credits. Paid membership starts at approximately 66 CNY/month. For domestic creators, the value far exceeds Sora.

Runway — The Swiss Army Knife for Professional Video Creators

Runway was one of the earliest companies in the AI video space, and its product positioning is fundamentally different from Sora and Kling: Runway isn't a "text in → video out" one-click tool, but an AI-enhanced platform embedded in the video production workflow.

Core Feature Matrix

The Runway Gen-3 model supports text-to-video, but what truly sets it apart is its companion professional toolset:

  • Motion Brush: "Paint" the direction and area where you want motion on a static image—water ripples, smoke, hair flowing—AI generates dynamic effects only in that region.
  • Green Screen Removal: AI-driven background removal and replacement, more convenient than traditional green screen techniques—no actual green screen needed.
  • Video Expansion: Extend the duration of existing video; AI infers and generates subsequent frames based on the first few frames.
  • Style Transfer: Transform video into a specific visual style (anime, oil painting, cyberpunk, etc.).
  • Frame Interpolation: Generate smooth transition frames between existing video frames for slow-motion effects.

For video editors, VFX artists, ad directors, and other professionals, Runway's "AI toolbox" positioning is more aligned with real workflows than Sora's "one-click generation."

Pricing: Free tier offers limited generation credits, Standard plan $15/month, Pro plan $35/month.

Pika — The Simple, Accessible Entry Point

Pika is the most "friendly" of the AI video generation tools. Its design philosophy is wrapping complex AI capabilities in an extremely simple interface. You don't need to learn prompt engineering—pick a video style template (anime, realistic, 3D, pixel, etc.), enter a short description, click generate, and see results in 30 seconds.

Pika 2.0 introduced some interesting features: Lip Sync—making characters in videos "speak" according to your input audio or text; Sound Effect Generation—AI automatically generates matching sound effects for silent videos (footsteps, wind, rain, etc.).

Pika suits social media content creators—those who need large volumes of short video material daily but don't require film-grade production quality. Free tier available, paid plans from $10/month.

Jimeng AI (即梦AI) — ByteDance's AI Video Play

ByteDance's Jimeng AI (formerly Dreamina) is catching up fast. It's deeply integrated with the Jianying (CapCut) ecosystem—videos generated in Jimeng can be imported into CapCut with one click for editing, subtitling, and music addition. For creators already using the CapCut/Douyin/Toutiao ecosystem, this closed-loop experience is extremely compelling.

Jimeng AI's Chinese understanding is naturally excellent, and both image-to-video and text-to-video quality are rapidly iterating. The free credits are friendly for individual creators.

Scenario-Based Recommendations

Highest quality, overseas projects → Sora (via ChatGPT Plus), the ceiling of video generation. Chinese short videos, domestic creators → Kling AI + Jimeng AI, best Chinese scene understanding and value. Professional video post-production, VFX → Runway, not "one-click generation" but an "AI toolbox." Social media content, quick output → Pika + CapCut, easy to learn and fast to produce. Free first → Kling AI + Jimeng AI + Pika, combining the free credits of these three tools covers most daily needs.

The true value of AI video generation isn't about "replacing photographers and directors" but about "giving everyone with a story to tell the chance to turn the images in their mind into visible footage." When the technical barrier drops to zero, creativity becomes the only barrier.
