Overview

Janitor AI is an AI roleplay and interactive chat platform aimed at consumer users. Users can create custom AI characters (including appearance, personality, backstory, speaking style, etc.) and engage in open-ended text conversations with them. The platform's core positioning is "the intersection of human creativity and AI magic" — it gives creators tools to build immersive virtual worlds and characters, while also offering a space for users who enjoy interacting to explore AI storytelling and roleplay.

Janitor AI initially attracted a large wave of users migrating from Character.AI, differentiating itself as an "uncensored roleplay platform." Unlike Character.AI, Janitor AI lets users power AI characters with their own API keys (such as the OpenAI API) and also offers its own in-house JanitorLLM model as a free built-in option. This flexible design lets users choose high-performance commercial models or a completely free internal model.

Core Features

Custom character creation: Users can create AI characters with detailed settings, including name, gender, personality tags, appearance description, backstory, and conversation style. Character settings can be described in natural language (e.g., "a detective from the Victorian era who speaks with an old-English flair, reclusive but with a strong sense of justice"), and the system generates dialogue consistent with the character's identity based on these settings. Characters can be set to public (other users can chat with them too) or private (visible only to you).

Multi-model support: One of Janitor AI's most distinctive features is letting users choose the underlying model that drives their AI characters. Users can use JanitorLLM (the platform's free in-house model) or connect OpenAI's GPT models, Anthropic's Claude, or other third-party models via API keys. This "BYO (bring your own model)" approach lets users pick the model that best fits their needs and their preference for content freedom.

Public character community: The platform hosts an active public character community where users can browse, search, and interact with tens of thousands of characters created by other creators. Character categories span anime, games, fantasy, romance, sci-fi, and more, with a highly active community.

Custom themes & interface: Janitor AI lets users personalize the look of their chat interface with custom CSS, including fonts, colors, bubble styles, and more, satisfying users' visual customization needs.

Getting Started

Janitor AI's usage flow is primarily browser-based: visit janitorai.com → register an account (email signup supported) → browse public characters or create your own. The character creation flow: fill in the character name and avatar → write the character settings in natural language → choose the driving model → start chatting. The more detailed your character settings (personality, speaking style, knowledge boundaries, forbidden behaviors, etc.), the better the AI's performance matches your expectations.

When using JanitorLLM (the free built-in model), responses typically complete within seconds, and dialogue quality sits at a mid-range level among community-driven platforms. Connecting commercial models (such as GPT-4) significantly improves dialogue quality, but you'll need to cover the API call costs yourself. The platform interface is fairly feature-complete, though information density is high and new users may need some time to adapt.

Pros & Limitations

Strengths: First, the low barrier to using JanitorLLM for free makes it an attractive option for users who don't want to pay. Second, the flexibility of multi-model support — users can freely switch between free and commercial models, choosing the best balance of performance or cost on demand. Third, the active public character community offers plenty of ready-made characters to interact with, lowering the barrier for starting from scratch. Fourth, character settings offer a high degree of freedom, supporting natural-language descriptions and non-standard character configurations.

Limitations: First, JanitorLLM's dialogue quality and stability can fluctuate at times, and its context retention in complex scenarios falls short of commercial models. Second, the platform's content policies and moderation are relatively loose, so the quality of public characters can be uneven. Third, there is no native mobile app; access relies mainly on the browser. Fourth, as a community-driven platform, service stability can occasionally be affected by traffic fluctuations.

FAQ

Q: Is Janitor AI completely free?
Using JanitorLLM (the platform's in-house model) is free, but there's a daily message limit. If you connect commercial models like OpenAI via API, you pay according to that service's pricing.

Q: Which is better, Janitor AI or Character.AI?
It depends on your needs. If you value the convenience of a mobile app, community scale, and conversation stability, Character.AI is the more mature choice. If you care more about content freedom, model-switching flexibility, or using it for free, Janitor AI offers a competitive alternative.

Q: Will my created characters be visible to others?
You can choose public or private when creating a character. Public characters appear in the community directory for other users to interact with; private characters are visible only to you. You can change this option anytime in the character settings.

Q: Is the quality gap between JanitorLLM and GPT models large?
There is a gap. In complex plot advancement, multi-character context tracking, and non-English conversation scenarios, commercial models like GPT-4 clearly outperform JanitorLLM. But for everyday casual chat and simple roleplay scenarios, JanitorLLM is sufficient for most users.

Q: How do I connect Janitor AI with my own API key?
In Janitor AI's settings page, find the "API Settings" option, choose the model provider (such as OpenAI), enter your API key, and save. Afterwards, when chatting with characters, the AI will use your specified model instead of JanitorLLM. Please manage the security of your API key yourself.
