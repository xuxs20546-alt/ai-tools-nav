Overview

Cleanup.pictures is an online image cleanup tool focused on "one-click removal." It first launched on Product Hunt as a free, open-source product, and later gradually shifted to a "free + subscription" model. It solves a very specific problem: previously, removing strangers, clutter, or watermarks from photos required complex operations in Photoshop like the clone stamp and content-aware fill; now you just brush over the target area and the AI automatically infers and rebuilds the occluded background.

The product is delivered as a web app – open the browser and it's ready to use, no download or installation needed. The official site doesn't disclose the company or team behind it, and the feature set is deliberately single-minded: it only does "content removal," and does it extremely well.

Core Features

Brush-based object removal. Brush over unwanted content such as passersby, vehicles, power lines, shadows, or trash bins, and the AI automatically rebuilds the background texture. This is the most common operation for cleaning up travel photos, street shots, and e-commerce images – typically taking only about ten seconds per image.

Watermark and text removal. Watermarks, subtitles, date stamps, and brand logos can all be brushed away, which is handy for reworking downloaded assets, creating course materials, or marketing images. Note that removing watermarks from others' work may involve copyright issues – make sure you have the right to use the material first.

Defect repair. Sensor dust spots, scratches, and creases on old photos are also within scope, which helps when restoring digitized vintage photos.

Processed directly in the browser. No software installation needed – upload an image on the web page and the whole job is done, on virtually any device. Since images are uploaded to servers for processing, be cautious with privacy-sensitive images.

Tiered by image size. The free plan handles smaller images; the paid plan supports images of any size, suitable for users who need to process high-resolution originals.

Hands-on Experience

Open cleanup.pictures, drag an image onto the page, and brush over the areas you want to remove – the brush size is adjustable. When you release the mouse, the AI rebuilds the background within seconds and shows the result, with a before/after comparison. Not satisfied? Keep brushing to layer more edits, then click export to download.

The whole flow is "upload → brush → download" in three steps, with almost no learning curve. Larger images or complex backgrounds take longer to process; if you see unnatural artifacts at the edges of brushed areas, try a smaller brush and process in multiple passes – you'll usually get a cleaner result.

Pros & Limitations

Pros: minimal interaction and zero learning curve – one of the most "use-it-and-leave" products in its category; runs instantly in the browser without consuming local resources; for images with simple backgrounds, removal results are clean and natural, replacing complex manual work in Photoshop.

Limitations: in crowded areas, repetitive textures, or regions with large occlusions, the rebuilt result may show smudges or artifacts; the free plan limits image size, and processing high-res originals requires a subscription; the official site doesn't disclose data handling and privacy details, so weigh the risks for sensitive images.

Who It's For

FAQ

Q: How large an image can the free plan process? A: The official site says the free plan handles smaller images; the subscription supports any size. See the official site for the exact limits.

Q: Will processing reduce image quality? A: The tool does local reconstruction on the uploaded original; edge quality around processed areas depends on the image's own sharpness. We recommend keeping a backup of the original.

Q: Does it support batch processing? A: The tool is designed for fast single-image processing; the official site provides no batch processing instructions, so consider other options for high-volume needs.

Q: Do I need to register an account? A: The web version can be used directly; subscribing and paying requires an account. Follow the official site's process.
