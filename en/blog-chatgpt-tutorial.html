ChatGPT Complete Tutorial: From Registration to Advanced Tips (2025 Edition)

2025-04-28 · 8 min read · 🧠

ChatGPT's Real Value Isn't "Chatting"

With the GPT-4o model released by OpenAI in May 2024, ChatGPT evolved from a "text chatbot" into a multimodal work platform. It can simultaneously understand text, images, audio, and files, and invoke tools like code execution, image generation, and web search within a single conversation. If you're still using it for simple "question and answer" exchanges, you've only unlocked about 20% of its capabilities.

Registration & Model Selection: Choosing the Right Option Saves Money

Free Plan (chat.openai.com): Sign up and use GPT-4o immediately, with a daily free quota (not publicly disclosed; real-world testing shows about 10–20 complex conversations before throttling downgrades you to GPT-4o-mini). Supports text conversations, file uploads (images, PDF, Word, Excel), and DALL·E image generation (limited to 2 images per day).

ChatGPT Plus ($20/month): Upgrades over the Free plan: approximately 5× the GPT-4o usage quota, unlimited DALL·E, ability to create and use custom GPTs, more stable data analysis, and priority access to new features.

ChatGPT Pro ($200/month): Designed for heavy professional users. Includes unlimited access to GPT-4o, the o1-pro reasoning model, and o3-pro (OpenAI's strongest reasoning model to date). Regular users absolutely don't need Pro — unless your work requires dozens of high-complexity reasoning tasks daily.

Team ($25/person/month) and Enterprise: Enterprise-grade features — data not used for training, admin console, SSO single sign-on. Choose these if your team handles sensitive data.

7 Easily Overlooked Yet Extremely Useful Features

1. Data Analysis (Code Interpreter) — A Severely Underrated Feature

Upload CSV/Excel/JSON files and ChatGPT automatically writes Python code to analyze them. This isn't simply "read the data and summarize" — it can perform descriptive statistics, correlation analysis, regression, clustering, time series analysis, and generate high-quality matplotlib/seaborn visualization charts.

A validated workflow: Upload raw data → Have ChatGPT perform data exploration (field descriptions, missing value statistics, distribution overview) → Determine analysis direction based on exploration results → Analyze item by item and generate charts → Compile the analysis process and conclusions into a report. The entire workflow can complete in 30 minutes what would take a junior data analyst 2 days.

Note: The Free plan's file analysis uses GPT-4o-mini, which has a capability gap compared to GPT-4o. If data analysis is a frequent use case for you, Plus is worth subscribing to.

2. Custom GPTs — Build Your Own AI Toolkit

No programming required. On the "Explore GPTs" page, click "+ Create," describe your desired GPT behavior rules in natural language, upload knowledge base files (your company's product documentation, writing style guide, industry terminology, etc.), and a customized AI assistant is ready.

Three High-Value GPT Examples:

  • "Company Email Assistant": Upload your company's email templates and brand tone guide → Set rules: "Use formal, professional tone for all replies; always use the company signature at the end; for emails involving quotes, explicitly state whether prices include tax."
  • "Personal Learning Tutor": Tell it what you're learning, your current level, and learning style preferences → Whenever you encounter an unfamiliar concept, ask it directly → It remembers your progress and previous discussions.
  • "Meeting Minutes Officer": Upload raw meeting recording transcripts → GPT automatically identifies speakers, extracts decisions and action items, and outputs structured results by agenda item.

3. Image Analysis — More Than Just "Recognizing What's in the Image"

In practice, GPT-4o's vision capabilities can: digitize and organize handwritten notes, review UI designs from screenshots, reconstruct whiteboard sketches into proposals, and analyze reference image styles.

Practical example: Take a photo of a whiteboard brainstorming session → "Organize my whiteboard notes into a structured Markdown document in mind map format. If relationships between certain nodes aren't clearly drawn on the whiteboard, mark them as [TBD]."

4. Web Search — Manually Triggered

ChatGPT's web search requires manually clicking the "Search" button below the input field to activate. It doesn't automatically decide whether to search. Many users are unaware of this design detail and assume ChatGPT can't access the internet at all.

5. DALL·E Image Generation — Available Even on the Free Plan

Simply say "Generate an image of XXX" in the conversation, and ChatGPT automatically invokes DALL·E. Compared to Midjourney, DALL·E's strength is high adherence to text instructions (precise control over elements and their positional relationships in the image); its weakness is that artistic feel and lighting texture aren't as good as MJ. It's suitable for illustrations, diagrams, and concept images that require precise expression of certain concepts — not for artistic creation.

6. Conversation Search — Your ChatGPT History Is Searchable

The search box at the top of the left sidebar can search all your conversation history. This feature becomes incredibly valuable when you have hundreds of conversation threads — a previously discussed idea doesn't need to be re-explained; just search for the keyword and continue from that conversation.

7. Voice Conversation (Advanced Voice Mode) — Chat Like a Phone Call

ChatGPT's mobile voice conversation mode supports interruptions, features natural pauses and tonal variations, and can express emotions (excitement/hesitation/humor). Ideal scenarios: brainstorming during your commute, practicing spoken English conversation, or having ChatGPT "read" a long document to you.

Four Scenarios Where ChatGPT Is the Worst Choice (Free Alternatives Are Better)

  • Long document analysis (over 20,000 words): Use Kimi — longer context window, more stable deep analysis of Chinese documents.
  • Deep Chinese-only writing: Use DeepSeek or Claude — more natural Chinese language feel.
  • Math and logical reasoning: Use DeepSeek R1 or ChatGPT's o1/o3-mini models (if you subscribe to Plus/Pro) — GPT-4o falls short of specialized reasoning models for deep reasoning.
  • Code development and debugging: Use Cursor (with built-in Claude Sonnet) — IDE-integrated AI programming is far more efficient than copy-pasting code in the ChatGPT web interface.

Is Plus Worth Subscribing? An Honest Assessment

If you meet two or more of the following criteria, Plus is worth it:

  1. You use ChatGPT more than 15 times a day
  2. You generate over 10 DALL·E images per month
  3. You do data analysis (uploading spreadsheets for AI analysis) at least twice a week
  4. You need to create at least 3 custom GPTs to solidify your workflows
  5. The Free plan's throttling has noticeably impacted your productivity

If only one applies, stick with the Free plan.

ChatGPT is currently the most feature-integrated AI tool — it brings text, images, code, search, and image generation all into one conversation interface. But "most features" doesn't mean "best at every feature." Understand its strengths and boundaries, place it in the right scenarios, and it becomes the greatest leverage for your productivity.
