Overview

Zhipu Qingyan is the consumer-grade AI assistant product launched by Zhipu AI, powered by Zhipu's in-house ChatGLM family of large language models. Zhipu AI originated from the Knowledge Engineering Laboratory (KEG) of Tsinghua University's Department of Computer Science and is the only Chinese team ranked among the global top 10 in Stanford's Foundation Model Transparency Index. From the GLM-10B in 2021 to the flagship GLM-5 model in 2025, Zhipu has pursued a "two-wheel drive" strategy — staying active in the open-source community (ChatGLM-6B was China's first open-source dense model at the 100-billion-parameter scale) while continuously shipping more powerful closed-source commercial versions. In June 2025, Zhipu announced its API had surpassed 40 billion tokens of daily calls, serving more than 1 million developers.

Zhipu Qingyan's differentiation lies in its "All-in-One" super-app concept: a single chat window integrates five core capabilities — text conversation, code generation, image understanding, document analysis, and web search — so users never need to jump between tools. Its document analysis is especially noteworthy: Zhipu has invested heavily in engineering optimization for long-text processing. In real-world tests on a 500,000-character legal contract review, it achieved 94% recall on key clauses with only a 6% miss rate. In addition, Zhipu Qingyan's web search is powered by Sogou's search engine, offering strong coverage of Chinese web pages, and its result relevance ranks in the first tier among domestic models.

✨ Pros & Cons Analysis

👍 Pros

  • Academic rigor: Tsinghua KEG's scholarly tradition keeps the Zhipu team refreshingly honest in model evaluation — their public evaluation reports list both successful and failed cases, which is extremely rare in a domestic AI market that leans heavily toward marketing
  • Solid long-text engineering: GLM-5's native context window reaches 2 million tokens, and it does not suffer from the classic "lost-in-the-middle" problem when handling very long documents — especially useful for document-intensive industries like law and finance
  • Stable Function Calling with excellent documentation: Zhipu's API docs are among the highest quality of any domestic model, with code samples covering Python, Node.js, and Java, keeping the learning curve for developers very low

👎 Cons

  • Weak consumer brand awareness: compared with the massive marketing pushes of Baidu, Alibaba, and ByteDance, Zhipu Qingyan's visibility among ordinary users remains low, putting it at a disadvantage in non-technical communities and organic traffic
  • Image generation lags professional tools: although the built-in CogView can generate images, it trails Midjourney or Stable Diffusion by a generation in composition detail, style consistency, and resolution — not suitable for professional design work
  • Overseas deployment and compliance still incomplete: Zhipu's main server clusters are located in China, so overseas users experience noticeable latency and throughput issues from cross-border network fluctuations, and it has not obtained international security certifications such as SOC 2

🎯 Ideal Use Cases

  • Legal contracts & long-document review — Upload a 200-page M&A agreement to Zhipu Qingyan and specify the clause types to review (e.g., non-compete, breach liability). The AI completes a full scan within 10 minutes, flagging the page number and clause content of every risk point; traditional manual review takes 3-5 business days.
  • Academic paper writing assistance — Researchers can upload paper drafts, and Zhipu Qingyan offers suggestions on structure optimization, citation formatting, and argument logic based on disciplinary writing standards. It performs especially well in computer science and linguistics.
  • API-driven enterprise customer service — Small and medium businesses can build an internal Q&A system on the Zhipu API, uploading product manuals and FAQs as a knowledge base to get a customer-service bot that handles multi-turn follow-ups, with a deployment cycle of about 1-2 business days.

💡 Usage Tips

  • Split long documents by chapter before analysis: although Zhipu supports an ultra-long 2-million-token context, for extremely long documents (e.g., a full textbook), manually splitting them into 100,000-200,000-character batches per chapter produces better analysis quality than a single full upload
  • Turn on "Academic Mode" for research searches: Zhipu's web search supports both regular and academic modes; academic mode prioritizes scholarly databases such as CNKI, Wanfang, and IEEE Xplore — always enable it in research scenarios
  • Read the Rate Limit docs before using the API: Zhipu's API concurrency limits vary significantly by plan — the free tier supports only 3 QPS — so evaluate peak traffic and choose an appropriate prepaid plan before going commercial

Core Features

Zhipu Qingyan is designed around the "All-in-One super app" concept, integrating multiple AI capabilities into a single chat window. Text conversation is the foundation — built on the GLM family, it supports natural-language interaction in Chinese and English across Q&A, writing, translation, and code generation. For image understanding, users can upload images and have the AI read text in them, analyze chart data, or describe visual content. For document analysis, it supports PDF, Word, TXT and other formats; the AI can summarize, answer questions about, and extract key information from long documents — GLM-5's native context window reaches 2 million tokens, so it doesn't suffer from lost-in-the-middle when processing long text. Web search is backed by Sogou's engine and academic databases (CNKI, Wanfang, IEEE Xplore, etc.), and users can switch to "Academic Mode" to retrieve scholarly literature. In addition, Zhipu Qingyan offers AI image generation (CogView engine), a code interpreter, and a plugin marketplace, letting users execute Python code directly in conversation or call third-party services.

First-Hand Experience

Zhipu Qingyan is available on the Web (chatglm.cn), iOS/Android apps, and WeChat Mini Program. Registration supports phone number and WeChat login. On first entry, the interface is a standard LLM chat window — a history list on the left, the conversation area in the middle, and an input box at the bottom. Above the input box is a row of function buttons: file upload (PDF, Word, images), a web-search toggle, and model selection (GLM-4/GLM-5 series). After a file is uploaded, the system automatically parses the content and displays it in the conversation, and users can ask questions about the document. With web search enabled, each reply includes links to its sources. On the app, Zhipu Qingyan supports voice input and voice playback, making the experience close to everyday chatting. The interface is clean and responsive overall, and Chinese input quality ranks in the first tier among domestic models.

Strengths and Limitations

Strengths: The Zhipu team comes from Tsinghua's KEG lab, and its academic DNA brings rigor to model evaluation — public evaluation reports list both successes and failures, which is rare among domestic AI vendors. Long-text capability reaches 2 million tokens on GLM-5 with well-controlled lost-in-the-middle issues, especially useful for document-intensive industries like law and finance. Web search's "Academic Mode" connects directly to Chinese academic databases, which is highly valuable for researchers and students. API documentation is among the best of any domestic model, with code samples in Python, Node.js, and Java.

Limitations: Consumer brand awareness trails Baidu, Alibaba, and ByteDance significantly, with low organic traffic and discussion among ordinary users. The AI image generation (CogView) lags Midjourney by a generation in composition detail and style consistency, making it unsuitable for professional design. Overseas deployment is incomplete — overseas users experience noticeable latency from cross-border network issues, and it has not yet obtained international security certifications such as SOC 2.

FAQ

Q: Is Zhipu Qingyan completely free?
A: The consumer product (chatglm.cn) is currently free to use. API calls are billed per token but include a certain free quota.

Q: What's the difference between Zhipu Qingyan and ChatGPT?
A: Zhipu Qingyan is developed by a Tsinghua-affiliated team with a deeper understanding of Chinese contexts, giving it unique strengths in long-document processing and academic search. ChatGPT currently has stronger multimodal capabilities and ecosystem maturity.

Q: Is my data safe with Zhipu Qingyan?
A: Zhipu has passed relevant national security reviews, and the enterprise edition offers private deployment and data isolation. For the consumer version, we recommend reading the privacy policy carefully before use.

Q: How large a file can Zhipu Qingyan handle?
A: It supports contexts up to 2 million tokens, roughly equivalent to 1.5 million Chinese characters or 3,000 pages of PDF. File upload size limits follow the official site's latest documentation.
