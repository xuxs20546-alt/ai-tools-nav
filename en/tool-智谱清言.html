Zhipu Qingyan In-Depth Review

Zhipu AI's GLM-powered assistant with deep Chinese understanding and strong data analysis

📖 Overview

Zhipu Qingyan was developed by Beijing Zhipu Huazhang Technology Co., Ltd. (Zhipu AI), a company spun out of the Knowledge Engineering Laboratory (KEG) of Tsinghua University's Department of Computer Science, and one of the earliest large-model startups in China incubated from academia. In March 2023, Zhipu AI open-sourced GLM-130B — then the world's largest open-source bilingual (Chinese-English) model — and has since iterated to the GLM-4 series. Beyond opening up its API and cloud services, it delivers its capabilities directly to consumers through the Zhipu Qingyan app. What sets Zhipu Qingyan apart is its fully self-developed GLM architecture: unlike most domestic products that remix the LLaMA architecture, GLM (General Language Model) is built from the ground up — from the underlying architecture to the training framework — giving it a fundamentally different mechanism for Chinese semantic understanding compared with solutions that merely localize English-centric architectures.

Zhipu Qingyan integrates a code interpreter, image generation (CogView), web search, document analysis, and agent creation, making it one of the most feature-complete AI assistants in China. Its 128K context window is not as extreme as Kimi's touted two million characters, but it delivers steady performance in conversational coherence and cross-turn memory. In addition, Zhipu AI has cultivated the B2B market for years: the Zhipu Qingyan Pro edition is integrated at the API level with enterprise collaboration tools such as Feishu and DingTalk, giving it a unique edge in boosting enterprise office efficiency.

✨ Pros & Cons Analysis

👍 Strengths

  • Complete and free code interpreter: Zhipu Qingyan offers the most complete ChatGPT-style code interpreter among domestic products — real-time Python execution, CSV/Excel file processing, data visualization, and instant chart generation, all available to free users, making it exceptional value for data analysis and lightweight programming tasks
  • Mature CogView image-generation ecosystem: Zhipu's self-developed CogView-3 supports text-to-image and image-to-image, with Chinese prompt understanding far surpassing Midjourney — type "a Great Wall sunrise in ink-wash style with cranes flying in the distance" and it accurately grasps the brushwork texture of ink-wash painting and the visual features of the Great Wall, ranking among the best domestic models for style consistency and detail fidelity
  • Low-barrier agent creation with enterprise system integration: Zhipu Qingyan's agent builder lets you define roles, knowledge, and tools through natural-language descriptions, and enterprise users can connect agents to Feishu/DingTalk bots and internal APIs, standing up a production-grade AI customer-service or workflow assistant within hours
  • Strong academic pedigree with standout benchmark results: the GLM-4 series scores consistently near the top on comprehensive knowledge benchmarks such as MMLU and CEval, and has long led the Chinese C-Eval benchmark — a more reliable choice for academic scenarios requiring rigorous knowledge verification

👎 Weaknesses

  • Consumer adoption lags well behind Doubao and Kimi: compared with Doubao and Tencent Yuanbao, which grow quickly through the Douyin and WeChat ecosystems, Zhipu Qingyan trails noticeably in consumer brand awareness and monthly active users, and is at a disadvantage in community ecosystem, third-party tutorials, and word-of-mouth spread
  • Long-text efficiency trails specialized rivals: a 128K context window is not small, but when processing hundred-page PDFs, recall of information near the end of the context drops noticeably — the probability of missing key information rises to about 15% after roughly 100K tokens; if your main use case is ultra-long document analysis, Kimi or Claude is a better fit
  • Conversational style skews academic and lacks playfulness: Zhipu Qingyan's responses tend to be rigorous, systematic, and citation-heavy — an asset in research settings, but overly formal for casual chat, creative brainstorming, or humorous interaction, lacking personal charm

🎯 Best Use Cases

  • Research assistant and thesis support for university students and faculty — through Zhipu Qingyan's code interpreter, students can upload experimental data directly (CSV format) and have it run Python scripts for statistical tests, generating charts and tables that meet academic standards; its strong Chinese knowledge base also helps with literature background review and academic terminology, making it an all-around academic tool for graduate students working on theses and high-level papers.
  • Low-code analytics workbench for data analysts — upload an Excel file containing 100,000 rows of sales records and describe the analysis in natural language — "aggregate sales by region and quarter, show the monthly trend of the TOP 5 provinces as a bar chart" — and Zhipu Qingyan executes the code and returns visualizations, replacing the traditional three-step flow of "write SQL → export → chart in Excel".
  • Fast setup of intelligent customer service for SMBs — using Zhipu Qingyan's agent platform, businesses can import product manuals, FAQ databases, and service policies to create a 24/7 online customer-service bot that supports web embedding and Feishu/DingTalk bot integration, going live within days at a tenth of the cost of in-house development.

💡 Tips & Recommendations

  • Use the code interpreter instead of Excel for complex data processing: for pivot tables, regression analysis, clustering, and other complex operations Excel can't handle well, simply upload the data file and describe the analysis requirements — its Python execution environment can process data on the scale of millions of rows, and results can be exported as images or CSV
  • Leverage CogView's Chinese prompt advantage: if you need to generate images with elements of traditional Chinese culture, Chinese scenes, or Chinese-character titles, CogView's Chinese comprehension far exceeds DALL·E and Midjourney — no need to translate prompts; describing directly in Chinese yields more accurate results
  • Enterprise users should prioritize the Zhipu Open Platform API: B2B users are advised not to stay on the app alone, but to integrate the GLM-4 model directly into internal systems via the Zhipu Open Platform API, which provides dedicated compute and data isolation services — a qualitative leap in data security and customization compared with web-based use
