Overview

Pictory is an AI video tool founded in 2021 in Seattle, USA, by Vikram Chalana and Krishnan Menon, focused on two core capabilities: text-to-video and automatic caption generation. No editing experience is needed — with just a piece of text (a blog post, script, presentation, or simple description), you can produce a finished video with narration, captions, and visuals.

Unlike professional editing tools such as Premiere or CapCut that require timeline work, Pictory automates everything — scriptwriting, asset selection, voiceover, and captions — turning video production from a technical chore into a fill-in-the-blanks process. It is especially suited to content marketing: one article can spawn one or even several short videos for distribution across different platforms.

Core Features

  • Script to Video: type or paste a script and the AI automatically assigns scenes, generating voiceover and captions.
  • URL to Video: paste an article URL and the AI extracts key content to generate a video script.
  • Visual to Video: upload several images and the AI arranges them into a short video with transitions and voiceover.
  • Automatic captions: upload a local video or use generated content; speech is automatically recognized and turned into editable captions.
  • Multilingual AI voiceover: integrated with voice engines such as ElevenLabs, supporting narration in multiple languages.
  • Copyright-safe stock library: a built-in library of commercially usable images and video clips to avoid copyright disputes.

Getting Started

After signing up, you get a 14-day free trial. Create a new project, choose Script to Video, and paste a script of a few hundred words — the AI automatically assigns sentences to the visual timeline with default voiceover and captions. You can also switch to URL to Video, paste your article link, and let the AI extract the key points first.

Once the first draft is generated, replace assets sentence by sentence in the editor, adjust caption placement and branding, then export MP4. For beginners, going from sign-up to a first finished video typically takes just ten to twenty minutes.

Pros and Limitations

Pros: zero editing barrier with a complete automated text-to-video pipeline; captions, voiceover, and assets all in one place; great for batch-producing social media content; copyright-safe stock library.

Limitations: videos can feel template-like with limited style customization; auto-selected assets occasionally don't match the script's meaning and need manual adjustment; Chinese voiceover and caption quality varies, so audition first; the free trial has time and export restrictions.

Who It's For

  • Content creators and teams repurposing text-and-image content into videos;
  • Operators who need quick explainer videos for products and courses;
  • Podcasters and video creators who need automatic captions;
  • Small teams with limited budgets and no professional editing skills.

FAQ

  • How are auto captions generated? After uploading audio/video, the AI automatically recognizes speech and generates captions that can be edited in the editor.
  • Which languages are supported? Voiceover and captions support multiple languages; see the official site for the full list.
  • Is there a free trial? Yes — within the 14-day trial you can generate about 15 minutes of video.
  • Can generated videos be used commercially? Content created during an active subscription can be used commercially; refer to the terms of service.
  • What's the relationship between Pictory and Pictory Pro? Pro is a higher-tier subscription with more video time, stock library access, and branding features.
  • How long does export take? It depends on video length and server load; usually done within a few minutes.
