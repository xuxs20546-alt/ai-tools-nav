Boomy In-Depth Review

AI-assisted music creation and distribution — generate a full song in one click and release it directly to Spotify and Apple Music

Overview

Boomy is the world's first platform to combine AI music generation with streaming distribution, founded by Alex Mitchell and Matthew Aitchison in California in 2019. Its core philosophy is bold: let anyone with zero music background create and release an original song to major global streaming platforms like Spotify, Apple Music and TikTok within minutes — and earn royalties from it. As of mid-2026, Boomy users have created over 20 million songs in total, accounting for roughly 15% of all recorded music worldwide. While this figure includes a large number of "experimental" works, it still highlights the growing mainstream appeal of AI music creation.

Boomy's workflow is extremely streamlined: first pick a music style (such as lo-fi, EDM, hip-hop or ambient), then optionally enter a song title and lyrics, and the AI generates a complete song (including arrangement, melody and AI-synthesized vocals) in about 30 seconds. In the player, you can fine-tune parameters such as tempo, instrumentation and mixing. Once satisfied, you can submit the song to streaming platforms through Boomy's distribution service. Boomy offers a free basic plan (publish 3 songs per month) and a subscription plan (around $9.99/month for unlimited releases), with royalties shared between Boomy and creators on a percentage basis. Boomy also maintains an active creator community where users can share their work, get feedback and discover collaboration opportunities.

✨ Strengths & Weaknesses

👍 Strengths

  • All-in-one pipeline from creation to distribution: Boomy is the only tool that lets you put AI-generated music directly on Spotify, Apple Music and other platforms without a third-party distributor. The entire flow — creation, mixing, mastering, distribution and royalty collection — happens within one platform
  • Extremely beginner-friendly: you don't need to understand keys, chords or beats. Just pick style and mood tags, adjust a few sliders, and you get a decent-sounding song. This low barrier has attracted a large number of creators without a music background
  • Transparent and actually profitable royalty sharing: according to official Boomy data, the platform has paid creators over $5 million in royalties as of the end of 2025, with top creators earning thousands of dollars per month
  • Active community and social features: Boomy has built-in comments, likes, discovery and more, letting creators find and give feedback to each other — a community vibe similar to "SoundCloud for AI music"

👎 Limitations

  • AI vocals still sound stiff: although Boomy has improved its AI singing synthesis in recent years, its vocals still lag clearly behind human singers or high-end TTS in emotional expression and naturalness, often feeling "robotic"
  • Limited musical style diversity: the platform currently supports about 30 styles, mostly electronic music and pop; generation quality for complex genres like jazz, classical and world music is not high
  • Streaming platforms' acceptance of AI music remains uncertain: some streaming platforms are still wavering on AI-generated content, with risks of songs being taken down or royalties withheld — an uncertainty shared by all AI music distribution platforms
  • Limited deep-customization capability: while some parameters can be adjusted, Boomy offers far less freedom than DAW software or more professional AI composition tools for advanced users who want to control every single note

🎯 Use Cases

  • Non-musicians exploring the joy of music creation — Boomy's greatest value is lowering the barrier to entry for music creation. If you've always wanted to "make a song of your own" but never learned an instrument or music theory, Boomy offers a zero-cost way in: you can finish a track and share it with friends within 10 minutes.
  • Royalty-free background music for content creators — YouTubers and video bloggers can use Boomy's free plan to generate plenty of royalty-free background music (mind the free plan's terms of use), avoiding copyright strikes from platforms' content detection systems for using unauthorized music.
  • AI music experimentation and academic research — For researchers studying how audiences accept AI music, the boundaries of copyright law, and the impact on streaming ecosystems, Boomy offers extremely rich research samples and real commercial data.

💡 Tips

  • Treat Boomy as a creative starting point, not the finish line: Songs generated by Boomy can serve as demo templates. Download the stems, import them into a DAW for further editing, remixing or swapping instrument sounds to give your final track more character and quality
  • Set realistic royalty expectations: Don't expect to upload one song and get rich. Streaming royalties depend on play counts — you need social media promotion to earn meaningful income. Treat Boomy as a creative tool rather than a "get rich lying down" scheme
  • Keep an eye on AI content policy changes: Spotify, Apple Music and other platforms are still evolving their distribution policies on AI music. Before investing a lot of time and effort, regularly check Boomy's official blog and related news to stay up to date

⚙️ Core Features

One-click AI music generation: Boomy's workflow is extremely simple — pick a style (about 30 options like lo-fi hip hop, EDM, ambient, indie pop), optionally enter a title and lyrics, and the AI produces a complete song with arrangement, melody and vocals in about 30 seconds. After generation, you can fine-tune tempo, instrumentation and mixing parameters in the player for a second pass of polishing.

One-click streaming distribution: This is Boomy's most commercially valuable feature. Once satisfied with a track, you can submit it directly to major streaming platforms — Spotify, Apple Music, TikTok, YouTube Music, Amazon Music and more — through Boomy's built-in distribution service. The whole process happens online, with no need to contact third-party distributors or sign complex licensing agreements. Boomy generates an ISRC (International Standard Recording Code) for every released track, ensuring royalty tracking and allocation.

Royalty split system: Royalties earned from streaming are shared between Boomy and creators at an agreed percentage. Boomy provides a transparent royalty dashboard where creators can view each song's play counts, earnings and split details per platform in real time. According to official Boomy data, the platform has paid creators over $5 million in royalties as of the end of 2025.

Creator community and discovery: Boomy has built-in SoundCloud-like social features — you can follow other creators, like and comment on songs. The platform's "Explore" page recommends trending tracks and rising creators, forming an active AI music community.

🖱️ Getting Started

Signing up for Boomy only requires an email, or you can log in with your Google/Apple account. After registration, the system guides new users through a "quick create" flow — pick a style, enter a song title, hit "Generate", and about 30 seconds later your first AI song appears in the playlist. The whole process is extremely friendly for users with zero music background.

The player offers several intuitive sliders: tempo (BPM), lead instrument volume, drum intensity and reverb. While these controls are far simpler than professional DAW software, they're more than enough for most users who just want to "make a song of their own". If you feel the AI vocals aren't natural enough, Boomy also lets you record your own vocals and layer them over the AI-generated backing track.

Distribution is just as wizard-driven: confirm the song details (title, artist name, cover art) → choose distribution platforms → submit for review. Boomy's review cycle usually takes 1-3 business days; once approved, the song goes live on major streaming platforms within 1-2 weeks.

✅ Pros and Limitations

Pros: The all-in-one pipeline from creation to distribution is unique among AI music tools, saving the hassle of finding a third-party distributor; extremely friendly to users with no music background — no music theory needed; the $2.99/month Personal plan offers great value compared with similar products; the active community boosts retention and creative motivation.

Limitations: AI vocals still have a noticeable "mechanical" feel, with emotional expression and pronunciation naturalness far behind human singers; styles are concentrated in electronic and pop, while complex genres like jazz and classical are generated poorly; streaming platforms' policies on AI music remain unstable, with risks of takedowns or withheld royalties; advanced users' deep-customization needs can't be met — Boomy suits "quick creation" more than "meticulous refinement".

❓ FAQ

Q: Who owns the copyright of songs generated with Boomy?
A: According to Boomy's terms of service, creators own the copyright of their songs, while Boomy obtains distribution and administration rights. The specific allocation is governed by the terms you agreed to at registration.

Q: Can I make money making music with Boomy?
A: Yes, but keep expectations realistic. Streaming royalties depend on play counts, typically $2-5 per 1,000 streams. Boomy's top creators earn thousands of dollars a month, but the vast majority of users earn single digits. Treat Boomy as a creative tool rather than a "money machine".

Q: Will Boomy's AI music be taken down by streaming platforms?
A: There is a risk. Spotify, Apple Music and other platforms are still evolving their policies on AI music. Boomy says it monitors policy changes and helps creators handle takedowns, but cannot fully guarantee against them.

Q: Does Boomy support Chinese lyrics?
A: Yes. Boomy's AI vocals support multiple languages, including Chinese, English and Japanese. The clarity of Chinese pronunciation is continuously improving, though there is still room for progress.
