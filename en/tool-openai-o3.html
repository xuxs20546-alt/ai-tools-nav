🧭 Tool Overview

OpenAI o3 is a reasoning model in the o series, unveiled by OpenAI on the final day of the "12 Days of OpenAI" event in December 2024. Unlike conversational models that answer directly, o-series models are trained with reinforcement learning to "think before answering," making them markedly stronger than their predecessors on tasks that require multi-step reasoning, such as mathematics, programming, and science. On January 31, 2025, the lighter o3-mini launched on ChatGPT and the API; on April 16, 2025, the full o3 and o3-pro — designed for the most difficult tasks — became available to ChatGPT Plus, Pro, and Team users.

Notably, after OpenAI released GPT-5 on August 7, 2025, older models such as o3, o3-pro, and o4-mini began a gradual retirement, with reasoning capabilities integrated into the GPT-5 family. Today, o3 is therefore more accurately described as a "milestone model that has been superseded." This review is a retrospective assessment based on public information from its service period.

⚙️ Core Features

Deep reasoning capability: o3 is positioned as "solving tasks that take humans hours to complete." On benchmarks such as math competitions, programming contests, and science Q&A, o3 shows significant gains over its predecessor o1 — the direct reason it drew attention at launch. For users, the quality of step-by-step reasoning on complex problems is its core value.

Controllable thinking time: The o3 series supports a "reasoning effort" budget that users can adjust among low, medium, and high levels, trading off response speed against answer quality. Use low for simple tasks to save time; use high for hard problems to gain depth.

o3-pro premium tier: o3-pro targets scenarios with the highest accuracy requirements; OpenAI says it uses stronger compute allocations, suitable for critical decision-making. It was initially available only to ChatGPT Pro users and, from June 19, 2025, to API customers via Azure OpenAI, priced at $20 per million input tokens and $80 per million output tokens.

Tool & ecosystem integration: In ChatGPT, o3 supports tool calls such as web search and code execution; on the API side, developers can integrate o3 into their own applications just like any other model — for data analysis, code review, agent orchestration, and more.

Safety & alignment mechanisms: When releasing o3, OpenAI published a system card describing "deliberative alignment," a training method for reasoning models, as well as red-teaming by external experts before release. Such mechanisms let the model follow safety guidelines during the "thinking" phase rather than only filtering at output time — a signature practice of the o series in safety engineering, and a foundation for the safety design of the later GPT-5.

🖥️ Hands-on Experience

During o3's service period, the workflow for ChatGPT users was: switch to o3 or o3-mini in the model picker, enter a question, wait through a "thinking" phase, and receive an answer with a reasoning summary. For math proofs and algorithm problems, o3 provides step-by-step derivations instead of just conclusions, which reads much better than ordinary conversational models. API developers simply specify the model name in code and set the reasoning effort parameter to call it on demand.

The way to experience o3 today is different: the GPT-5 family retains a reasoning mode while older models are gradually being retired. The practical advice for new users: use GPT-5 and its reasoning mode directly in ChatGPT — they inherit o3's capabilities with integrated optimizations; if you need to call the older models for compatibility, check their availability and retirement schedule on the platform.

For developers, integrating o3 into existing applications is not costly at the API level: the model name and parameter structure match OpenAI's other models; the main additions are the thinking budget setting and longer response time budgets. When migrating to new models, watch out for prompt compatibility — reasoning models parse instructions differently from conversational models, so the same prompt may behave quite differently across generations; run a round of regression testing before going live.

💰 Pricing Plans

In ChatGPT, the o3 series is included in subscriptions with no separate charge; on the API side it is billed per token. According to data officially published by Microsoft Azure OpenAI, from June 2025 the o3 API price was $2 per million input tokens and $8 per million output tokens, o3-pro was $20/$80 per million tokens, and o3-mini was priced significantly lower. Note that these are historical public figures; with the models' retirement and the GPT-5 pricing system, they no longer serve as current price references.

Placing the o3 series in the pricing landscape of its time: o3-mini carried the cost-effective daily reasoning role, the full o3 targeted medium-to-high difficulty tasks, and o3-pro explicitly served business and research scenarios extremely sensitive to accuracy. The three tiers let teams pick a model by task importance instead of always paying for the most expensive one. This tiered pricing approach was later inherited by the GPT-5 family — o3 not only contributed reasoning capability, but also established a template for OpenAI's product segmentation.

👍 Pros & Cons

Pros: High reasoning quality — performance on complex math and programming tasks was industry-leading at launch; adjustable thinking time balances speed and depth; o3-pro offered a more reliable option for critical tasks; the API ecosystem and toolchain are mature and easy to integrate.

Cons: As a standalone model it has been replaced by GPT-5, so new projects should not be built around it; reasoning models are slow and expensive, unsuitable for casual everyday chat; the thinking process is only summarized rather than fully shown, so complex conclusions still need manual verification.

🎯 Who It's For

Mathematicians and researchers needing reliable symbolic reasoning and proof assistance; programmers in algorithm competitions; developers needing high-quality code review and complex debugging; and technical teams building agent applications that demand reasoning accuracy. For new users, going straight to the GPT-5 family is the better choice.

🔄 Comparison with Similar Tools

o3's direct competitors are its predecessor o1 and the open-source DeepSeek-R1. o1 is its "predecessor" and has been superseded; DeepSeek-R1 is known for open source and low prices, matching the o series on reasoning capability and forming a clear market divergence.

❓ FAQ

Can o3 still be used now? After GPT-5's release, o3 is being gradually retired; switch to the GPT-5 family. Check OpenAI's official announcements for current availability.

What's the difference between o3 and o3-mini? o3-mini is the lightweight version — faster and cheaper, suitable for general reasoning tasks; the full o3 and o3-pro target harder tasks.

Why is o3-pro expensive? It is billed at $20/$80 per million tokens (historical public pricing) — the premium reflects stronger compute and higher accuracy requirements.

How are o3 and GPT-5 related? GPT-5 integrates the o series' reasoning capabilities and is o3's successor.

Does o3 support image input? The o3 series focuses on text reasoning; vision and other multimodal capabilities are handled by models such as GPT-4o and GPT-5.

How good is o3 at Chinese? It supports Chinese input and output, performing well on Chinese logic tasks; for critical scenarios, verify with a Chinese test set before putting it into production.
