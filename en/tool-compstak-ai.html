🔧 Core Features

CompStak is a leading data and analytics platform in the U.S. commercial real estate (CRE) market, aggregating over 2 million records of commercial leases, property sales, and loans through its crowdsourced data exchange model. Its core value lies in solving the long-standing information asymmetry in commercial real estate — historically, lease transaction data (rents, free rent periods, tenant improvement allowances, etc.) was not public. CompStak incentivizes brokers to anonymously contribute data, building an industry-level information-sharing network.

Lease Comps: This is CompStak's flagship feature. Users can filter and query historical transaction data by geographic location, property type (office, retail, industrial, etc.), transaction date, leased area, and more. Each record includes key terms that are normally not disclosed publicly: rent per square foot, free rent periods, tenant improvement (TI) allowances, lease term length, renewal options, and more. For brokers who need to advise tenants or landlords on market rents, this data is an irreplaceable basis for decision-making.

Sale Comps: CompStak also aggregates commercial property sales records, including transaction price, cap rate, price per square foot, and buyer/seller information. Investors, appraisers, and lenders can use these to assess the market value and investment return of target properties.

AI-Driven Market Analysis: CompStak applies AI to its massive datasets to deliver trend analysis and forecasting. For example, the platform can automatically identify rising or falling rent trends within a submarket, compare rent gap changes across property classes, and forecast market direction for a specific area over the next 12 months. These analyses help investors and asset managers make more precise, data-backed decisions.

CompStak Exchange Data Contribution Network: This is CompStak's unique data acquisition model. Brokers or appraisers anonymously upload the transaction data they have handled to earn credits, which can be used to unlock data contributed by others on the platform. This "data-for-data" model sustains a continuously growing data ecosystem — more than 15,000 commercial real estate professionals regularly contribute data.

🚀 Getting Started

CompStak's onboarding path varies by user role. Data consumers (investors, asset managers, lenders) need an enterprise subscription to gain access, with CompStak's customer success team configuring accounts and data scopes. After logging in, the main interface is an interactive map with a search panel: users can zoom to a geographic area, draw a target region, set filters (property type, transaction period, size range, etc.), and the system returns matching transaction records in real time.

Each transaction's detail page displays the complete deal terms: rent structure (starting rent, escalation method, net effective rent), lease term details, free rent and TI allowance amounts, and broker notes. On data quality, CompStak manually reviews every submission to ensure completeness and reasonableness — including cross-validating whether rent levels fall within a reasonable market range and whether transaction dates are credible.

For data contributors (brokers and appraisers), the experience is "enter data → earn credits → unlock data." Contributors anonymously submit transaction records through a simplified entry form, earning credits for each approved record. Browsing and exporting data consumes credits. This mechanism incentivizes continuous, high-quality contributions.

👍 Strengths and Limitations

Strengths

  • Broad and deep data coverage: covers major U.S. commercial real estate markets, with each record containing complete transaction terms (rent, free rent, TI allowances, etc.) — far more detailed than public data.
  • Guaranteed data quality: dual quality control — AI-powered anomaly detection plus manual review — ensures the reasonableness and accuracy of the data.
  • Sustainable crowdsourced exchange model: the "data-for-data" incentive maintains a growing network of active contributors; the more the data is used, the richer it becomes.
  • Professional market analytics: AI-driven trend analysis and forecasting give investors data-backed market insights — not only answering "what," but also helping understand "why" and "what comes next."

Limitations

  • U.S.-centric geographic coverage: CompStak's core data is concentrated in major U.S. commercial real estate markets; coverage in Asia (including China) and Europe is limited or unavailable.
  • Opaque pricing: the quote-on-demand model makes it difficult for small teams or independent investors to assess whether the cost is affordable.
  • High barrier for non-contributors: if you don't participate in data exchange (e.g., investors who rarely handle transactions), data is only accessible through a paid enterprise subscription, which can be costly.

👥 Who It's For

  • Commercial real estate brokers: professional brokers who need market rent data to advise clients on leasing or buying — free to use through the data exchange model.
  • Real estate investment firms: private equity funds, REITs, and asset management companies that need market valuation and return analysis for target properties.
  • Bank lending departments: credit approval professionals who need to assess the market value of collateral properties and the sustainability of rental income.
  • Real estate appraisers: professional appraisal firms that need comparable transaction data to support valuation reports.

❓ FAQ

Q: Is CompStak's data source reliable?
A: CompStak's data comes from anonymous submissions by commercial real estate brokers and appraisers who actually handled the transactions. Each record passes dual verification: AI-powered anomaly detection (screening implausibly high/low rent levels) and manual review. However, because the data is anonymously crowdsourced, individual records may contain deviations, so we recommend cross-referencing multiple data sources when making key decisions.

Q: Does CompStak work in the Chinese market?
A: Currently, CompStak's core data is concentrated in the U.S. commercial real estate market. Coverage of Chinese commercial real estate data is very limited. Users who need China market data are advised to look for localized commercial real estate data platforms.

Q: What does CompStak's "data exchange" model mean?
A: Brokers earn platform credits by anonymously uploading the transaction data they have handled; credits are used to unlock and export data contributed by other users. Users who don't contribute data cannot access it — this is the core mechanism that keeps the platform's data updated.

Q: What's the difference between CompStak and CoStar?
A: CoStar is a giant in the commercial real estate data industry, with its own proprietary data collection team and the broadest coverage, but at a high price (typically $5,000+ per year). CompStak differentiates itself through the data exchange model (which lowers usage costs for active brokers) and the depth of transaction terms (surfacing data that is usually not public, such as free rent periods and TI allowances).
