Baidu AI Search In-Depth Review

Baidu's next-generation intelligent search engine upgraded with the ERNIE large language model — AI summaries plus traditional search in a dual-engine architecture, with the most comprehensive index of Chinese web pages

Overview

Baidu AI Search is not a standalone search product, but an AI-enhanced version of Baidu's traditional search, fully upgraded in early 2024. When a user types a question into Baidu's search box, an "AI summary" generated by the ERNIE large language model appears at the top of the results page, synthesizing information from multiple authoritative web pages and presenting the answer directly in a card. This "traditional search + AI summary" dual-engine architecture is Baidu's defensive product strategy in response to the wave of AI search: the AI summary captures the traffic of zero-click searches (users read the summary and click on no links), while the traditional results below remain for in-depth reading. By the end of 2025, Baidu's AI summaries covered more than 60% of search query types, generating about 800 million AI summaries per day.

Baidu AI Search's biggest moat is its crawler index of the most complete set of web pages in the Chinese internet — more than 100 billion Chinese web pages indexed — giving it an unrivaled advantage in the breadth of Chinese content search. The RAG (retrieval-augmented generation) mechanism of the ERNIE model directly taps into this vast index, delivering outstanding results on queries that require synthesizing multiple sources. In addition, Baidu AI Search is deeply integrated with Baidu's own content platforms such as Baidu Baike, Baidu Knows, Baidu Wenku and Baidu Tieba, so for queries about everyday knowledge, education and local services, Baidu's ecosystem data coverage far exceeds any competitor.

✨ Pros & Cons Analysis

👍 Strengths

  • Absolute dominance of the Chinese web index: with more than 100 billion Chinese web pages indexed, Baidu almost never returns "no results" for niche Chinese queries — an advantage that new players like Tiangong AI Search cannot catch up with in the short term
  • Synergy with Baidu's own content ecosystem: AI summaries can directly cite structured knowledge from Baidu Baike, in-depth documents from Baidu Wenku and POI data from Baidu Maps; this multi-source data fusion keeps information density and accuracy ahead of the pack in Chinese-language scenarios
  • Zero-switching-cost transition: for the 700 million Chinese internet users who have used Baidu search for more than 10 years, AI search is not a new product but "Baidu getting smarter" — this smooth upgrade avoids the cost of learning a new tool and the resistance to change

👎 Drawbacks

  • The blurring of ads and AI summaries is a serious problem: Baidu's results contain an average of 4-7 sponsored listings; although AI summaries contain no ads themselves, the ad content right after them easily makes users think it is also AI-recommended, steadily eroding trust in the information
  • Inconsistent AI summary quality: on low-traffic or low-quality long-tail queries, summaries generated by ERNIE may contain logical errors or cite inaccurate low-quality pages, with no human review mechanism in place — users have to judge for themselves
  • Missing deep-research capability: unlike Metaso and Perplexity with their multi-round search and deep analysis, Baidu's AI summary is based only on the first round of retrieval and does not proactively run a second search or cross-validation, so it performs superficially on questions that require in-depth analysis

🎯 Use Cases

  • Chinese everyday knowledge and practical queries — searching "what documents are needed to reissue a lost ID card", Baidu's AI summary aggregates official information from multiple government websites and directly lists the required materials and procedures, an order of magnitude more efficient than clicking through result links one by one.
  • Quick briefs on breaking news — when a major news event breaks (such as a company making a major announcement), Baidu's AI summary can synthesize the core of multiple media reports within 5 minutes, producing a concise overview covering the cause, key figures and responses from all sides.
  • One-question-answer for local services — searching "best-rated Sichuan restaurant near Chaoyang Joy City", the AI summary combines Baidu Maps data and user reviews to directly list the top 3 restaurants with average spend per person and recommended dishes, with no need to hop between multiple apps.

💡 Usage Tips

  • "Click-verify" AI summaries: for high-stakes queries (such as medical advice and legal information), be sure to open at least 2-3 source links after reading the summary to verify the information sources — ERNIE's hallucination risk on obscure topics still exists
  • Use Baidu's own content filters wisely: if you want to search specifically within Baidu Baike or Baidu Wenku, append "site:baike.baidu.com" or "site:wenku.baidu.com" to your query; AI summary quality improves markedly as source quality rises
  • Cross-verify critical queries with Metaso or Perplexity: Baidu AI Search is comprehensive but occasionally shallow; for research-grade queries, use Baidu's AI summary as a first reconnaissance step, then follow up with Metaso's deep research mode for supplementary analysis

Core Features

AI smart summaries: The core feature of Baidu AI Search. At the top of the results page, the ERNIE model synthesizes multiple web pages into an AI summary that directly answers the user's question. Below the summary, a "This result was generated by AI" note appears, along with links to the cited sources. For practical queries like "what documents are needed to reissue a lost ID card", the AI summary can directly list the complete checklist, so users don't have to open pages one by one.

Multimodal search: Since 2025, Baidu Search supports AI-enhanced image search and voice search. Upload a photo of a plant and the AI not only identifies the species but also offers care tips and common pest and disease treatments. Voice search can understand mixed Chinese-English queries.

Baidu ecosystem integration: AI summaries can cite structured knowledge from Baidu Baike, in-depth materials from Baidu Wenku, POI data from Baidu Maps and Q&A content from Baidu Knows. When a user searches "best-rated Sichuan restaurant near Chaoyang Joy City", the AI summary merges map ratings and user reviews to produce a recommendation list. This multi-source data fusion is a moat unique to Baidu.

Real-time hot topic aggregation: For breaking news events, Baidu AI Search can aggregate the core of multiple media reports within minutes, producing a real-time overview covering the cause, key developments and responses from all sides. Users get the full picture without hopping between news websites.

Getting Started

For the vast majority of Chinese internet users, Baidu AI Search feels like a "seamless upgrade" — no new app to install, nothing new to learn. Type a question in the search box on Baidu's homepage and press Enter; if the query triggers an AI summary, an AI-generated answer card appears at the top of the results page. The whole experience is identical to how Baidu has been used for the past 20 years.

The AI summary card uses a visual style clearly distinct from regular result links — a light blue background with an AI icon on the left — so users can easily distinguish the "AI-generated summary" from the "original pages found by the search engine". The bottom of the card usually has a few follow-up buttons, such as "What's the exact process?" and "What materials do I need?", and clicking them makes the AI append more detailed information right within the card.

One thing to get used to: the AI summary occupies about 40% of the first screen, pushing traditional results quite far down. If you are used to scanning the link list for answers, the AI card may initially feel like it's "in the way". But once you adapt, you'll find that most simple queries no longer need any scrolling.

Pricing

Baidu AI Search is completely free for regular users. Baidu's business model is built on search advertising, and the introduction of AI search does not change that fundamental logic. Although AI summaries themselves contain no direct ads, sponsored listings still appear below and to the right of the summary. Baidu currently has no plans to charge separately for AI search features.

Strengths and Limitations

Strengths: The Chinese web index is absolutely dominant — no one can match the coverage when searching niche Chinese questions; Baidu's own content ecosystem (Baike, Knows, Wenku, Maps) provides high-quality structured data sources for AI summaries; zero learning cost — for over a billion users already on Baidu, AI search is simply "Baidu getting smarter", with no migration barrier.

Limitations: Ads sit right next to AI summaries, and users may mistake ads for AI-recommended content; long-tail query summary quality is inconsistent with no human review mechanism; it lacks the multi-round deep research of Metaso and Perplexity, summarizing only from the first round of retrieval; SEO spam content is sometimes cited by AI summaries as a reliable source.

  • News readers interested in breaking events who want to quickly grasp the full picture

FAQ

Q: Do Baidu AI Search summaries cite ad content?
A: According to Baidu's official statements, AI summaries do not directly cite sponsored listings as information sources. However, the ad slots below the summary still display ad links, and the two need to be carefully distinguished visually.

Q: Why do some searches have no AI summary?
A: About 60% of query types are currently covered by AI summaries. Some long-tail queries may not trigger a summary due to insufficient or low-quality sources.

Q: Does the information in AI summaries get updated?
A: Baidu AI Search summaries are generated from real-time retrieval results, so when sources update, the summary refreshes accordingly. However, Baidu does not proactively push notifications about updates to generated summaries.

Q: Does Baidu AI Search support English queries?
A: Yes, but summary quality on English queries is not as good as on Chinese. For deep academic searches in pure English, Google or Perplexity may be better suited.
