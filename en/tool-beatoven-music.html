🎵 Overview

Beatoven.ai is an AI music generation platform from India, focused on providing "emotive background music" for video creators. Its core idea is to let people without music theory knowledge get royalty-free soundtracks that match the emotion of their footage within minutes: upload a video or set a duration, choose a mood, style, and tempo, and the AI generates music ready for commercial projects. Official site: beatoven.ai.

🔧 Core Features

Mood-driven generation: Built-in mood options like "tense, warm, inspirational, melancholic, upbeat". The model understands the musical language behind each mood — warm scenes match major keys and gentle tempos, chase scenes match minor keys and percussion — no music theory required.

Video-synced scoring: Upload a video and the AI analyzes the visual rhythm and emotional shifts, automatically generating a soundtrack that matches the video's duration and section structure.

Online multi-track editing: After generation, adjust the volume of melody, bass, drums, ambience and other tracks right in the browser, set fades and section changes — no professional audio software needed.

Commercial licensing: Music generated with paid plans includes global commercial licensing, ready for YouTube, TikTok, ads and commercial projects with no extra royalty fees.

Style & duration control: Switch between electronic, orchestral, Lo-fi, ambient and other styles, and customize the soundtrack duration and section structure to fit videos of different lengths.

🧭 Getting Started

After signing up, you land on the creation page: first choose "upload a video" or "set a duration only", then pick a mood and style (electronic, orchestral, Lo-fi, ambient, etc.), and finally click generate. Generation usually takes a minute or two; after previewing, you can fine-tune the volume of each track online and download the audio file once satisfied. Free accounts get a limited monthly quota of generations and downloads — enough to try out the workflow.

Compared with general music generation tools, Beatoven's workflow is organized around "video projects": you can generate multiple mood versions for the same video, preview and compare them in your editing software, then finalize — avoiding the waste of generating a pile of music you never use.

❓ FAQ

Can music generated by Beatoven be used commercially? Paid plans include global commercial licensing; the free plan has quota and usage limits to note.

Can it generate songs with vocals? No — Beatoven only generates instrumental background music.

Do I need to know music production? No — just choose a mood and style; the editing interface is simplified too.

Is the free plan enough? It suits trying the tool and light use; for steady content output, consider a paid subscription.

Can I download lossless files? Paid plans support high-quality audio downloads; check the official site for specific formats.
