Tool Overview

Founded by Kevin Baragona in 2017, DeepAI is a long-established online AI tools site, known for its "free + minimalist" generative AI services, and is often cited as one of the earliest online text-to-image tools. Starting with text-to-image generation, the platform has gradually expanded to AI chat, video generation, music generation, and voice, while continuing to offer free trial credits and developer APIs.

Its positioning is not about pursuing the strongest results in a single niche, but rather "a bit of everything, low barrier to entry, good enough to use"—which makes it very friendly for AI-curious newcomers and small-project integrations.

Core Features

AI text-to-image.Enter a prompt to generate images, with multiple style models including photorealistic and anime (see the official site for the current model list). Images generated on the free plan carry a watermark; the paid plan supports watermark-free output and higher resolutions.

AI Chat assistant.A web-based chat tool for writing, translating, Q&A, and brainstorming. The paid plan offers higher message quotas, suitable for light everyday use.

Video and music generation.In recent years the platform has added text-to-video and music generation capabilities, forming a multimodal toolkit alongside image and chat, meeting one-stop content creation needs.

Developer API.Provides APIs for text, image, and other models, billed by usage, with concise documentation—ideal for quickly integrating generation capabilities into your own products, scripts, or automation workflows.

Low-barrier free trial.Register to get free credits with no credit card required, minimizing the cost of "giving it a try."

Getting Started

Open deepai.org, register for free, select AI Image Generator on the feature page, enter a prompt, and click generate—images usually appear within seconds. Not satisfied? Adjust the prompt and regenerate, or switch to another style model to compare results. AI Chat works like a regular chat window, ready to use out of the box.

Developers can log into the console to create an API Key and, following the official docs, make their first API call within minutes. Billing is usage-based, so you can validate the workflow before estimating costs. The overall path is very direct: choose a feature → input content → generate → download or call via API.

Pros and Limitations

Pros:Relatively generous free quota—register and use, no credit card required; multimodal coverage spanning image, chat, video, and music; simple, direct API suited to quick integration; prices are low among comparable paid tools.

Limitations:Free-tier resolution and generation quality trail leading models; understanding of Chinese prompts is limited—English prompts produce more consistent results; the site carries a lot of ads and membership promotions, giving the interface a commercial feel.
