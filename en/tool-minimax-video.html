Core Features

  • Hailuo AI text-to-video:Enter a prompt in Chinese or English and generate a video. Its understanding of Chinese-language descriptions is outstanding, letting domestic creators describe scenes in their native language.
  • Image-to-video:Turns static images into dynamic videos. The feature launched officially in October 2024, supporting camera movement extended from reference images — ideal for e-commerce and ad creative production.
  • Multi-shot and camera control:Supports specifying shot types and camera movements (wide angle, close-up, push-in/pull-out, pan, tilt, etc.), helping creators express visual language more precisely.
  • Continuously evolving model family:MiniMax released its first video model, Video-01, in September 2024. The Hailuo series has kept evolving since, with APIs opened to developers alongside.
  • Multimodal product matrix:MiniMax offers text, speech, and video models plus consumer apps, making it one of the few Chinese AI companies covering multiple modalities.

Getting Started

Getting started with Hailuo AI requires no complex setup: open the official website or mobile app, register, and claim your daily free credits to start creating. Describe the scene you want in Chinese in the input box, choose the aspect ratio and video duration, then hit generate.

Results arrive within a few minutes. Not satisfied? Simply regenerate or tweak your prompt. Users with specific shot requirements can specify camera movement in the prompt for more cinematic results. When free credits run out, you can buy credits on demand or subscribe — overall, the barrier to entry is very low.

For beginners, writing good prompts is the key to better output: first describe the subject (who/what and the action), then the environment (scene, lighting), and finally the camera language (wide shot, close-up, movement direction). Plenty of Chinese prompt examples are available in the community — mimicking proven structures is often more efficient than figuring everything out from scratch.

Strengths & Limitations

Strengths: Outstanding understanding of Chinese prompts, with virtually no language barrier for domestic users; free credits let beginners start at zero cost; fast model iteration with continuously improving quality and motion fluidity; a multimodal product matrix leaves room for future voice and digital-human applications.

Limitations: Character consistency and hand details in complex scenes can still show flaws, requiring multiple generations to filter; queue times during peak hours are unpredictable; check asset licensing terms before commercial use.
