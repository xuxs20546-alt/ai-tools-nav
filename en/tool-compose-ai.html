🔧 Core Features

Compose AI is an AI writing assistant built on a Chrome extension, positioning itself as the "AI upgrade" of keyboard autocomplete — it predicts what you want to write in real time as you type and offers one-click completion suggestions, helping users cut down on repetitive typing. Unlike Grammarly, which focuses on grammar correction, or ChatGPT, which focuses on conversational generation, Compose AI's core is "writing acceleration".

Real-time text autocomplete: in any web text field (Gmail, Google Docs, Twitter, LinkedIn, Slack, etc.), Compose AI shows semi-transparent gray text suggestions after the cursor — similar to the code-completion experience in VS Code. Press Tab to accept a suggestion and finish an entire sentence or paragraph in one stroke. The tool gradually learns your personal writing style from your typing history, making suggestions increasingly aligned with how you express yourself.

One-click rewriting and rephrasing: select an existing passage and Compose AI can rewrite it in a different tone or style — for example, turning a stiff email into a friendlier, more polite version, turning technical documentation into plain language for non-technical readers, or compressing a long paragraph into a concise summary. This feature is extremely useful for professionals who switch between writing styles for different audiences.

Quick replies and templates: Compose AI includes a library of common reply templates — email follow-ups, meeting confirmations, customer thank-yous, polite ways to decline requests, and more. Type a short keyword (like "followup") and the AI expands it into a complete professional email template you can fine-tune to the situation. For professionals who handle a large volume of email daily, this feature significantly cuts down mechanical typing.

Consistent cross-platform experience: as a Chrome extension, Compose AI works in almost every web-based text editor — Gmail, Outlook Web, Google Docs, Notion, Slack, WhatsApp Web, Twitter/X, LinkedIn, and more. No need to switch tools or copy-paste between platforms; the unified completion experience reduces friction.

🚀 Getting Started

Installing and launching Compose AI is extremely simple: search for "Compose AI" in the Chrome Web Store, click "Add to Chrome", and the extension icon appears in the top-right corner of your browser when it's ready. You don't need an account to use the basic features (free plan); after signing up, you unlock personalized learning and more advanced features.

Once installed, Compose AI is active by default in all text input fields. When writing an email in Gmail, after typing a few words you'll see gray completion suggestions appear after the cursor. Suggestions are usually highly context-relevant — for example, type "Hi John, I'm writing to follow up on the..." and the AI completes "project proposal we discussed last week". Press Tab to accept a suggestion or Esc to dismiss it — it's that simple.

In daily use, Compose AI delivers the most value in two scenarios: email writing and social media posting. For email, it helps you quickly complete standardized business communication (follow-ups, confirmations, thanks, apologies, etc.); for social media, it helps you quickly pull scattered ideas into complete expressions. However, note that for highly specialized or creative writing (such as technical white papers or literature), the AI's suggestions can be too generic and require significant manual rewriting.

👍 Strengths and Limitations

Strengths

  • Fast, smooth completions: suggestions trigger after 2-3 words, with latency typically under 200-500ms, so your writing flow is never interrupted.
  • Consistent cross-platform experience: works in nearly every web-based text editor, with no need to switch tools or copy-paste.
  • Learns your personal writing style: Premium's personalized learning adapts to how you express yourself, and suggestions become more accurate the longer you use it.
  • Generous free plan: basic completion is free forever and fully sufficient for light users.

Limitations

  • Limited Chinese support: Compose AI's core training data is primarily English, so Chinese completion and rewriting quality is noticeably worse than English. Chinese users may find suggestions less natural.
  • Browser-only: as a Chrome extension, it can't be used in desktop apps (such as Microsoft Word or Apple Pages) or on mobile.
  • Weak for creative writing: for writing that needs unique perspectives and creative expression (like brand copy or fiction), AI suggestions tend to be generic and formulaic.

👥 Who Is It For?

  • Professionals with heavy daily email volume: sales, customer service, HR, and other roles that send dozens of standardized emails every day.
  • Active social media users: content creators who post frequently on Twitter/X, LinkedIn, and other platforms and need to turn ideas into complete posts quickly.
  • Non-native English writers: anyone who needs AI help completing sentences and phrasing in daily English writing to sound more fluent and natural.
  • Efficiency enthusiasts: light users who want to reduce repetitive typing and speed up their daily text input.

❓ FAQ

Q: Is Compose AI's free plan enough?
A: For light users, yes. The free plan includes basic text autocomplete and a limited number of rewrite suggestions — if you mainly write emails and social media posts, it will largely cover your needs. Premium's advantages are unlimited use and personalized learning, making it a fit for heavy users who write 2+ hours a day.

Q: Can Compose AI replace Grammarly?
A: Not completely. Compose AI's core is "completion and speed", while Grammarly's core is "correction and polishing". They serve different purposes and complement each other — Grammarly makes sure you write correctly, Compose AI helps you write faster.

Q: Does Compose AI collect my data?
A: Compose AI needs to read the text content of the current page to provide completion suggestions. According to its privacy policy, the basic plan may use anonymized data to improve models, while Premium offers stricter privacy options. For writing involving sensitive information (such as contract terms or financial data), we recommend avoiding AI tools or using the Enterprise plan.

Q: Can I use Compose AI on my phone?
A: Not directly. Compose AI is a Chrome desktop browser extension and doesn't support mobile Chrome or Safari. For mobile writing needs, consider native mobile solutions such as ChatGPT or Grammarly Keyboard.
