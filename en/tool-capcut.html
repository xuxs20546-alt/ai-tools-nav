📖 Overview

JianYing (domestic version) / CapCut (international version) is a video editing tool developed by ByteDance and is now one of the most downloaded video editing apps in the world. Although it is essentially a traditional video editing application, ByteDance has deeply integrated a wide range of AI capabilities into it since 2023, making it the practical flagship of AI-assisted video creation. JianYing's AI features cover the entire video production workflow: AI speech recognition that auto-generates subtitles (highly accurate, supporting Chinese, English, Japanese, Korean and more), AI smart editing (automatically analyzing highlight moments in your footage and generating beat-synced videos), AI text-to-speech voiceovers (natural Chinese voices), AI digital human presenters, AI color grading and quality enhancement, and one-click AI background removal.

One of JianYing's most practical AI features is "Text-to-Video": you paste in an article or a piece of copy, and the AI automatically analyzes the content, matches suitable footage and background music, adds subtitles and voiceover, and generates a complete short video in minutes. For self-media content creators, this is a productivity revolution: a news-style video that used to take 1–2 hours to edit can now be done in 5 minutes. JianYing's template ecosystem is also extremely powerful, with millions of video templates contributed by creators at home and abroad — you only need to swap in your own footage to quickly apply complex transitions and effects. Most importantly, the core features of JianYing/CapCut are completely free (including AI subtitles, Text-to-Video and other core AI features); the paid CapCut Pro mainly unlocks more advanced effects, templates and watermark-free export.

✨ Pros & Cons Analysis

👍 Strengths

  • AI features that are practical and free: The accuracy and speed of AI auto-captions are among the best in their class, with multilingual recognition available completely free. Text-to-Video lets writers become video creators with zero learning curve.
  • An extremely rich template ecosystem: Templates contributed by millions of creators cover virtually every popular video style on Douyin/TikTok. Even with no editing knowledge at all, you can produce professional-looking videos by applying templates.
  • Full platform support with a consistent experience: iOS, Android, Windows, Mac and Web are all covered, with project files synced through the cloud — shoot footage on your phone, fine-cut on your computer, seamlessly connected.

👎 Limitations

  • Weak AI video generation: Compared with professional AI video generation tools like Runway, Sora and Kling AI, JianYing's AI leans toward assisted editing rather than generation from scratch. Its AI cannot "create" brand-new footage — it can only process existing material.
  • Feature gaps between the domestic and international versions: JianYing (domestic) and CapCut (international) iterate at different paces; some AI features (such as AI digital humans) launch earlier in the Chinese version and may be missing from the international version.
  • Premium assets and effects require payment: There are plenty of free templates and assets, but most of the most popular high-quality effects, transitions and commercially licensed music require a Pro subscription. Heavy users will feel "lured in free, then constantly blocked by paywalls".

🎯 Use Cases

  • An all-in-one tool for self-media content creators: From shooting, editing, AI captions, AI voiceover to one-click template application — JianYing is currently the only tool that lets you complete the entire self-media video workflow on a phone.
  • For text bloggers transitioning to video creation: Text-to-Video lets WeChat official account authors, Zhihu answerers and Xiaohongshu bloggers easily turn their existing written content into videos and enter the short-video arena with zero learning cost.
  • For educational and training video production: After recording an instructional video, AI auto-generates subtitles, AI editing optimizes the pacing, and AI voiceover handles multilingual versions — teachers and trainers can dramatically cut the production time of video courses.

💡 Usage Tips

  • Always proofread AI-generated subtitles: JianYing's speech recognition is highly accurate, but it still makes mistakes on technical terms, dialects and unclear pronunciation. Spend 3 minutes quickly proofreading after generation to avoid obvious typos that undermine your content's professionalism.
  • Adjust pacing and colors after applying templates: Videos made by directly applying templates tend to look identical. At minimum, adjust the text color/font to match your brand colors, and manually fine-tune the timing of a few key transitions to fit your footage's rhythm.
  • Text-to-Video + manual footage replacement = best efficiency: The AI-matched footage is often not precise enough. First generate a skeleton with Text-to-Video, then manually replace unsuitable clips with footage you shot yourself or hand-picked from the stock library — you get both quality and efficiency.

Overview

JianYing (domestic version) / CapCut (international version) is a video editing tool launched by ByteDance in 2019. Born as a companion editing tool for Douyin, JianYing quickly grew into one of the most downloaded video editing apps in the world within just a few years, with cumulative downloads exceeding 1 billion. Since 2023, ByteDance has deeply integrated a large amount of AI capability into JianYing — including AI speech recognition for auto captions, AI Text-to-Video, AI digital human presenters, AI smart background removal, and AI color grading and quality enhancement — evolving it from a "traditional video editing application" into an "AI-assisted video creation platform". JianYing's unique positioning is that it is not only an editing tool but a content creation ecosystem: millions of creator-contributed video templates let you apply the most popular visual effects and transitions with one click, and the Text-to-Video feature lets text creators enter the video arena with zero learning curve. Full platform support (iOS, Android, Windows, Mac, Web) and cloud project sync make multi-device collaboration very smooth.

Core Features

AI Auto Captions: This is JianYing's most popular and most practical AI feature. After importing a video, the AI automatically recognizes the speech and generates timeline-aligned subtitles, supporting more than ten languages including Chinese, English, Japanese and Korean — it can even recognize mixed Chinese-English speech at the same time. Subtitles automatically get animation effects, and popular subtitle style templates can be applied with one click. Recognition accuracy is among the best in its class, especially for Chinese speech recognition, which far outperforms most international competitors. After generation, subtitles can be batch-edited, re-timed and exported as SRT files.

Text-to-Video: Users input an article or a piece of copy, and the AI automatically analyzes the content semantics, matches suitable video clips and images from the stock library, adds AI voiceover and background music, overlays subtitles, and generates a complete short video in minutes. This feature is revolutionary for self-media content creators — a news-style video that used to take 1 to 2 hours to edit can now be completed in 5 minutes. The AI-matched footage isn't guaranteed to be 100% accurate, but it works well enough as the video's "skeleton", and users can manually replace unsuitable clips on top of it.

AI Digital Human Presenters: JianYing comes with dozens of built-in AI digital human presenters. After you enter a text script, the digital human reads it with natural intonation and lip-sync. Both Chinese and English digital humans are supported, suitable for news broadcasts, product introductions, teaching explanations and other video scenarios that don't require a real person on camera. The naturalness of Chinese digital human voices and lip-sync accuracy are among the best among domestic tools.

AI Background Removal & Quality Enhancement: One-click video background removal (subject cutout) is extremely fast, with edge accuracy that stands out among mobile tools. The AI quality enhancement feature improves the clarity of low-resolution videos, repairs blurry footage and optimizes low-light scenes — particularly useful for phone-shot material.

Template Ecosystem & One-Click Creation: JianYing has millions of creator-contributed video templates covering virtually all popular video styles on Douyin/TikTok — beat-sync videos, Vlog intros, product showcases, holiday greetings, travel journals and more. Users simply pick a template, replace their own photos/video footage, edit the text, and produce a professional-looking short video in minutes. This template ecosystem is JianYing's core differentiator versus professional tools like Premiere Pro and DaVinci Resolve.

Hands-On Experience

JianYing's onboarding experience is arguably the friendliest among video editing tools. The mobile app opens straight into a shoot/import interface with the editing timeline below. Editing is mostly intuitive drag-and-drop and tap — split, delete, reorder, change speed and other basic operations all have clear icon guides. Using AI subtitles is very simple: import the video, tap "Text" → "Recognize Captions" → select the language → wait a few seconds → and the subtitles automatically appear on the timeline. You can then apply a subtitle style template in one click or fine-tune individual words.

Text-to-Video is even simpler: tap "Text-to-Video" → paste the copy (or enter an article link) → the AI analyzes it automatically → choose a voiceover tone → wait for the generation. Once generated, you enter the editing interface, where you can replace unsuitable clips on the timeline, adjust the voiceover pacing, and edit the subtitle text. The whole process from "having an article" to "having a publishable video" takes as little as 5 to 10 minutes. The desktop version offers a larger editing space and finer control — multi-track timeline, keyframe animation, color correction panels — but the overall interaction logic stays consistent with the mobile version, so switching from phone to computer has a low learning cost. For short-video creators chasing "fast and good", JianYing's AI feature combination (captions + Text-to-Video + templates + digital humans) provides a complete solution for continuously producing content without learning almost any complex editing techniques.

FAQ

Q: What's the difference between JianYing and CapCut?
A: JianYing is the version for the Chinese market, while CapCut is for the international market. The core editing features are the same, but the template library, asset library and some AI features differ — JianYing's templates and assets are closer to the tastes and needs of Chinese users, while CapCut's content is more international. The two versions have separate account systems, and project files are not interchangeable.

Q: Is the free version enough?
A: For the vast majority of short-video creators, the free version is already very complete — AI captions, Text-to-Video, AI voiceover, basic templates and other core features are all free. The main limitations are: exported videos carry a watermark, premium templates and effects require Pro, and cloud storage is limited (usually 5GB on the free plan).

Q: Can JianYing replace Premiere Pro?
A: For short-video and social media content creation, JianYing is not only sufficient but more efficient. But for film post-production work requiring 8K editing, broadcast-grade output, professional color grading, complex compositing and multi-camera editing, Premiere Pro or DaVinci Resolve remain irreplaceable. The two are positioned differently — JianYing pursues "most people making good videos quickly", while professional tools pursue "a few people making top-tier videos".

Q: How accurate is the footage matching in Text-to-Video?
A: The accuracy of AI-matched footage is between 60% and 80% — matching for core content is usually correct, but detailed matching for specific scenes may not be precise enough. We recommend treating Text-to-Video as a "fast skeleton generator", then manually checking and replacing unsuitable clips after generation.

Q: What languages does JianYing's AI captioning support?
A: It supports more than ten languages including Chinese (Mandarin), English, Japanese, Korean, Spanish, Portuguese, Indonesian, Thai and more. Recognition accuracy is highest for Chinese and English, with slightly lower accuracy for less common languages. JianYing's mixed Chinese-English recognition is particularly outstanding — for speech mixing Chinese and English, the AI can automatically distinguish and label both.
