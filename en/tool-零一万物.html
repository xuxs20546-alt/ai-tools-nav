Tool Overview

01.AI is an artificial intelligence company founded by Kai-Fu Lee, chairman of Sinovation Ventures, established in 2023 with its headquarters in Beijing. The company started with self-developed large models, and the Yi series gained significant attention in the open-source community. In 2024, it released the Yi-Large and Yi-Lightning models with hundreds of billions of parameters. From 2025 onward, 01.AI shifted its focus to enterprise-grade AI applications and Agent services, launching the Wanzhi one-stop enterprise large-model platform covering model deployment, application practice, and model fine-tuning.

For users, 01.AI offers three main entry points: model APIs for developers, privatization and Agent solutions for enterprises, and a conversational app for hands-on experience. Its strengths lie in the industry influence of its founding team, its open-source community track record, and a pricing strategy aimed at "making AI more affordable."

Core Features

Yi series large models.01.AI's core asset is the Yi series of models. In November 2023, Yi-34B was open-sourced and performed strongly on Hugging Face's open-source model leaderboard; in May 2024, it released Yi-Large with hundreds of billions of parameters, which the company says ranked among the top domestic large models on multiple benchmarks; in October of the same year, it released Yi-Lightning, built on a MoE architecture, focusing on high performance and low inference cost.

Model API service.Developers can call Yi series models through the 01.AI API platform. Public information shows Yi-Large input pricing is around 20 RMB per million tokens, which the company says is roughly one-third of GPT-4 Turbo's price, emphasizing cost-effectiveness.

Enterprise large-model platform.The Wanzhi platform offers model deployment, application practice, and fine-tuning tools for enterprises, helping them bring large-model capabilities into business systems, including private deployment options.

AI Agent solutions.After its pivot, 01.AI places greater emphasis on Agent applications: from top-level strategic consulting and solution delivery to secure deployment, helping enterprises turn AI transformation into measurable operational results. Its website slogan is "make AI actually work."

Open-source ecosystem.The open-source Yi models have accumulated community users and ecosystem tools; technical teams and developers can build on the open-source weights for further development, lowering the barrier to research.

Hands-On Experience

For developers, using 01.AI starts with registering on the API platform: create an account, apply for an API key, and call Yi series models following the documentation — a process consistent with mainstream large-model platforms, with a conversational request achievable in a few dozen lines of code. For general users, the model's capabilities can be experienced through its conversational app, which works like other AI assistants — just ask a question.

The path for enterprise users is quite different: typically, they first discuss requirements with the business team, assess the feasibility of private deployment or Agent solutions, and then move into implementation. Public reports show that in recent years 01.AI has emphasized enterprise-side delivery capabilities, while the online experience entry point for general users has been streamlined. Overall, 01.AI is more of a "model and solution provider" than a consumer-facing chat product.

In terms of user experience, developers will most easily notice the cost-effectiveness of the API and the completeness of documentation; those interested in open source can start with the Yi series open-source weights for local deployment and fine-tuning experiments. For those following industry trends, public reports on its strategic adjustments, financing, and executive changes are also an important reference for understanding the landscape of China's domestic large-model industry.

Pricing Plans

01.AI's pricing falls into three parts: developer APIs are billed per token — public information shows Yi-Large input is around 20 RMB per million tokens, roughly one-third of GPT-4 Turbo's price, with the exact rate subject to the API platform's real-time pricing; open-source models are free to download, with deployment costs borne by the user; enterprise solutions are quoted per project through business negotiation. The conversational app offers a free trial allowance; detailed rules follow the official page.

Pros and Cons

Pros.The founding team has strong backgrounds and deep industry resources; the Yi series models have a good reputation for Chinese-language and reasoning capabilities, with a deep open-source ecosystem; API pricing emphasizes cost-effectiveness, friendly to cost-sensitive developers; the enterprise service chain from consulting to delivery is complete.

Cons.After the 2025 pivot, the focus shifted to enterprise services, and consumer-facing C-end products and operational visibility have noticeably decreased; public reports mention financing and strategic adjustments, and the iteration pace of foundation models has slowed compared with leading vendors; enterprise solutions have a high threshold, so individual developers gain limited direct value.

Who It's For

  • Developers seeking low-cost access to Chinese large-model APIs;
  • Mid-to-large enterprises needing private deployment and Agent implementation;
  • Technical teams using the Yi open-source models for research and further development;
  • Industry observers following the strategic transformation of Chinese AI companies.
