📖 Tool Overview

SenseNova (RiRiXin), SenseTime's large model platform, was released in April 2023 and upgraded to SenseNova 5.5 in 2024. SenseTime has spent over a decade in computer vision, holding one of the world's largest CV patent portfolios (over 8,000 patents), which gives the SenseNova platform a deep foundation in visual understanding. Unlike other large models that evolved by bolting vision onto a language-first architecture, SenseNova jointly models vision and language as two equally important modalities from the very beginning of its architecture design — enabling more precise vision-language alignment in tasks such as image captioning.

SenseNova is positioned as "Model-as-a-Service" (MaaS), providing full-stack support from training to inference through SenseTime's SenseCore AI infrastructure. The platform covers about 30 pre-trained industry-specific models spanning finance, healthcare, autonomous driving, remote sensing and mapping, and industrial quality inspection. One highlight is its remote sensing analysis capability — it can automatically identify building types, road distribution, and vegetation coverage in satellite imagery, and government departments are already piloting it for urban planning and disaster assessment. As of 2026, SenseNova has served more than 3,000 enterprise customers and processes over 1 billion image inference requests per day.

✨ Strengths & Weaknesses

👍 Strengths

  • Industrial-grade precision and reliability in computer vision: With over a decade of engineering experience in core CV tasks such as face recognition, object detection, and image segmentation, SenseNova achieves automotive-grade or even industrial-grade accuracy in industrial quality inspection (e.g., 99.3% accuracy in PCB defect detection) and autonomous driving perception
  • Professional remote sensing and geospatial analysis: In geographic information, agricultural monitoring, and urban management, SenseNova's satellite/UAV imagery analysis capability is virtually unrivaled among domestic large models, directly outputting structured analysis results with geographic coordinates
  • Guaranteed compute from SenseCore: SenseTime's self-built SenseCore houses over 30,000 GPUs and is one of Asia's largest AI computing centers, providing hard infrastructure assurance for training and inference capacity

👎 Limitations

  • Weak presence in consumer products: SenseNova mainly targets B2B customers, so ordinary consumers barely notice SenseTime AI, and brand awareness in the consumer market is nearly zero
  • Notable gap in general text conversation vs. leading models: While excellent at vision tasks, SenseNova's pure-text conversation, writing, and reasoning capabilities lag noticeably behind GPT-4o and Claude 3.5, making it unsuitable as a general-purpose chat assistant
  • Lack of pricing transparency: Price differences between enterprise customers are huge, and billing rules for some core features are complex (call counts, image pixels, model specifications, and other dimensions layered together), making it hard for small and mid-sized customers to understand real costs

🎯 Ideal Use Cases

  • Automated visual quality inspection in manufacturing — High-precision cameras on production lines capture product images in real time; SenseNova detects micron-level surface defects, solder joint anomalies, and assembly errors, automatically generating inspection reports and statistics to replace traditional manual visual inspection.
  • Remote sensing monitoring and analysis for smart cities — Urban planning departments upload the latest high-resolution satellite imagery; SenseNova automatically segments newly built areas, green-space changes, and road network density changes, outputting semi-annual city development heatmaps.
  • Data annotation and scene understanding for autonomous driving — Vehicle testing teams use SenseNova to automatically annotate traffic participants (pedestrians, vehicles, cyclists), traffic signs, and lane lines in road test videos, compressing what once took an outsourced team months into just a few days.

💡 Usage Tips

  • Choose pre-trained industry models over training from scratch:SenseNova's 30+ industry vertical models are pre-trained on petabytes of SenseTime industry data; enterprises should fine-tune on top of pre-trained models rather than train from zero, saving roughly 80% of training time and cost
  • Use the SenseCore API for vision tasks instead of on-premises deployment:SenseTime's cloud inference service is deeply optimized for its own models, usually delivering better latency and throughput than generic deployments on a company's own clusters
  • Run a POC test before signing large contracts:Since industry models may perform differently on real customer data, run a proof-of-concept with 1,000–5,000 of your own images before signing, to confirm accuracy meets business requirements

Overview

SenseNova (RiRiXin) is a multimodal large model platform officially released by SenseTime in April 2023, iterated to SenseNova V6 by 2025. Unlike most general-purpose large models that start with pure text and gradually add vision, SenseNova established a "natively multimodal" technical path from day one — jointly modeling vision and language as two equally important modalities. The platform is built on SenseTime's self-developed SenseCore infrastructure (one of Asia's largest AI computing centers with tens of thousands of GPUs), offering full-stack "Model-as-a-Service" (MaaS) capabilities from model training to inference deployment. SenseNova covers roughly 30 vertical models pre-trained on industry data, spanning finance, healthcare, autonomous driving, remote sensing and mapping, and industrial quality inspection. The V6 release in 2025 introduced a "global memory" capability supporting full-frame-rate analysis of videos up to 10 minutes and cross-modal reasoning, with breakthroughs in spatial understanding and reasoning tasks. As of 2026, SenseNova has served more than 3,000 enterprise customers.

Core Features

Multimodal conversation and understanding:SenseNova accepts multimodal input across text, images, video, and audio. Users can upload a chart, a surveillance video clip, or a PDF document, and the model describes, analyzes, and reasons about the content. The V6 version performs strongly on multimodal benchmarks, particularly reaching industry-leading levels in chart understanding and spatial relationship reasoning.

Visual generation:The platform includes text-to-image, image-to-image, automatic infographic generation, and mixed text-image layout. Unlike standalone AI art tools, SenseNova's visual generation is deeply coupled with language understanding — for example, when a user submits a paragraph of product specifications, the system can generate a matching infographic and automatically design a sensible layout and color scheme.

Video understanding and generation:The V6 version supports full-frame-rate analysis of videos up to 10 minutes long, recognizing objects, actions, scene transitions, and human interactions in video. For digital human video generation, SenseNova can automatically produce a digital human broadcast video with lip-sync and natural expressions from a text script and a single photo of a person.

Pre-trained industry models:The platform offers around 30 vertical models covering remote sensing image analysis (automatically identifying building types, roads, and vegetation), industrial visual quality inspection (PCB defect detection), medical imaging assisted diagnosis, and autonomous driving perception annotation. Enterprises can fine-tune directly on pre-trained models, dramatically cutting the compute and time costs of training from scratch.

Developer tools and API:SenseNova provides standardized model interfaces through the SenseCore API, supporting Python and RESTful integration. Developers can choose model specifications, tune inference parameters, and complete training and deployment on SenseTime's cloud without building their own GPU clusters.

Getting Started

After applying for a SenseNova account on SenseTime's official website, enterprise users enter a unified console. The left sidebar lists module entries for conversation, vision, video, and training. On first use, the system guides users to select an industry scenario and recommends matching pre-trained models. For example, after selecting the "Industrial Quality Inspection" scenario, the platform automatically loads the PCB defect detection model and provides sample images and API call code. The conversation module works similarly to ChatGPT — after typing text or uploading an image/document, the model returns an answer within seconds. The model training flow is more complex: users upload a labeled dataset, choose a base model and training parameters (learning rate, number of epochs, etc.), submit the job for asynchronous training on SenseCore, and receive an email notification when it completes. For enterprise users without an AI background, the pre-trained models offer a smooth out-of-the-box experience; but training from scratch or deep fine-tuning still requires some machine learning engineering experience.

Strengths & Limitations

Strengths:Industrial-grade precision in computer vision — over a decade of technical accumulation in CV gives SenseNova deep engineering reliability on vision tasks; the natively multimodal architecture avoids the modality alignment loss of "text-first, vision-added" patchwork approaches; rich pre-trained industry models (about 30) covering real business scenarios such as industry, healthcare, and remote sensing; and SenseCore infrastructure provides hard guarantees for training and inference, avoiding the uncertainty of third-party cloud services.

Limitations:Low brand awareness among consumers — ordinary users barely perceive SenseTime AI's existence; general pure-text conversation and writing capabilities lag behind top models such as GPT-4o and Claude, making it unsuitable as a general-purpose chat assistant; insufficient pricing transparency, making real costs hard to estimate for small and mid-sized customers; limited ecosystem openness — third-party plugins and community contributions still trail the OpenAI and HuggingFace ecosystems; and some features lack complete documentation and examples, slowing developer onboarding.

FAQ

Q: Is SenseNova a chatbot?
A: Not exactly. SenseNova is a multimodal large model platform; chat is just one of its capabilities. Its more core positioning is as a visual AI capability foundation for B2B enterprises, covering professional scenarios such as image recognition, video analysis, and remote sensing interpretation.

Q: Can individual users use SenseNova?
A: SenseNova mainly serves enterprise customers. Individuals who want to experience SenseTime's AI capabilities can try SenseTime MiaoHua (AI painting) or other consumer products from SenseTime.

Q: How does SenseNova compare with GPT-4o?
A: For general text conversation and content creation, GPT-4o performs better. But on professional vision tasks such as industrial quality inspection and remote sensing image analysis, SenseNova's industry customization depth and engineering precision give it the edge.

Q: What is the "global memory" in SenseNova V6?
A: Traditional large models typically process long videos by sampling frames, which easily loses detail. "Global memory" means the model can analyze up to 10 minutes of video frame by frame and establish event correlations across the time dimension, enabling it to answer complex cross-time reasoning questions.

Q: Does SenseNova support private deployment?
A: Yes. For enterprises with data security and compliance requirements, SenseTime offers private deployment options that place the models and capabilities in the customer's own data center.
