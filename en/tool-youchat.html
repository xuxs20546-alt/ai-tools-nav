Tool Overview

YouChat is a conversational AI assistant launched by the AI search company You.com. You.com released YouChat in December 2022, describing it as a conversational tool that combines traditional search with ChatGPT-style generative AI: after asking a question, users receive not only web links but also direct answers with source citations. This format places more emphasis on "getting the latest information by connecting to the web in real time" than generic chatbots, and it is one of the representative early AI search products.

Since then, You.com has gradually expanded into an AI platform: consumer-facing conversational search, the Web Search API for developers, and enterprise AI solutions have all advanced in parallel, and the official site now markets "the leading AI web search API" as its main selling point. For everyday users, YouChat's value lies in combining "search" and "conversation" into one, making it well suited to fact-checking scenarios that require quick answers with cited sources.

Core Features

Real-time web-connected conversational search. When answering questions, YouChat retrieves web information in real time and attaches source links to its answers, so users can click through to verify directly. Compared with chatbots that rely only on training data, it is better suited to queries about news, product specifications, and time-sensitive content.

Multi-turn follow-ups and clarification. Users can keep asking follow-up questions in a single session, and YouChat revises or refines its answers based on context — no need to restate the question every time, and the experience is close to talking with a real human assistant.

Multiple model choices. In paid plans, users can switch between several mainstream large models (public information shows coverage of the GPT series, Claude, Gemini, and more), choosing a different model for different tasks — more flexible than comparable products tied to a single model.

Built-in mini-apps. You.com has launched mini-apps such as YouImagine, which let users complete operations like AI image generation directly within a conversation, bringing generative capabilities beyond search into the same entry point.

Web Search API. The developer-facing search API provides real-time web retrieval. The official pricing page shows new users receive $100 in free credits, and the free tier offers 100 queries per day — suitable for developers building AI agents and RAG applications.

Hands-On Experience

Getting started with YouChat is straightforward: visit you.com, sign up with an email or Google account, and type your question into the search box. The page first presents a conversational answer with cited sources listed below; if you need more detail, you can keep asking "tell me more" or click the source links directly. The whole interaction saves one step compared with traditional search — no more "opening links to find the answer."

Pricing Plans

You.com's current structure is mainly "free trial + Pro subscription + usage-based API billing." The free tier keeps basic search and conversation but with limited quotas; according to public data from several third-party review sites in 2026, Pro costs about $15–20 per month and provides multi-model access with higher query quotas; the API is billed by usage, with $100 in free credits for new users. For exact prices, please refer to the official site's live page.

Pros and Limitations

Pros. YouChat combines search and conversation into one, and answers come with source citations, making fact-checking efficient; the paid version supports switching between several mainstream models, so task adaptation is flexible; the API's free credits are generous, which is developer-friendly. The company keeps investing in enterprise-grade AI services, and its product roadmap is relatively clear.

Limitations. Free-tier quotas have tightened noticeably after the policy adjustment, so heavy use basically requires paying; compared with leading products like ChatGPT and Perplexity, its brand awareness and third-party ecosystem still lag; the search experience for Chinese content is overall less mature than in English, and access from mainland China is also affected by network conditions.

Who It's For

  • Office workers who often need to verify time-sensitive information and want answers with cited sources;
  • Curious users comparing multiple AI assistants who want to switch models from a single entry point;
  • Developers who need to integrate real-time search capabilities into AI applications;
  • Researchers following overseas AI products who are comfortable searching in English.

Comparison with Similar Tools

YouChat's most direct competitors are Perplexity and ChatGPT. Perplexity also focuses on AI search with citations; ChatGPT is a general-purpose conversational assistant, with web search provided as an add-on capability. YouChat's advantages lie in flexible model selection and the developer ecosystem around its search API.

FAQ

Q: What's the difference between YouChat and a regular search engine?
YouChat directly generates structured answers with citations and attaches source links, so users don't need to open search result pages one by one to find answers.

Q: Can I still use the free plan?
Yes, but free quotas were adjusted around 2025; the exact number of queries and available features depend on the official site's current terms.

Q: Does it support questions in Chinese?
It supports multiple languages, including Chinese; however, search quality for English content is usually more stable. For Chinese scenarios, we also recommend checking domestic Chinese AI search products.

Q: Who is the Web Search API for?
For developers building AI agents, Q&A bots, or RAG applications — the official $100 in free credits is provided for evaluation.
