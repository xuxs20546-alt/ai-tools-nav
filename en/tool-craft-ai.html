Craft AI Review

AI-powered collaborative document tool — smart writing & team collaboration

Overview

Craft AI is a productivity tool that deeply embeds an AI writing assistant into a collaborative document editor. Unlike Notion, which treats AI as an "add-on feature," Craft makes AI a first-class citizen from the product design level — you can select any text anywhere and summon the AI to continue writing, rewrite, translate, summarize, or change its style. The AI understands your document's context and generates highly relevant content. Craft's AI engine integrates GPT-4o capabilities while being deeply customized for document context understanding and formatted output.

Craft's native experience across the Apple ecosystem (macOS, iOS, iPadOS) is its other major core advantage. Documents are stored locally, offline editing is supported, and AI features are available when online. Craft's signature AI features include: smart outline generation (enter a topic and the AI automatically generates a complete document structure with key points), style transfer (convert a passage from "formal report" style to "social media post" style), and multilingual translation (translate an entire document into 20+ languages while preserving the formatting).

✨ Pros & Cons Analysis

👍 Pros

  • Deep AI integration: not a separate chat window, but an AI action menu that pops up right next to the text you select — the interaction feels incredibly natural and smooth
  • Native Apple ecosystem experience: native apps on macOS/iOS/iPadOS with seamless Handoff support and offline availability
  • Beautiful documents: Craft's layout engine makes even plain text look great, with card-based layouts and rich media embeds
  • Unique AI style transfer: converts the same content between styles (formal/casual/marketing/academic) without losing key information

👎 Cons

  • Weaker experience outside Apple platforms: the web version is feature-complete but not as smooth as native apps; Windows users can only use the web version
  • Limited free-tier AI quota: roughly 50 AI requests per month; heavy users must upgrade to Pro ($8/month)
  • Not ideal for large knowledge bases: compared with Notion, its database, board, and project management features are weaker — positioned more toward "writing" than "management"

🎯 Use Cases

  • Fast business document drafting — need a project proposal? Type "write a proposal for a mobile redesign for an e-commerce platform," and the AI generates an outline and first draft that you refine manually
  • Multilingual team collaboration — shared documents between Chinese and English teams; use Craft AI to convert a Chinese document to English in one click, keeping the formatting intact
  • Personal knowledge capture and organization — take reading notes and meeting minutes in Craft; the AI automatically generates summaries and key takeaways for easy review

💡 Tips & Best Practices

  • Use /ai to summon AI:type /ai anywhere and Craft pops up the AI command menu — choose continue writing, rewrite, translate, and more; faster than manually selecting text
  • Set a document style preference: clearly state the desired tone and style at the top of the document so AI-generated content stays consistent
  • Write locally first, AI second: draft the framework in your own words, then use AI to fill in and polish — avoid entire documents that read like AI output

Core Features

Craft's AI writing assistant doesn't exist as a separate chat window — it's deeply embedded inside the document editor. Type the slash command `/ai` anywhere or click the AI menu that pops up after selecting text to invoke AI for continuing, rewriting, summarizing, translating, Q&A, or style conversion. This "in-context" design removes the friction of switching between an editor and an AI tool, significantly improving writing flow.

Smart outline generation is one of the most time-saving features in Craft AI. Just enter a topic (e.g., "2025 SaaS industry trend analysis") and the AI automatically generates a document outline with 5–10 sections, each with 2–3 expansion points. The generated outline can serve directly as the document skeleton — click each section to expand and write it out paragraph by paragraph. For users who frequently write structured documents (reports, proposals, plans), this feature saves a huge amount of time on brainstorming and outlining.

Style transfer is Craft AI's most distinctive capability. Select a passage and the AI can convert it between styles: formal/business, casual/social media, academic/paper, marketing/ad copy, creative/storytelling, and more. The core information and factual accuracy stay stable, but wording, sentence structure, and tone change noticeably. Real-world uses include rewriting a technical product document into a customer-facing marketing page, or adapting an internal project report into a concise executive summary.

For multilingual translation and localization, Craft can translate an entire document into 20+ languages while preserving the original layout formatting (fonts, colors, hierarchy, image positions). Translation quality sits between Google Translate and DeepL — suitable for fast multilingual distribution of internal documents. Craft's AI also supports document-level smart Q&A — ask questions about the whole document, such as "What is the core conclusion of this document?" or "Do the arguments in Section 3 and Section 5 contradict each other?"

Getting Started & Hands-On Experience

Registration and onboarding in the Apple ecosystem are extremely smooth. After signing in with your Apple ID on a Mac, documents in iCloud sync automatically. The main interface follows a minimalist design — document list and folder structure on the left, editor in the center, and an optional info panel on the right. The editor's layout engine is a highlight: default font, line spacing, whitespace, and color palette are carefully tuned, so documents look professional and polished even without any manual formatting.

There are two ways to use AI features: type `/ai` anywhere to open the command menu and pick an action, or select a passage and an AI action popover appears above the editor. The most common workflow: manually write an outline or a few key sentences as "seed content," then use the `/ai` command to have the AI expand each section. AI-generated content is marked in a diff color so you can tell it apart from your own writing. After generation you can choose "Accept," "Reject," or "Rewrite further."

Craft's offline editing experience deserves praise. Without a network connection, document creation and editing work fully; only AI features require connectivity. That's an important practical guarantee for people who often work on planes, on the subway, or in unstable network environments.

Strengths & Limitations

Strengths: AI is deeply embedded in the editor, making interactions natural and efficient without leaving the creative flow. The native Apple ecosystem experience is unmatched — seamless Handoff, iCloud sync, and offline availability, with smooth performance on Mac and iPad. Document typography stands out: the default layout engine lets ordinary users produce visually pleasing documents. Style transfer is unique among competitors, solving the real pain point of "adapting the same content to different channels."

Limitations: Ecosystem lock-in is a sensitive issue — if you're not an Apple user, the web version is feature-complete but less fluid than native apps; Windows and Android users have no native client. The free tier is quite restricted (10-document limit, 50 AI requests), which isn't friendly to users who want to explore deeply. Team collaboration features remain weaker than competitors like Notion — lacking databases, board views, and other advanced collaboration capabilities; Craft is closer to a "writing tool for individuals and small teams" than an "enterprise collaboration platform."

Who It's For

  • Apple ecosystem users: daily workflows centered on Mac, iPad, and iPhone who need a writing tool that works seamlessly across all devices.
  • Content creators and freelance writers: need to efficiently produce structured documents such as blogs, newsletters, and reports, with high standards for document aesthetics.
  • Small teams collaborating on writing: teams of 2–5 people who need to co-edit and review project documents, proposals, and plans.
  • Students and researchers: need to organize notes, write papers, and build presentations; AI-assisted outlines and summaries are especially useful.

FAQ

Q: Can I use Craft's AI features in the free tier?
A: Yes — the free tier (Starter) includes 50 AI requests every 30 days. That's basically enough for light users: generate a few document outlines, translate a few passages, or rewrite some paragraphs each month. But if you need heavy AI-assisted writing every day, consider upgrading to Plus.

Q: What's the Windows experience like?
A: Craft currently has no native Windows client; Windows users can only access the web version (craft.do). The web version is largely feature-equivalent, but performance and interaction fluidity lag behind. If you primarily work on Windows, Notion or Google Docs may be better options.

Q: Can Craft replace Notion?
A: It depends on your needs. If your focus is "writing and document creation," Craft's typography and AI experience beat Notion. But if you need databases, boards, project management, and large-team collaboration, Notion is still the more comprehensive choice. The two complement each other — create content with Craft and manage knowledge with Notion.

Q: What export formats does Craft support?
A: PDF, Markdown, Word, HTML, TextBundle, and image formats. Plus and above also support custom export styles and batch export.
