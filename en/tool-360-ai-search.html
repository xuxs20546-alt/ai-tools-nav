360AI Search In-Depth Review

360's AI-enhanced search engine, integrating the 360 Zhinao LLM, focused on privacy protection and content safety

📖 Overview

360AI Search is an AI-upgraded search engine launched by 360 Technology Group in June 2024, built by integrating the 360 Zhinao LLM into the original 360 Search (so.com). As China's second-largest search engine (after Baidu), 360 Search already indexes more than 20 billion Chinese web pages and handles roughly 120 million search requests per day. This AI upgrade did not start from scratch — instead, it layered generative AI capabilities on top of a massive existing search infrastructure. The 360 Zhinao LLM is developed in-house by 360 and strongly positioned around two product labels: "safe" and "trustworthy" — when generating answers, the model passes through a safety review mechanism that automatically filters out pornographic, violent, and fraudulent content, and flags answers that may contain uncertain information.

In terms of product form, 360AI Search follows a "two-column" interaction approach: the left column shows AI-generated structured answers (with source citations, related follow-up questions, and timeline summaries), while the right column shows the traditional search result list. This design visually draws a clear line between AI and traditional search, making it easy for users to switch freely between the two. 360's differentiating advantage also lies in its ecosystem integration with 360 Browser and 360 Security Guard — more than 400 million PC users can use AI search directly from the browser address bar without opening any new page or installing a new app. Group CEO Zhou Hongyi has repeatedly emphasized 360AI Search's "great and affordable" strategy in public: basic AI search features are currently completely free, with no plans to introduce a paywall.

✨ Pros & Cons Analysis

👍 Pros

  • Safety filtering is family-friendly: the built-in "Teen Mode" in search results effectively blocks inappropriate content. For families with underage children, this is a reassuring safeguard that requires no extra setup
  • Zero-friction experience thanks to high PC penetration: 400+ million users with 360 Browser and 360 Security Guard installed can use AI search without any extra steps — a traffic-entry advantage competitors can hardly replicate in the mass market
  • Two-column layout makes AI and traditional search complementary: the interface of AI answers on the left and traditional results on the right avoids the dilemma of "the AI might be wrong and I don't know who to trust", letting users cross-check AI summaries against original links at any time

👎 Cons

  • Safety review can be overly sensitive and cause "false positives": when searching for sex education, mental health, or socially sensitive topics, 360's safety filter may misclassify legitimate knowledge queries as "unsafe content" and refuse to generate AI answers, hurting the completeness of information access
  • Mobile experience is noticeably inferior to PC: the mobile web and app versions of 360AI Search have many features removed, and AI summary formatting often gets truncated or broken on phone screens — the product team's focus still seems to be on PC
  • Strong self-promotion bias in search results: AI summaries occasionally prioritize 360's own ecosystem content (such as 360 Q&A and 360 Encyclopedia) even when higher-quality external sources exist — this "self-preference" may affect the objectivity of answers

🎯 Use Cases

  • Safe search for shared family computers — For families with school-age children and teenagers, 360AI Search's Teen Mode effectively filters inappropriate content. Parents don't need to install additional parental-control software; a shared family computer is a safe search environment by default.
  • Large-scale deployment on enterprise office PCs — Many companies and government agencies already have 360 Security Guard and 360 Browser preinstalled. As a built-in feature, 360AI Search requires no extra purchase or deployment, so IT departments incur zero management costs.
  • "Double-check" reading for users skeptical about information credibility — When searching for health, medical, or investment topics, the two-column design lets you read the AI summary and the original pages side by side: if both agree, confidence is higher; if they disagree, you can dig deeper.

💡 Usage Tips

  • Turn off Teen Mode when searching normal knowledge as an adult: if you are an adult searching for legitimate content such as medical knowledge or social-science explainers, turn off Teen Mode in settings to avoid incomplete results due to false safety blocks
  • Use the PC version, not mobile, for the best experience: 360AI Search's interface design, AI summary completeness, and interaction smoothness are far better on PC than on mobile — prefer using it on a computer when you have the choice
  • Treat AI summaries as a starting point, not the end: for financial-investment and medical-advice queries, always read the original links in the traditional results on the right. The quality of 360's AI summaries depends on the quality of sources and may not be accurate on controversial topics

Core Features

AI Smart Summary: after you type a question in the search box, the first screen of 360AI Search shows an AI summary answer generated by the 360 Zhinao LLM. Source URLs are cited below the summary, and users can click through to the original pages. For time-sensitive queries like "latest developments of a certain event", the AI summary automatically organizes key milestones on a timeline — several times more efficient than reading news articles one by one.

Two-column search results layout: AI-generated answers (with follow-up suggestions and related topics) appear on the left, while the traditional link list remains on the right. This design avoids the trust problem of "the AI said it, but should I believe it?" — users can compare the AI summary with original pages at any time for fact-checking, a practical balance between AI and traditional search.

AI Mind Map: one of 360AI Search's signature features. For knowledge-organizing questions like "what should I learn to get started with machine learning", the system automatically generates a mind map that arranges scattered knowledge points into a logical hierarchy, and supports exporting as an image or PDF. This is especially useful in learning and research scenarios.

Safety filtering and Teen Mode: building on 360's security expertise, 360AI Search includes a content-safety review mechanism. When Teen Mode is enabled, the system automatically filters inappropriate content and adds reliability ratings to search results. For families with underage children, this amounts to a free parental-control solution.

Hands-On Experience

360AI Search requires no extra installation — if you already use 360 Browser or 360 Security Guard, simply type a search term in the browser's address bar to trigger AI search. New users can visit the so.com homepage to find the search box; type a question and press Enter, and the AI summary is generated within 2-3 seconds.

The interaction design of search results is fairly intuitive: below the AI summary area there are several "you might also want to ask" follow-up buttons — click one to dig deeper on top of the current answer. The traditional results on the right keep the classic layout familiar to Baidu/Google users: blue titles + green URLs + gray snippets. If you need deeper material, a "Deep Search" button at the bottom of the AI summary triggers a second round of retrieval and analysis, producing a more detailed answer — though it takes 5-8 seconds.

One usage detail worth noting: when searching certain socially sensitive topics, 360AI Search may skip generating the AI summary due to the safety review and only show traditional results. In that case, a notice at the top of the page says "AI summaries are temporarily unavailable for this topic", and users can still get information via the links on the right.

Pricing

360AI Search is currently completely free. Zhou Hongyi has said publicly on several occasions that "basic AI search features will not be charged", and 360's business model leans toward monetizing through search ads. On the enterprise API side, the 360 Zhinao LLM is offered as a paid service through the 360 Cloud platform, but the AI search features used by regular users involve no cost.

Strengths & Limitations

Strengths: 360AI Search's biggest advantage is zero friction — 400 million 360 Browser users can start using it without any extra steps; the two-column layout and mind-map feature are innovative among similar AI search products; the safety filtering is family-friendly, especially for households with underage children.

Limitations: the safety review mechanism can occasionally be overly sensitive, and even normal knowledge queries may be wrongly blocked; the mobile experience is clearly weaker than PC, with many features removed; search results occasionally prioritize 360's own products (360 Q&A, 360 Encyclopedia), raising objectivity concerns; AI summaries don't support multi-turn conversations — every query starts a fresh, independent session.

Who It's For

  • Existing users of 360 Browser and 360 Security Guard — no need to learn a new tool
  • Family users, especially households with school-age children who need a safe search environment
  • Ordinary netizens sensitive to information credibility who prefer the "AI + traditional" double-check reading style
  • Students and self-learners who want to organize their knowledge with the AI mind-map feature

FAQ

Q: What's the difference between 360AI Search and regular 360 Search?
A: 360AI Search adds AI smart summaries and mind maps on top of regular search. For the same keyword, the AI version directly presents an integrated answer at the top of the page instead of just listing links.

Q: Is the information in AI summaries reliable?
A: Summaries cite their sources, so reliability depends on the quality of the source pages. For important information (health, medical, investment), we recommend clicking the original links on the right for cross-verification.

Q: Why do some questions have no AI summary?
A: The topic may have triggered the safety review mechanism, or there may not be enough search results for that query type to generate a reliable summary. In such cases, only traditional results are shown.

Q: Does 360AI Search support voice search?
A: The 360AI Search mobile app supports voice input, but the PC web version currently only supports text input.
