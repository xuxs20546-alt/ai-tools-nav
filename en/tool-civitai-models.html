🎭 Overview

Launched in November 2022, Civitai is one of the largest community model-sharing platforms for Stable Diffusion in the world. Creators upload trained models such as Checkpoints (base models), LoRAs, Embeddings, and ControlNet to the platform; users can filter by category, base model, and type, and download them for free. The platform also offers in-browser image generation, Bounties, and a creator incentive system — a true "model marketplace" in the AI art ecosystem.

🔧 Core Features

Massive model library: tens of thousands of Checkpoint and LoRA models spanning realistic, anime, pixel art, 3D rendering, and other styles, with precise filtering by base models such as Stable Diffusion 1.5, SDXL, and Flux.

One-click download and deployment: model pages provide trigger words, recommended parameters, and sample images; after downloading, you can import models into local tools such as WebUI and ComfyUI.

Online generation: the built-in online generator requires no local GPU — spend Buzz points to generate images right in the browser.

Bounties and incentives: launch Bounties for commissioned creations; the platform introduced the Buzz points system in 2023, letting creators earn revenue from their works.

Community feed: the homepage feed showcases trending models and creations, browsable by "Popular Today", "Most Collected", and more — ideal for finding inspiration.

🧭 Getting Started

You can browse and search models on civitai.com without registering. Once you find a model you like, check the sample images, trigger words, and recommended parameters, then click download to get the model file; place it in WebUI's models folder and refresh, and you can generate locally. Users who want to try it online can register an account, top up Buzz, and use the web generator. The whole process feels very natural for anyone familiar with the Stable Diffusion ecosystem.

✅ Pros and Limitations

Pros: industry-leading variety and volume of models; complete sample images and parameter documentation with a low learning curve; an active community with fast-updating creative content.

Limitations: descriptions of high-quality models are mostly in English; the platform applies age restrictions and review rules to some content, so check before use; online generation consumes Buzz, and heavy usage can be costly.

❓ FAQ

Do I need to pay to download models? The vast majority of models are free to download; some creators may set a points threshold.

Can I use Civitai without a GPU? Yes — use the online generation feature, though it consumes Buzz points.

What's the difference between LoRA and Checkpoint? A Checkpoint is a complete base model, while a LoRA is a lightweight style or character add-on attached to a model.

Are there copyright risks with models? Before downloading, check the license statement on the model page — some models restrict commercial use.
