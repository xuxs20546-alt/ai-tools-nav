Tool Overview

Baichuan AI is a Chinese large-model company founded in April 2023 by Wang Xiaochuan, former CEO of Sogou, with the mission of "helping everyone easily and affordably access world knowledge and professional services," aiming to build a leading large-model foundation in China. It is both a foundation-model developer and an application service provider: its Baichuan series models were once released to the open-source community, while its official website chat interface and cloud platform APIs serve consumers and enterprises.

Since its founding, Baichuan AI has iterated through multiple model versions, from the early Baichuan and Baichuan 2 to Baichuan 3 with over 100 billion parameters, and the search-augmented Baichuan2-Turbo series, as well as specialized models for vertical sectors such as healthcare. For everyday users, the most direct entry point is the web chat on the official site; for developers, Baichuan model APIs can be called through platforms such as Alibaba Cloud Bailian.

Key Features

Web chat and knowledge Q&A.The Baichuan AI official website offers a conversational experience where users can ask questions directly and engage in multi-turn exchanges. According to official materials, its models have a solid foundation in Chinese comprehension, knowledge Q&A, and long-text processing, making them suitable for information lookup, content organization, and similar use cases.

Long-text understanding.Baichuan models support processing text with long contexts, enabling summarization, Q&A, and rewriting of long documents such as contracts, reports, and papers — an important capability for office scenarios.

Search-augmented models.The Baichuan2-Turbo series uses search augmentation to connect the large model with domain knowledge and web-wide knowledge, incorporating real-time retrieval results into answers and reducing the timeliness issues that come from relying solely on training data.

Vertical-domain models.Baichuan AI continues to invest in vertical sectors such as healthcare; public reports in early 2026 noted the release of a large model for the medical field, targeting clinical and medical decision-making scenarios, reflecting its "professional services" direction.

API and cloud services.Developers can call Baichuan series model APIs through the Alibaba Cloud Bailian platform to integrate Chinese large-model capabilities into their own applications, lowering the cost of building models from scratch. The cloud approach removes the operational burden of running your own inference clusters, and pay-as-you-go pricing lets small teams get started at low cost.

Open-source foundation.In its early days, Baichuan AI released the weights of some Baichuan series models to the community; public materials show its open models received considerable attention on Chinese-language tasks, laying a foundation for later technical iteration and ecosystem building.

Hands-On Experience

The path for everyday users is simple: visit the Baichuan AI official website, register an account, enter the chat interface, and start typing questions. The interface is consistent with mainstream AI assistants, supports multi-turn conversations, and response speed is acceptable. To test long-text capabilities, you can paste a long passage and ask the model to summarize the key points, or keep asking questions about the document content.

Developers take a different path: enable Baichuan model services on the Alibaba Cloud Bailian platform, create an API key following the documentation, and call the models in code. The two entry points serve different audiences, and everyday users do not need to worry about API details. Note that the model versions and quota rules of the web version and the API version are not exactly the same — refer to each entry page for specifics.

For enterprise customers, Baichuan AI also provides channels for discussing industry solutions, especially in vertical sectors where it has deep expertise, such as healthcare, where model application plans can be designed around specific business scenarios. Overall, Baichuan AI's "everyday user entry" stays simple, while its real value proposition lies more in model capabilities and industry services. Users are advised to choose the entry point that fits their role rather than treating web chat as the whole story.

Pricing Plans

Baichuan AI's pricing is clearly tiered: web chat is free for everyday users; API services are billed on a pay-as-you-go basis through cloud platforms, with specific prices subject to the Alibaba Cloud Bailian pricing page; enterprise customers needing private deployment or custom model customization go through a sales-quote process. The company has not published a unified price list on public channels, so refer to each entry point's real-time pricing.

Pros and Cons

Pros.The team background is solid — founder Wang Xiaochuan has long-term experience in search and algorithmic engineering; the models have strong Chinese-language capabilities, with long-text processing and search augmentation as practical highlights; the open-source history provides technical reference for developers; cloud platform integration lowers the barrier to entry; and there is clear exploration of vertical sectors such as healthcare.

Cons.Compared with leading large models, Baichuan models have relatively limited visibility in general capability benchmarks; APIs are mainly distributed through third-party cloud platforms, and the independent developer ecosystem is still being built; vertical models mostly target industry customers, so everyday users can perceive few differentiating features.

Ideal Users

  • Office workers who need Chinese knowledge Q&A and long-text processing;
  • Developers who want to quickly integrate Chinese large models via cloud APIs;
  • Enterprise customers interested in AI applications for vertical industries such as healthcare;
  • Students and practitioners researching the technical paths of Chinese large models.
