CogView In-Depth Review

📖 Overview

CogView is a text-to-image model released by Zhipu AI in June 2023, now iterated to the CogView-4 version. Unlike mainstream text-to-image models (such as DALL·E 3 and Midjourney) trained on English prompts, CogView's key differentiator is that it shares its semantic understanding foundation with the ChatGLM text model — this means CogView understands Chinese descriptions far more deeply than English-native image models. When you describe a poetic scene in Chinese such as "sunset clouds flying with a lone wild goose, autumn water blending with the vast sky," CogView accurately captures the color relationships, spatial layers, and atmosphere of the scene, while English models often drift into producing a single bird against a red sky.

Another major highlight of the 2025-upgraded CogView-4 is a breakthrough in Chinese text rendering: it can generate full paragraphs of readable Chinese text within a single image, with font styles consistent with the overall aesthetics of the artwork. Previously, generating readable Chinese text in images had always been the "Achilles' heel" of text-to-image models — most English models could only produce gibberish resembling Chinese glyphs. CogView also supports image editing — users upload a photo and describe the part they want to change in Chinese (e.g., "change the background to a snowy scene"), and the model performs naturally blended local edits while keeping the main subject unchanged. CogView is currently available for free through the Zhipu Qingyan app and web version.

✨ Pros & Cons Analysis

👍 Pros

  • Chinese understanding-driven image generation stands out: unlike other pipelines that machine-translate Chinese into English and then generate images, CogView works directly in the Chinese semantic space for text-to-image conversion, achieving significantly higher accuracy in understanding classical poetry, idioms, and cultural symbols unique to Chinese
  • Chinese text rendering solves an industry pain point: CogView-4 can generate clear, readable Chinese titles, banners, and captions within images, so product posters, promotional materials, and WeChat official account cover designs can be fully completed by AI without manually adding text afterward
  • Seamless integration with Zhipu Qingyan lowers the barrier to entry: users don't need to register on a separate drawing platform — just type "draw a..." in the Zhipu Qingyan chat box to invoke CogView, which is extremely friendly for beginners new to AI drawing

👎 Cons

  • Image resolution and fine detail fall short of paid professional tools: when zoomed in at 4K resolution, CogView-generated images have a higher probability of blurry details or artifacts, so professional designers still need to pair it with Midjourney or Stable Diffusion
  • Image editing accuracy and controllability are unstable: for local edits on complex backgrounds or when precise masking is needed, the success rate is about 60-70%, with roughly a 30% chance of incorrectly altering parts that shouldn't be changed or missing areas that need editing
  • The diversity and professionalism of the style library have room to grow: although it supports multiple painting styles, its performance in high-frequency needs such as commercial photography style, 3D rendering style, and realistic portraits still shows a visible gap compared to benchmark products like Midjourney

🎯 Use Cases

  • Quick generation of WeChat official account covers and images — Operators type "please create a WeChat official account cover about returning home for the Spring Festival, with the title 'Home' in two characters and a warm color palette" directly into the Zhipu Qingyan chat box, and CogView outputs a finished cover with a Chinese title within 30 seconds.
  • Visualizing the mood of classical poetry in teaching — Language teachers generate images on the spot from famous classical verses in class, helping students intuitively feel visual imagery such as "a solitary plume of smoke rises straight in the vast desert," turning the beauty of words into the beauty of visuals and sparking students' interest in learning.
  • Rapid prototyping of product concept images — Product managers describe ideas in Chinese during requirement review meetings, such as "a white round smart speaker with a touch panel on top and a ring of LED lights at the bottom," and CogView generates multiple design options in real time for the team to discuss.

💡 Usage Tips

  • Use concrete descriptions rather than abstract words in Chinese prompts: "warm afternoon sunlight" works far better than "a comfortable feeling," because CogView needs specific visual elements (light, colors, objects) to build the image — purely abstract emotional descriptions lead to unstable results
  • For images with text, generate in two rounds: first have CogView generate a base image without text, and after confirming the composition, use the image editing feature to add text separately — this gives you more control over text position and font
  • Polish commercial-grade designs with professional tools at the end: CogView is great for creative exploration and rapid prototyping, but for final commercial visual deliverables, we recommend exporting and doing a second pass on resolution and details in Photoshop or Canva

Overview

CogView is a series of text-to-image models launched by Zhipu AI, now iterated to the CogView-4 version. Unlike DALL·E, Midjourney, and other models trained on English, CogView's core differentiator is that it shares its semantic understanding foundation with Zhipu's ChatGLM text model — meaning it directly converts between Chinese semantic space and images without translating Chinese prompts into English first, resulting in higher fidelity to Chinese descriptions.

In March 2025, Zhipu released the open-source CogView-4 model with 6 billion parameters, supporting native Chinese input and Chinese text-to-image generation. This is a major technical breakthrough — generating readable Chinese text in images had previously been the "Achilles' heel" of text-to-image models. At the same time, Zhipu also launched the free CogView-3-Flash model, focused on ultra-fast generation (images in seconds), offered free to users as an entry-level product. The CogView series is available through the Zhipu Qingyan app and web version, and developers can also access it via the Zhipu API platform.

Core Features

Chinese text-to-image:CogView's core capability is generating images from Chinese text descriptions. Because it works directly in the Chinese semantic space, its understanding of classical poetry moods, idioms, Chinese-specific cultural symbols, and aesthetic preferences is far superior to English-native models. For example, with the same prompt "a solitary plume of smoke rises straight in the vast desert," CogView's output is closer to Chinese users' aesthetic expectations in color, composition, and cultural elements.

Chinese text rendering:CogView-4 supports embedding clear, readable Chinese text in generated images, with font styles consistent with the overall aesthetics of the artwork. This is highly practical for users who need posters, WeChat official account covers, or promotional materials with Chinese titles — previously you had to generate the image first and add text in Photoshop, now it's done in one step.

Image editing and local modification:Users upload an image and describe in natural Chinese what they want to change (e.g., "change the background to a snowy scene" or "remove the person on the right"), and CogView performs local edits while keeping the main subject and other areas unchanged. This feature is still being continuously improved and works well in simple scenarios.

Multiple resolutions and ultra-fast generation:CogView-3-Flash focuses on ultra-fast generation, outputting an image in a few seconds on average. CogView-4 supports multiple resolution outputs, suitable for different uses (social media images, print materials, etc.). The API supports batch generation and enterprise-level concurrency.

Hands-On Experience

The most convenient way to use CogView is through the Zhipu Qingyan app or web version. Simply type "draw me a..." directly in the chat box to invoke the image generation feature, with no need to switch to a dedicated drawing tool or platform. In terms of generation speed, CogView-3-Flash typically produces an image in 3-5 seconds, while CogView-4 takes 10-20 seconds. For simple needs (such as illustrations, avatars, wallpapers), CogView-3-Flash's speed and quality are basically satisfactory.

The response accuracy for Chinese prompts is what surprises people most about CogView. Testing CogView and Midjourney with the same Chinese prompts, CogView shows clearly higher understanding accuracy in Chinese cultural scenarios. However, in terms of image fineness, color richness, and artistic expressiveness, CogView still lags behind Midjourney — the latter remains the industry benchmark in high-end artistic creation. The success rate of the image editing feature is also not stable enough in complex scenarios, and it sometimes alters areas that shouldn't be changed.

FAQ

Q: Is CogView free or paid?
A: CogView-3-Flash is completely free. CogView-4 is billed by API usage. Using it in the Zhipu Qingyan app comes with a free quota.

Q: Can images generated by CogView be used commercially?
A: Specific commercial terms are subject to Zhipu AI's service agreement. We recommend reviewing the latest user agreement and intellectual property policy before commercial use.

Q: Which is more suitable for Chinese users, CogView or Midjourney?
A: If your need is fast image generation in Chinese scenarios (posters with text, classical poetry mood images, Chinese cultural elements), CogView is the better fit. If you need high-quality artistic creation and English prompts are not a barrier, Midjourney offers better image quality.

Q: Can I use CogView to generate images with Chinese characters?
A: CogView-4 supports generating clear, readable Chinese text in images — that's one of its core strengths. CogView-3-Flash's Chinese text generation capability is relatively limited.
