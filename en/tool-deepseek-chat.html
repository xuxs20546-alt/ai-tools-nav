DeepSeek Chat In-Depth Review

Developed by DeepSeek, this open-source large language model delivers exceptional reasoning, with industry-leading code generation and mathematical derivation.

📖 Overview

DeepSeek Chat is a conversational AI product launched in early 2024 by Hangzhou DeepSeek Artificial Intelligence Basic Technology Research Co., Ltd. Its underlying DeepSeek model series shook the global AI community in 2024–2025 with a strategy of "low cost, high performance, fully open source." The DeepSeek-R1 reasoning model released in January 2025 achieved parity with OpenAI o1 on the AIME 2024 math competition and the Codeforces programming contest, with training costs reportedly just one-tenth of the latter — a milestone breakthrough that directly triggered major swings in US AI stocks, dubbed the "DeepSeek Shock" by the media.

DeepSeek Chat's core competitive edge lies in its "chain-of-thought visualization" feature: when a user poses a complex math problem or programming challenge, the model doesn't jump straight to an answer — it progressively reveals its internal reasoning, listing known conditions, trying different approaches, and comparing the feasibility of each path — before outputting the final result. This transparent reasoning chain lets users verify the correctness of answers and doubles as an excellent learning tool: students can watch how an AI "thinks" like a human. As of mid-2026, DeepSeek Chat's daily active users have surpassed 50 million, and its App Store downloads have long ranked No. 1 among productivity apps in the China region.

✨ Pros & Cons Analysis

👍 Pros

  • Reasoning that tops its class: the R1 model achieves 79.8% accuracy on the AIME math competition, comparable to OpenAI o1-preview, while being fully open-sourced at just 3–5% of the cost — developers worldwide can deploy it themselves
  • Transparent chain-of-thought with unique educational value: students who don't just want the standard answer, and engineers who want to understand the derivation process, can learn "how to think" through DeepSeek's chain-of-thought mode — a design philosophy that surpasses most competitors
  • Cost advantage translates into an extremely generous free experience: the free tier has virtually no practical limits, allowing hundreds of conversation rounds and dozens of code executions per day — a key factor in DeepSeek's rapid user growth

👎 Cons

  • Multimodal capabilities seriously lag behind: as of mid-2026, DeepSeek Chat still doesn't support native image recognition or generation; uploaded images are only processed as OCR text extraction, with almost zero "understanding" of visual content
  • Server stability frequently strained under heavy traffic: due to explosive user growth, DeepSeek's service experienced widespread delays and even brief outages during several peak periods in 2025; reliability still trails ChatGPT and Claude
  • Lacks "human touch" in non-reasoning conversations: DeepSeek's reply style leans rational and academic, feeling stiff in conversations that need empathy and creative expression — not natural or warm enough

🎯 Use Cases

  • Math competitions and academic tutoring — Feed an olympiad problem into DeepSeek Chat and enable chain-of-thought mode: it not only gives the answer but walks through a complete 5–8 step derivation, annotating the theorem used at each step, so students can fully grasp the solution logic as if following a teacher step by step.
  • Code debugging and architecture design — Paste a buggy Python snippet and DeepSeek will pinpoint the error, write a fixed version, explain why the original code failed, and even write unit tests when needed — an experience close to a mid-level engineer's code review.
  • Verifying formula derivations in academic papers — Before submission, researchers can feed the complex derivation steps of their paper into DeepSeek one by one, letting it independently verify the mathematical correctness of each step and catch errors caused by typos or skipped steps.

💡 Usage Tips

  • Turn on "code execution" mode for programming questions to get runnable results: in normal chat mode, code from DeepSeek isn't verified at runtime; click the "Run Code" button at the bottom-right of the reply so the model actually executes it in a sandbox and returns the output along with any error messages
  • For complex math problems, explicitly request "step-by-step derivation" instead of a direct answer: add "please show your derivation step by step, annotating the theorem or formula used at each step" at the end of your prompt; otherwise DeepSeek may skip steps and give the answer directly in simple mode
  • Switch to API mode when you hit delays during peak hours: if the web version queues under heavy traffic, call DeepSeek models via third-party API relay services (such as OpenRouter) to bypass the web congestion

Core Features

DeepSeek Chat's core selling points are reasoning capability and cost efficiency, offering the following feature modules:

1. Chain-of-thought visualization reasoning — DeepSeek's most educational feature. When a user asks a complex math problem, logic puzzle, or coding challenge, the model doesn't answer directly but progressively reveals its internal reasoning: listing known conditions, trying different approaches, comparing path feasibility, and annotating the theorem or formula used at each step. This transparent reasoning chain serves as both an answer-verification mechanism and an excellent learning tool.

2. Code execution sandbox — A built-in Python runtime in the chat interface. You can ask DeepSeek to write code and click the "Run Code" button; the model actually executes it in a sandbox and returns results and error messages. This feature turns code verification from "looks right" into "runs right" — ideal for learning algorithms and rapid prototype validation.

3. Ultra-long context handling — DeepSeek supports a context window of up to a million tokens, meaning you can upload an entire book, complete technical documentation, or all code files of a large project at once, letting the AI analyze and answer with a global view. This capability is highly practical for long-text summarization and whole-repository code review.

4. Web search integration — In the web and app versions, you can manually enable web search (you need to toggle it on yourself), and DeepSeek will search the internet in real time and incorporate the results into its answers. This feature is very useful when you need to verify the latest facts or data.

5. File upload and parsing — Supports uploading common formats such as PDF, Word, Excel, TXT, and images; the AI automatically extracts text and answers questions based on it. Note that image uploads only perform OCR text extraction, with no visual content understanding. If you upload blueprints or photos, the model can only "read" the text on them, not "see" the images.

Getting Started

DeepSeek Chat has an extremely low barrier to entry. Visit chat.deepseek.com and start chatting without even registering (registering lets you save chat history). The interface resembles ChatGPT: conversation list on the left, conversation area in the center, input box at the bottom. First-time users should enable "Deep Think" mode (the toggle near the input box) so the model displays its full chain of thought, letting you experience DeepSeek's distinctive reasoning ability firsthand.

Take a typical programming question as an example: paste a buggy Python snippet into the input box and append "what's wrong with this code? Please analyze and provide a fix." DeepSeek will analyze the code line by line, point out the exact location and cause of the bug, provide the fixed code, and finally explain the differences before and after the fix. If you click "Run Code," it executes the fixed code in a sandbox and returns the output — a seamless end-to-end process.

On mobile, DeepSeek's iOS/Android apps are also quite mature, supporting voice input (accurate Mandarin recognition), photo upload, and chat history sync. The app has long topped the productivity category download charts on the China App Store — a rare achievement for a domestic Chinese AI product. Note that during peak hours (usually 10:00–12:00 and 14:00–17:00 on domestic workdays) you may hit service congestion, with response times stretching from the normal 3–5 seconds to 15–30 seconds.

FAQ

Q: Is DeepSeek Chat completely free?
A: The web and app versions are currently completely free for regular users, with no daily limits. However, if too many users are online simultaneously, free users may face queuing. The paid API targets developers and business users.

Q: Can DeepSeek see the files I upload?
A: Uploaded files are sent to DeepSeek's servers for processing. The company officially states it does not sell user data, but it's advisable not to upload files containing personal identity information or business secrets. Privacy-conscious users can download the open-source model and deploy it locally.

Q: What's the difference between DeepSeek R1 and DeepSeek V4?
A: R1 is a model focused on reasoning (similar to OpenAI o1), extremely strong at math and programming reasoning but slower to respond. V4 is the general conversational model, faster and more comprehensive, suited for everyday questions and content generation. On chat.deepseek.com, you can invoke R1 reasoning mode via the "Deep Think" toggle.

Q: Why is the response sometimes very slow?
A: DeepSeek has a huge user base, and servers are heavily loaded during peak hours (weekday daytime), which may cause response delays. If slowness persists, try using it outside peak hours or via API relay services such as OpenRouter.
