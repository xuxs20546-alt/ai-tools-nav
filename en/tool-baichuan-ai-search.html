Baichuan AI Search In-Depth Review

The vertical AI search engine from Baichuan Intelligence, built on the Baichuan LLM, with clear advantages in medical health and professional knowledge retrieval

📖 Overview

Baichuan AI Search is a vertical AI search engine launched by Baichuan Intelligence in October 2024, founded by Wang Xiaochuan, former CEO of Sogou. Unlike other AI search engines that pursue "full-domain coverage," Baichuan AI Search has followed a "deep vertical" strategy from day one, positioning medical health and legal services as its two core vertical scenarios. This choice is closely tied to its founder's background — during his Sogou era, Wang Xiaochuan championed Sogou Mingyi (a medical vertical search project), accumulating extensive experience in medical search and knowledge graphs. Under the hood, Baichuan AI Search is powered by Baichuan's self-developed Baichuan series LLMs, whose medical-domain fine-tuning data includes more than 5 million high-quality medical literature entries and clinical cases.

The core advantage of Baichuan AI Search in medical scenarios comes down to one word: "trustworthiness" — every AI-generated piece of medical information is automatically labeled with an evidence level (e.g., from peer-reviewed journals, authoritative medical institution websites, or personal blogs) and accompanied by a link to the original source so users can verify it themselves. In the drug lookup feature, Baichuan AI Search simultaneously displays key information from the drug insert, data summaries of relevant clinical studies, and potential drug interaction warnings. In 2025, Baichuan AI Search obtained internet medical information service qualifications, further consolidating its compliance advantage in medical information retrieval. Baichuan Intelligence says it will gradually expand its vertical search capabilities from medicine to law, education, finance, and more.

✨ Pros & Cons Analysis

👍 Strengths

  • Evidence-labeling system for medical queries sets an industry standard: Baichuan labels every piece of medical information with an evidence level and source type (authoritative guidelines/clinical trials/expert opinions/personal experience). This level of transparency is extremely rare among AI search products and helps users judge information credibility
  • Outstanding completeness and accuracy in drug information retrieval: for drug interaction queries, Baichuan AI Search can simultaneously compare cross-reactions between Western and Chinese medicines, with information sourced from the National Medical Products Administration database, ensuring both accuracy and timeliness
  • Deeper understanding of professional terminology than general search: thanks to the Baichuan LLM's targeted training on medical and legal corpora, its comprehension and phrasing depth for queries involving professional terms (such as "EGFR-T790M mutation" or the "principle of changed circumstances") is clearly superior to general AI search engines

👎 Weaknesses

  • Extremely limited search coverage outside medicine: if you search "what exhibitions are in Beijing today" or "Python asyncio usage" on Baichuan AI Search, the answer quality may be worse than a plain Google search — it hasn't built advantage indexes in these domains yet
  • Commercialization leads some answers to favor the Baichuan ecosystem: search results occasionally feature recommendations for Baichuan's own products (such as the Baichuan AI assistant), and these recommendations are labeled less transparently than external results, raising "self-promotion" concerns
  • Mobile experience still needs major optimization: the mobile web pages of Baichuan AI Search load slowly (average first screen time around 3.8 seconds), and AI summaries for complex queries often suffer layout glitches, putting the mobile experience at least one tier behind the desktop version

🎯 Ideal Use Cases

  • Preliminary information lookup for everyday health questions — when you want to understand a symptom (e.g., "recurrent headaches with nausea — what could it be"), Baichuan AI Search provides a list of possible causes based on authoritative sources along with medical advice, each labeled with an evidence level, helping you build a basic understanding before seeing a doctor.
  • Drug interaction checks across multiple medications — if an elderly family member is taking five medications at once, you can enter each drug name into Baichuan AI Search one by one, and the system automatically cross-checks interaction data from drug inserts, flagging potentially risky combinations to watch.
  • Legal scenario queries for regulations and case law — when you need to quickly understand a legal concept or find relevant case law (e.g., "the latest standards for economic compensation upon contract termination"), Baichuan AI Search synthesizes accurate answers from legal databases and judgment document repositories.

💡 Usage Tips

  • Always read the evidence labels after health queries: Baichuan's greatest value lies in its evidence-labeling system. Don't close the page right after reading the AI summary — spend at least 30 seconds checking the high/medium/low evidence level and source link at the end of each conclusion
  • Treat Baichuan as the "first stop" for medical information, not the "final destination": AI-generated information (even with evidence levels) cannot replace a doctor's professional diagnosis. Baichuan AI Search is best for "doing your homework" — understanding the basics and preparing questions before visiting a doctor
  • Pay attention to the "last updated" label on drug information: drug inserts and clinical guidelines are updated periodically. Baichuan's drug lookup results include a data update timestamp at the bottom — always confirm the information isn't stale data from over a year ago before citing it

Core Features

Medical health vertical search: this is Baichuan AI Search's core differentiator. After entering a symptom, disease name, or drug name in the search box, the system extracts information from authoritative medical literature, drug inserts, and clinical guidelines to generate structured AI answers. Each conclusion is labeled with an evidence level (high/medium/low) and source type (peer-reviewed journal/authoritative medical institution/personal experience) to help users judge how reliable the information is.

Drug interaction check: users can enter multiple drug names at once, and Baichuan AI Search automatically compares drug inserts in the National Medical Products Administration database, listing potential drug interaction risks and precautions. For chronic disease patients or elderly households taking multiple medications, this is an extremely practical tool.

Legal knowledge retrieval: beyond medicine, Baichuan has also made targeted optimizations for searching laws, regulations, and judicial precedents. Enter a legal question (e.g., "calculation standards for economic compensation upon contract termination"), and the system cites the latest legal provisions and relevant judgment documents, noting the effective date and scope of application of each statute.

Academic literature-assisted search: for medical students and researchers, Baichuan AI Search supports filtering results by study type (RCT/systematic review/case report) and can generate an initial draft framework for literature reviews. While it can't replace professional reference management tools, it's a highly efficient entry point for quickly understanding a research direction.

Getting Started Experience

On the Baichuan AI Search official website, the homepage features a clean search box, consistent with the interface style of Google or Baidu. After entering a query, AI answers are generated within 3-5 seconds. The structure of medical answers is especially clear: it sequentially shows "Possible Causes," "Recommended Tests," "When to See a Doctor," and "References," with each section distinguished by different icons and background colors.

Take "recurrent headaches with nausea — what could it be" as an example: Baichuan lists 5-6 possibilities such as migraine, tension-type headache, and cervicogenic headache, each with typical symptom descriptions and distinguishing features. At the bottom of the answer, there's a prominent red notice: "The above is AI analysis and cannot replace a doctor's diagnosis. If symptoms persist or worsen, seek medical attention promptly."

The drug lookup interface is especially commendable — enter "amoxicillin + ibuprofen," and the system not only displays basic information about both drugs but also flags the interaction note "the two can usually be combined, but watch for gastrointestinal irritation risk" and suggests an appropriate dosing interval.

Advantages & Limitations

Advantages: the evidence-labeling system for medical information is unique among AI search products, providing verifiable grounds for information credibility; the drug interaction check feature is directly connected to the National Medical Products Administration database, combining practicality and accuracy; professional terminology comprehension is clearly superior to general AI search in vertical domains.

Limitations: search coverage outside medicine is extremely limited, and answers to everyday knowledge queries may be worse than Baidu or Metaso; during the commercialization phase, there's an occasional tendency to promote Baichuan's own content; mobile web page loading speed and layout still have room for improvement.

Who It's For

FAQ

Q: Can Baichuan AI Search's medical advice replace a doctor?
A: No. Medical information from Baichuan AI Search is for public education and reference only and cannot replace the diagnosis and treatment advice of a licensed physician. If you feel unwell, please visit a formal medical institution promptly.

Q: What types of searches does Baichuan AI Search support?
A: Currently, medical health and legal knowledge are the core scenarios. It also supports basic general search, but answer quality in areas like lifestyle, entertainment, and tech news doesn't match mainstream general search engines.

Q: How is the evidence level determined?
A: High level corresponds to peer-reviewed journals, authoritative medical institution websites, and official guidelines; medium level corresponds to professional books and well-known medical media; low level corresponds to personal blogs and non-professional sources.

Q: Does Baichuan AI Search have a mobile app?
A: According to official website information, Baichuan currently provides services mainly through its web pages, with the mobile experience based on responsive web design. The launch date of a standalone app has not been publicly disclosed.
