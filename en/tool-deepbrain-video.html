About DeepBrain AI

DeepBrain AI is a Korean AI company founded in 2016. Its flagship product, AI Studios (formerly AI STUDIOS), is a video auto-generation platform built on AI digital humans (AI avatars). Users simply enter a text script and choose an AI virtual presenter (avatar), and the platform automatically generates a video in which the digital human "narrates" the content with natural speech, expressions, and synchronized lip movements. The core of DeepBrain AI's technology lies in its highly realistic face synthesis and lip-sync capabilities — the platform's AI avatars not only match lip movements precisely to the audio but also feature natural blinking, gestures, and subtle head movements, placing the overall output at the leading edge of the industry.

AI Studios currently supports speech synthesis in over 150 languages and more than 100 preset AI avatars (including figures of different ages, genders, and ethnicities). Enterprise users can also create custom "brand avatars" — after uploading footage of real people, DeepBrain AI trains an AI persona exclusive to that company. This "digital human spokesperson" model spread rapidly in 2025-2026 and is now widely used in corporate training videos, product introductions, news broadcasts, and e-commerce livestreams. DeepBrain AI's customers include major Korean enterprises such as Samsung, LG, and KB Kookmin Bank, as well as Chinese clients including ByteDance and Lenovo.

Core Features

1. AI Avatar Digital Human Video Generation: This is AI Studios' core feature. Users enter a text script, select an AI avatar and background scene, and the platform automatically generates a video of the digital human appearing on camera to present the content. AI avatars can display PPTs, images, and video materials as backgrounds or picture-in-picture elements, replicating the visual effect of a real human presenter. The lip-sync engine ensures the avatar's lip movements precisely match the speech (powered by TTS synthesis in 150+ languages), making it difficult even for non-professionals to tell that the video is AI-generated.

2. Custom Brand Avatars: Enterprises can upload real video footage of their spokesperson or CEO, and DeepBrain AI trains a dedicated AI avatar model based on that material. This custom avatar closely resembles the real person in appearance, voice, and mannerisms, and can take over large volumes of video recording work. For businesses that need continuous video output (such as online education providers and e-commerce brands), custom avatars save substantial shooting time and cost.

3. ChatGPT-Integrated Script Generation: AI Studios includes built-in integration with ChatGPT — users simply enter a topic (e.g., "introduce our company's newly released smartwatch"), and the AI automatically generates a well-structured video script with a moderate speaking pace. Once generated, the script can be imported directly into the video editor for fine-tuning wording and sections.

4. Multi-Scenario Template Library: The platform offers hundreds of scene templates covering common video types such as corporate training, product demos, news broadcasts, educational courses, and social media marketing. Each template comes preloaded with an AI avatar style, background, and text layout suited to that scenario, so users can quickly produce finished videos by simply replacing the text and images.

Getting Started

The AI Studios workflow is designed to be highly intuitive. After registering (via Google or email), the main interface shows a "Create New Video" onboarding card. Clicking it takes you through a three-step workflow:

Step 1, "Choose an avatar" — browse the AI avatar library, filtering by language, gender, style, and other attributes; each avatar has a preview video so you can see how it speaks and moves. Step 2, "Enter a script" — paste text directly or auto-generate it with the ChatGPT assistant. The platform estimates video duration in real time and offers a speaking-speed slider. Step 3, "Choose a scene" — pick a visual style from the template library and upload images or PPTs you want to display.

Clicking "Generate" starts video rendering. Depending on video length and resolution, rendering takes anywhere from a few minutes to over ten. Once complete, you can preview and adjust online (edit text or swap avatars), then export as an MP4 file or share a link directly. The overall workflow is very smooth — even someone with no video production experience can produce their first AI avatar video within 30 minutes. It's worth noting that the naturalness of Chinese-language avatars' speech and lip-sync has improved noticeably over the past two versions, though some polyphonic characters and proper nouns still require manual correction.

Strengths and Limitations

Strengths: First, the realism of the AI avatars leads the industry — particularly lip-sync precision and natural gestures and micro-expressions, far surpassing most competitors. Second, the language coverage is extremely broad — speech synthesis in 150+ languages spans all major global markets, making it especially useful for companies expanding overseas and cross-border teams. Third, video production efficiency is exceptional — the entire process from script to finished video can be completed in under 30 minutes, whereas traditional live shoots take at least several days from booking to post-production.

Limitations: First, a degree of artificiality remains — while lip-sync and gestures are excellent, the digital human's non-human feel becomes noticeable over longer viewing, which may fall short for premium brand campaigns that need deep emotional connection. Second, the naturalness of Chinese digital humans still has room for improvement — compared with Korean and English avatars, Chinese speech synthesis is relatively flat in tone variation and emotional expression. Third, custom avatars are expensive — enterprise-grade custom digital humans typically cost tens of thousands to over one hundred thousand US dollars, beyond the budget of many small and medium businesses.

FAQ

Q: How do I choose between DeepBrain AI and Synthesia?
A: If you need multilingual support (especially Asian languages like Korean, Japanese, and Chinese), DeepBrain AI offers superior speech naturalness and avatar realism. If your team is in Europe and only makes English videos, Synthesia's interface is cleaner and easier to use. Both offer free trials — we recommend trying both before deciding.

Q: How long does it take to create a custom avatar?
A: According to DeepBrain AI's official information, custom avatar training and delivery typically takes 2-6 weeks, depending on the level of detail required and the scope of customization.

Q: Can the generated videos be used commercially?
A: Videos generated on paid plans (Starter and above) generally include commercial usage rights. Free-plan videos carry a watermark and cannot be used commercially. Refer to the service agreement for specific terms.

Q: Does it support live streaming?
A: AI Studios is currently positioned primarily as an offline video generation tool and doesn't directly support live streaming. However, DeepBrain AI offers an API, so technically capable teams can build their own live-streaming setup by combining the API with real-time streaming tools.
