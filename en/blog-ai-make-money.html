How to Make Money with AI Tools: 10 Proven Practical Methods

2025-04-28 · 12 min read · 💰

The Era of AI Monetization

In 2025, AI tools have evolved from novelties into productivity tools. More and more people are using AI to generate income — it's no longer just for the tech world, but a monetization opportunity available to every ordinary person. ChatGPT surpassed 100 million users just two months after launch, and Midjourney enables people with zero drawing skills to create stunning artwork. AI is redefining the boundaries of "personal capability."

But the information gap still exists: many people know AI is powerful, but don't know exactly how to use it to make money. This article compiles 10 proven AI money-making methods suitable for ordinary people at different skill levels. Whether you're a student, office worker, or stay-at-home parent, you can find a direction that fits you.

1. Make Money with AI Writing

Use ChatGPT, Claude, and other AI writing tools to write articles, product descriptions, and SEO content. Recommended platforms: Fiverr, Upwork, and freelance marketplaces. Experienced writers can earn 5,000-20,000 RMB per month.

Writing goes far beyond articles: WeChat Official Account posts (800-2,000 words, 200-800 RMB per piece), e-commerce product descriptions (20-50 RMB each), SEO advertorials (800-1,500 words, 300-500 RMB per piece), and Zhihu engagement replies (50-200 RMB each). A highly recommended niche is Xiaohongshu (RED) recommendation copy — 300-600 words per piece, high demand, 100-300 RMB per piece, and AI-assisted completion in 10 minutes. Start by building a portfolio (write a few free samples to showcase), then gradually raise your rates. In terms of stability, the content industry never dies — writing freelancing is one of the most mature and stable AI side-hustle tracks.

2. Make Money with AI Art

Use Midjourney and Stable Diffusion to generate artwork and sell it on platforms like Etsy, Xiaohongshu, and Zcool. Suitable for: phone wallpapers, poster templates, NFT art. Single wallpapers sell for 5-30 RMB, and bundled sets are even more popular.

This direction has several high-margin niches: AI-generated T-shirt pattern designs (upload to Redbubble, Printful — earn 10-30% royalties per sale, truly passive income); sticker and emoji creation (on WeChat's Sticker Platform, popular creators can earn thousands per month in revenue share); and commercial stock materials for businesses (AI-generated images free of copyright risk, companies are more willing to pay).

3. Make Money with AI Video

Use Kling AI, Sora, Runway, and other tools to create short video content and operate channels on Douyin, YouTube, and Bilibili. AI-generated short videos are more likely to gain traffic. A hit AI short drama on Douyin can generate millions of views and substantial creator incentives.

AI video has three main monetization models: ad revenue sharing (platform incentives + brand sponsorships), with a 10K-follower video channel earning 2,000-8,000 RMB per month; selling courses alongside free content (post free AI tutorial videos to drive traffic to paid courses), where a 199 RMB AI video course selling 100 copies brings nearly 20,000 RMB; and creating AI promotional videos and product demos for businesses, priced at 500-5,000 RMB each.

4. Make Money with AI Music

Use Suno or Udio to generate background music, sell to content creators and game developers, or publish on music platforms for royalty income. By 2025, platforms like NetEase Cloud Music and QQ Music have officially embraced AI music.

Suno's V4 version can generate full songs over 4 minutes long with Chinese lyrics support. Monetization models: register as a musician on NetEase Cloud Music, upload AI original songs, and earn from streaming revenue — per-song income may be modest, but scaling up yields substantial passive monthly income; list AI background music on stock audio sites like Pond5 and AudioJungle at $5-50 per track; create custom BGM for podcasters and video creators at 200-500 RMB per track.

5. Make Money with AI Coding

Use Cursor, GitHub Copilot, and other AI coding tools to take on website development, mini-program development, and other freelance projects. Even if you're not a professional programmer, AI can handle most of the coding work for you. A simple company website developed with AI assistance can be completed in two days, priced at 3,000-8,000 RMB.

For absolute beginners, start with low-code + AI: use Bolt.new or v0 to directly generate React/Next.js web pages, then take on simple small-website projects on freelancing platforms. Or specialize in a niche like "AI-powered WeChat Mini Program store setup for merchants" — once you master it, it becomes a standardized workflow. We recommend Cursor (integrated with Claude/GPT-4o), an AI editor that understands your entire project context and helps you debug and refactor code.

6. Make Money with AI Translation

Use DeepL and ChatGPT to provide high-quality translation services and take orders on translation platforms. AI translation combined with human proofreading is 3-5 times more efficient than purely manual work. A traditional translator handles 3,000-5,000 words per day; AI-assisted translation easily reaches 10,000-15,000 words.

The translation market is far larger than you think: subtitle translation (movies/TV shows/YouTube videos), document translation (contracts/papers/product manuals), and e-commerce translation (cross-border product titles and descriptions) are all in high demand. Rates vary by language difficulty: Chinese-English translation at 80-150 RMB per 1,000 characters, Chinese-Japanese at 120-200 RMB per 1,000 characters. On freelance translation platforms, full-time AI-assisted translators typically earn 6,000-15,000 RMB per month.

7. Make Money with AI Design

Use Canva AI, Figma AI, Looka, and other tools to take on logo design, poster design, PPT beautification, and other design work. AI enables people with zero design background to produce professional-quality visual work.

Popular directions: logo design (200-800 RMB each — AI generates drafts, then fine-tune), e-commerce main images and detail pages (50-100 RMB per set, Taobao and Pinduoduo merchants are the main clients), PPT beautification (50-200 RMB per file, higher margins for custom orders). Primary channels for getting orders are Xianyu and Xiaohongshu; when starting out, offer a few "free revision" deals to attract clients.

8. Make Money with AI Courses and Teaching

Package your AI experience into courses and sell them on platforms like Zhihu, Dedao, and Udemy. AI tutorials are one of the hottest knowledge-pay tracks of 2025 — because the biggest gap between people is no longer "whether you can use AI," but "what results you've achieved with AI."

Courses can start from highly specific, practical angles: "30 Days to Master Midjourney Commercial Output from Zero," "Use AI to Write Xiaohongshu Copy That Earns 5,000 RMB a Month," "AI Saves Office Workers 10 Hours a Week." Price at 99-399 RMB, and use free content on Xiaohongshu and video channels to drive traffic. Annual incomes of 100,000-500,000 RMB are common among knowledge-pay creators in this track.

9. Make Money with AI-Powered E-commerce

Use AI to generate product images, write product descriptions, and handle customer service conversations. Reduce e-commerce operating costs and improve conversion rates. Photoshop AI's generative fill can turn a casual product photo into a professional studio shot.

Specific ways to make money: run your own AI-assisted e-commerce store, where one person manages 5-10 stores (AI handles descriptions, customer service, and data analysis); provide AI operations services for others' stores at 2,000-5,000 RMB per month; specialize in AI product image services — help sellers change backgrounds, add models, and adjust lighting in one click at 5-20 RMB per image, easily earning hundreds daily with batch processing.

10. Make Money with AI Digital Human Livestreaming

Use HeyGen, Tencent Smart Video, and other digital human tools for 24/7 livestream selling. One person can operate multiple livestream rooms simultaneously. Digital humans can answer viewer questions in real time and demonstrate products, dramatically lowering the human resource barrier to livestreaming.

The startup cost for one AI digital human livestream room: HeyGen basic plan at $24/month + one computer + product selection (you can use 1688 dropshipping without holding inventory). 24/7 non-stop livestreaming means your traffic window covers all time slots. Well-run digital human livestream rooms can earn 10,000-50,000 RMB per month in sales commissions. Of course, platform policies are tightening — we recommend proper compliance labeling and maintaining content quality.

Advice for Beginners

Choose one direction and dive deep — don't spread yourself too thin. Mastering one AI tool thoroughly is more valuable than dabbling in ten. Earning your first dollar matters more than planning to earn ten thousand. AI hasn't changed the essence of business — you still need to understand users, consistently deliver output, and build reputation. The difference is that AI lets you do what once required a small team, all by yourself.
