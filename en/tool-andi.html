📖 Overview

Andi is an AI search engine that reimagines the search experience — it doesn't show ten blue links like Google, nor does it simply output a text answer like Perplexity. Instead, Andi's results page is a "visual knowledge panel": an AI-generated summary sits at the top, followed by refined chat-like cards, each one a clear explanation synthesized by AI from multiple web pages, complete with source links. The advantage of this layout is that you can browse search results the way you scroll through a chat history, instead of jumping back and forth between ten links.

Another big selling point of Andi is its total no-ad commitment — now and in the future, Andi will not insert ads into search results; its business model relies on a future Pro subscription rather than advertising. Because the results interface is designed like a feed, it's especially friendly on mobile. Andi also includes a "Reader View" mode — with one click, you can turn any cited web page into a clean, text-only reading view, stripping out ads, popups and cluttered layouts. For everyday information queries, news searches and technical questions, Andi is an increasingly attractive Google alternative.

✨ Strengths & Weaknesses

✅ Strengths

  • Ad-free, clean experience: no ads means no SEO-driven junk content, and result relevance is noticeably higher than Google's.
  • Card-based browsing: browse results like scrolling a message feed — no need to click into links and back out, reducing friction in finding information.
  • Especially efficient for simple queries: simple factual questions like "Who is XX", "Where are good sushi places" or "How big is React" are answered far faster by Andi than by Google.

⚠️ Weaknesses

  • Deep research less capable than Perplexity: Andi favors quick, accurate short answers over deep multi-turn research — complex questions fall short of Perplexity's Pro Search.
  • Almost no Chinese content support: Andi's search sources are mainly English websites, so Chinese information retrieval is nearly unusable.
  • Product still in early stage: features and stability lag mature search engines, and occasionally it shows blank pages or very few results.

🎯 Use Cases

  • Everyday lightweight search: "What day is it today", "What else has this actor been in", "How do you spell XX" — for questions that don't require clicking into links, Andi answers instantly.
  • Mobile information lookup: use Andi in a mobile browser — the card layout naturally suits small screens, with no zooming or precise link tapping required.
  • Escaping an ad-filled search experience: tired of Google's first three screens being all ads and SEO filler — Andi provides a clean search experience.

💡 Tips

  • Use Andi for everyday questions, Perplexity for research: "today's weather" or "an actor's age" — Andi is fastest; "analyze the AI chip supply chain" — use Perplexity. Each tool has its own role.
  • Set it as your mobile browser's default search engine: in Chrome/Safari mobile you can set Andi as the default search engine — the experience is far better than Google's, especially when you're on a limited data plan.
  • English users benefit most: if your daily searches are mostly in English, Andi is an excellent Google alternative; users who search mainly in Chinese should wait a bit.

🌊 Overview (Additional)

Andi was developed by a US startup and is positioned as the "next-generation AI search engine". Unlike Google's list of links, Andi organizes answers directly with generative AI: search results appear as conversational cards, each generated by AI from multiple sources and accompanied by citation links. The official description: "search as if chatting with a smart friend".

🔧 Core Features

Semantic search & AI summaries: Andi uses semantic search to understand the intent behind a question rather than simply matching keywords. At the top of the results is a direct AI-generated answer, with explanatory cards synthesizing multiple web pages below, each labeled with sources for easy verification.

Conversational follow-ups: after a search you can keep asking questions, and Andi answers in context — ideal for gradually exploring a topic.

Reader View: one click turns a cited web page into a clean, text-only view, removing ads, popups and cluttered layouts so you can focus on the content.

Ad-free & privacy-friendly: Andi promises not to insert ads into search results and states it does not collect user data for ad targeting; the business model relies on a future subscription service.

🧭 Getting Started

Open andisearch.com and type your question directly — no registration needed. Ask "Who was the 16th US president" and the page delivers an answer card with sources; ask a comparison question (like "the difference between React and Vue") and it generates a comparative explanation. On mobile browsers you can set Andi as the default search engine — everyday lightweight queries run smoothly.

💲 Pricing

Andi is currently completely free and ad-free. The official team has said a subscription version will come in the future; specific pricing and features have not yet been announced — check the official site for the latest.

✅ Strengths & Limitations

Strengths: ad-free with little SEO junk content and highly relevant answers; the card-based interface works well on mobile; extremely fast on simple factual queries.

Limitations: deep multi-turn research is weaker than tools like Perplexity; search sources are mainly English content, so Chinese retrieval is limited; the product is still in early stage, with occasional blank results.
