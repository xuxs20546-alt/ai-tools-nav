Tool Overview

Cutout Pro is an online visual editing platform centered on AI image processing, developed by a team focused on computer vision technology. The platform's core positioning is "one-stop AI image editing" – offering AI automatic background removal, background replacement, image inpainting, photo enhancement, ID photo creation, cartoon avatar generation, AI video background removal, and more. Unlike traditional image editing software (such as Photoshop), every operation in Cutout Pro is completed automatically by AI – users typically just upload an image, and the AI finishes processing within seconds and returns the result.

Cutout Pro's technology stack is built on deep learning image segmentation models, and it excels at hair-level fine cutout – the most time-consuming and technically demanding scenario for traditional cutout tools. The platform supports images and videos up to 4K resolution, and provides an API for developers to integrate AI image processing capabilities into their own applications. Currently, Cutout Pro has a large user base in e-commerce product image processing, ID photo creation, and social media content creation.

Core Features

AI smart cutout / background removal is Cutout Pro's signature feature and the main driver of user growth. Users upload a photo containing a person, and the AI automatically identifies the subject and removes the background, typically in 3–8 seconds. Compared with traditional tools like Photoshop, where users manually cut out subjects with lasso/pen tools, Cutout Pro handles edge details such as hair strands, feathers, and transparent objects not only hundreds of times faster but often with higher precision – because the AI model has been trained on massive edge-case datasets and has learned how to handle semi-transparent pixels at complex boundaries.

AI ID photo creation is one of Cutout Pro's most popular features in the Chinese market. Users upload a selfie or a half-length photo, and the AI automatically handles background replacement (white, blue, red, and other standard background colors), subject centering and cropping, skin tone optimization, and size adaptation (one-inch, two-inch, visa photos, etc.). The whole process takes less than 10 seconds, and the result can be downloaded or printed directly. This feature solves the pain point of queuing at photo studios and retaking photos again and again.

AI photo enhancement and restoration can automatically improve the quality of low-quality photos. This includes: denoising (reducing noise in low-light environments), sharpening (improving clarity of blurry photos), color enhancement (automatically adjusting exposure and color balance), and old photo restoration (removing creases, stains, and fading). For families with large collections of old photos needing restoration, this feature is extremely valuable.

AI video background removal is a recently added upgrade. Users can upload a short video clip, and the AI identifies and removes the background frame by frame, with support for exporting videos with a transparent channel (for compositing onto other scenes). This feature consumes credits based on video length, and is suitable for short-video creators who need green-screen effects.

Hands-On Experience

After visiting the Cutout Pro official website, you can try the basic features without registering. The homepage is an upload area – just choose the feature you want and drag an image in. Take "Background Removal" as an example: after uploading a portrait photo, the result appears in about 3–5 seconds – the original image on the left, and the transparent-background result on the right. You can switch background colors to preview the effect, or upload a new background image for compositing. When satisfied, click download – the free version outputs a low-resolution preview with a watermark, while the paid version allows downloading high-definition, lossless images.

The ID photo creation experience is also very smooth. After uploading a photo, the AI automatically detects the face area and crops and centers it. You can choose the background color (white/blue/red/gradient), photo size (one-inch/two-inch/small one-inch, etc.), and print layout (4 per sheet / 6 per sheet). The generated ID photos are closer to professional photo studio results in skin tone reproduction and facial proportions than direct processing of phone selfies, and can meet the photo requirements of most civil affairs and visa agencies.

Overall, Cutout Pro packages the professional power of AI image processing into an ultra-simple "upload – process – download" three-step flow. Even users who know nothing about image editing can achieve professional-grade results within minutes. The platform's response speed is stable; when processing large batches of images, the paid plan is recommended to avoid queuing.

Strengths and Limitations

Strengths: Cutout Pro achieves industry-leading results in hair-level fine cutout – its handling of semi-transparent edge pixels and complex contours far surpasses traditional tools and one-click competitors. The workflow is extremely simple and requires no image editing knowledge whatsoever – truly "upload and done". Its feature matrix covers the complete AI image processing landscape, from cutout and restoration to ID photos and video backgrounds. The open API lets developers embed these capabilities into their own workflows or products.

Limitations: The free version is heavily restricted – low resolution and forced watermarks make it feel more like a "trial" than a complete free plan. On extremely complex edges (such as wind-blown stray hair or fine mesh fabrics), there can still be occasional boundary blurring or breakage. Subscription pricing has no absolute advantage over professional tools like Adobe; if you also need graphic design and layout features, a Photoshop subscription may be more cost-effective than buying Cutout Pro alone.

Who Is It For

  • E-commerce operators and designers: e-commerce professionals who process large volumes of white-background product images daily – Cutout Pro's batch cutout can replace manual Photoshop work.
  • Social media content creators: creators who need to quickly produce creative composite images, background-swapped selfies, and short-video effects.
  • Everyday consumers: regular users who need to make ID photos, restore old photos, or remove passersby from travel photos.
  • Developers: technical teams that need to integrate AI cutout/enhancement capabilities into their own web or mobile applications.

FAQ

Q: What is the difference between Cutout Pro and Photoshop's automatic cutout?
A: Photoshop's AI cutout is comparable to Cutout Pro for single objects, but Cutout Pro has the edge in fine edges (especially hair) and batch processing. However, Photoshop offers manual refinement – when the AI isn't perfect, you can correct it with manual tools, which Cutout Pro currently lacks.

Q: Can the ID photo feature be used for official passport and visa applications?
A: The generated ID photos meet most standards in size and background color, but different countries have different requirements (such as facial size ratio and expression rules). We recommend confirming the photo specifications of the target agency before submitting.

Q: What video formats does video background removal support?
A: It supports common MP4 and MOV formats, with a maximum file size of 2GB and resolution up to 4K. Video processing consumes credits per second, at $0.15/second.

Q: Will my images be saved or used to train AI?
A: According to Cutout Pro's privacy policy, user images are automatically deleted after processing within a certain period and are not used to train AI models. For specific retention periods and data handling details, please refer to the privacy policy on the official website.
