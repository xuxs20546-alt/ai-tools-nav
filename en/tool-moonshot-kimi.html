Overview

Kimi is an AI assistant launched by Moonshot AI in October 2023. Its founder, Yang Zhilin, holds a PhD in AI from Tsinghua University and Carnegie Mellon University, and previously worked on large-model research at Google Brain and Meta AI. What stunned the market most was Kimi's ultra-long context capability — in March 2024, Kimi became the first to announce lossless context comprehension of 2 million Chinese characters, setting a new record for context length among consumer-grade AI products worldwide at the time. In plain terms, you can feed the entire The Three-Body Problem trilogy (about 900,000 characters) to Kimi in one go, then ask questions about any detail in the book — Kimi's answers are accurate down to specific chapters and paragraphs.

Kimi's growth was equally striking: within just 6 months of launch, its monthly active users surpassed 20 million, making it one of the fastest-growing AI apps in China in 2024. The Kimi Explorer edition launched in 2025 further strengthened research scenarios — in that mode, Kimi automatically breaks complex questions into sub-questions, searches dozens of web pages in parallel, cross-validates the results, and outputs a structured research report. Throughout the process, users barely notice the multi-step reasoning happening in the background. Kimi also offers file upload, supporting parsing and Q&A over mainstream document formats such as PDF, Word, PPT, and Excel, and has processed more than 1 billion file uploads to date.

✨ Pros & Cons

👍 Strengths

  • Unmatched long-text processing: the 2-million-character context window remains unsurpassed among consumer-grade products, and in real-world "needle-in-a-haystack" tests (finding deliberately inserted irrelevant information in ultra-long texts), Kimi's recall rate consistently stays above 99%
  • Polished user experience with an extremely low learning curve: minimal interface, fast responses, drag-and-drop file uploads. Kimi's design philosophy is "make AI as simple as WeChat", and in practice it delivers out-of-the-box usability with no tutorial needed
  • Excellent web search and information integration: in Explorer mode, Kimi automatically builds a chain of thought when generating research reports — searching first, then integrating, then summarizing — and finally outputs structured documents with clear citations, ideal for students and researchers doing literature research

👎 Limitations

  • Multimodal capabilities still stuck in the text stage: as of mid-2026, Kimi does not support native image generation or audio/video understanding. Uploaded images can only be mined for their text, not for visual understanding such as "how many cats are in this picture", which significantly limits multimedia creation scenarios
  • API pricing is on the high side with frequent quota changes: Kimi's monetization strategy saw multiple adjustments in the second half of 2025; the free tier's file upload size and daily limits went through 3 changes, and developers report difficulty planning stable project budgets
  • Limited availability and compliance overseas: Kimi mainly serves the mainland China market; overseas access suffers from latency and stability issues, and it lacks international data protection compliance certifications such as GDPR

🎯 Best Use Cases

  • Rapid review of long literature and legal documents — upload a 300-page M&A agreement to Kimi and enter "list all clauses about intellectual property ownership along with their page numbers"; Kimi returns the complete list within 30 seconds, a task that would take a human at least half a day of page-flipping.
  • Consolidating study materials for exams and academic prep — students can upload e-versions of multiple textbooks at once and ask Kimi to "compare how textbook A and textbook B present the same concept in Chapter 5", quickly locating knowledge gaps and building a cross-textbook knowledge network.
  • Automated drafting of industry research reports — open Kimi Explorer mode and enter "analyze the market size and key challenges of Chinese NEV exports in 2025"; the system automatically searches at least 20 sources and produces a complete report with data charts and descriptions.

💡 Usage Tips

  • Ask Kimi to build a "chapter index" before questioning long documents: for documents over 500 pages, don't jump straight to specific questions in the first round; instead have Kimi generate a full table of contents and a key-term index first. Subsequent questions will hit with significantly better accuracy and speed
  • Use Explorer mode for research tasks, and fast mode for everyday chats: casual conversation and simple Q&A don't need Explorer mode (it consumes more quota) — turn it on only when you truly need a research-grade "look it up first, then answer" response
  • Wait for parsing to finish after uploading files: Kimi needs 5–20 seconds to parse and index large files in the background; if you ask too soon, you may get a "file not yet parsed" reply. Just wait for the status prompt

Introduction

Kimi is an AI assistant launched by Moonshot AI in October 2023. Founder Yang Zhilin is an AI PhD from Tsinghua University and Carnegie Mellon University who previously worked on large-model research at Google Brain and Meta AI. The feature that most shook the market is its ultra-long context capability — in March 2024, Kimi was the first to announce lossless context comprehension of 2 million Chinese characters, setting a new record for context length among global consumer-grade AI products at the time. In plain terms, you can throw the entire The Three-Body Problem trilogy at Kimi at once and ask questions about any detail in the book, with answers accurate to specific chapters and paragraphs. Within just 6 months of launch, monthly active users surpassed 20 million; cumulative users now exceed 50 million, and cumulative file uploads exceed 1 billion. In 2025–2026, Kimi kept iterating, launching the Explorer edition, Kimi Code (a programming-specific plan), and multimodal capabilities based on the K3 model.

Core Features

1. 2-million-character ultra-long context: Kimi's core differentiator. In real-world "needle-in-a-haystack" tests, Kimi's information extraction accuracy exceeds 90% at positions beyond 1.5 million characters, and it can still pinpoint specific data points in the middle chapters of a full academic monograph.

2. Multi-format document parsing and Q&A: supports mainstream formats including PDF, Word, PPT, Excel, and TXT. The parsing engine correctly handles special cases in Chinese PDFs such as vertical text, mixed traditional/simplified characters, and blurry scanned fonts; OCR accuracy for scanned Chinese papers is about 92%.

3. Web search + files combined: processes both user-uploaded local files and web-searched information simultaneously, cross-comparing sources in a single response. This capability is extremely efficient when writing industry research reports.

4. Explorer edition deep research: automatically decomposes questions, searches multiple sources, cross-validates, and outputs a structured research report with citations. Suited to research scenarios requiring information gathering and comprehensive analysis.

5. Kimi Code (programming plan): a coding assistance plan for developers supporting code generation, debugging, and explanation. In early 2026, an announced permanent 3x quota boost took effect.

Hands-On Experience

Kimi is extremely simple to use — open the web app (kimi.moonshot.cn) or download the mobile app, log in with your phone number or WeChat, and you're in the chat interface. The interface is minimal: a history list on the left (searchable and renamable) and the conversation flow on the right. Files are uploaded by drag-and-drop or click, with multiple files supported at once. After uploading large files, the system needs 5–20 seconds to parse and index them; once done, you can start asking. Users can use the "@filename" command to precisely target the analysis object. Kimi responds quickly in standard mode (usually 5–15 seconds); Explorer mode takes 3–10 minutes but shows research progress in real time. The core design philosophy is "make AI as simple as WeChat", and in practice it truly is ready to use out of the box.

FAQ

Q: Is the 2-million-character context real?
A: In testing, Kimi's ultra-long context genuinely works. In "needle-in-a-haystack" tests (finding deliberately inserted irrelevant information in ultra-long texts), Kimi's recall rate exceeds 99%. You can upload the whole The Three-Body Problem trilogy and then locate a specific line of dialogue in a middle paragraph.

Q: What's the relationship between Kimi and Kimi Explorer?
A: Kimi Explorer is a built-in mode of Kimi, not a separate product. Toggle "Explorer" mode at the top of the chat interface to use it. Explorer plans its own research path and performs multi-source search and verification, suitable for deep research tasks.

Q: Can Kimi handle English documents?
A: Yes, but comprehension accuracy on long English documents is about 70–80% of its Chinese performance. If your main need is processing lots of English documents, Claude or ChatGPT may work better.

Q: Are uploaded documents used to train models?
A: Moonshot AI states that user-uploaded documents are not used to train models, but please check the latest privacy policy on the official website for specifics. If documents contain highly sensitive information, consider processing them locally or desensitizing them before uploading.
