🎼 Overview

AIVA (Artificial Intelligence Virtual Artist) was founded in 2016 by Luxembourg-based AIVA Technologies, making it one of the world's earliest AI music generation tools built for professional composition. Its most unique claim to fame: in 2016, AIVA became the first AI officially registered as a composer with SACEM, the French society of authors and composers, confirming the legal authorship of AI-generated music within the copyright system. AIVA focuses on instrumental composition in classical, orchestral, and film-score styles, serving video producers, game developers, ad agencies, and independent musicians — solving the problem of "no budget for a composer, but still needing high-quality orchestral scores."

🔧 Core Features

Style-based composition: AIVA ships dozens of preset styles covering Baroque, Classical, Romantic, modern film scoring, electronic symphony, Chinese style, and more. Pick a style, mood, duration, and instrumentation, and the model generates complete compositions, offering multiple candidate versions to audition and choose from.

Influence tracks: Upload a reference track you like, and AIVA analyzes its melody, harmony, and orchestration to generate new works in a similar style — far more precise than text descriptions.

Score & MIDI export: The output isn't just audio — it includes editable MIDI files and scores that can be imported into Logic Pro, Cubase, Sibelius, and other pro tools for note-by-note editing, ideal for both re-creation and orchestration study.

Commercial licensing: Music generated under paid plans comes with a commercial license for YouTube videos, games, ads, and other commercial projects, with clear copyright ownership.

🧭 Hands-On Experience

After registering at aiva.ai, you can enter the composition interface. Step one: pick a preset style (e.g., "Modern Film Score"). Step two: set the mood, tempo, and track duration. Step three: choose the instrumentation (string quartet, full orchestra, etc.) and hit generate. AIVA typically produces multiple versions within tens of seconds to a few minutes — audition each segment, and once satisfied, download the MP3 audio and MIDI file. The free account allows 3 downloads per month, so you can try it before deciding to subscribe.

✅ Pros & Limitations

Pros: In classical and orchestral styles, AIVA's harmony, counterpoint, and orchestration clearly outperform general-purpose music generators; it outputs editable scores and MIDI — "material" rather than a one-off finished track; as a SACEM-registered AI composer, it holds a first-mover advantage on copyright pathways in the industry.

Limitations: AIVA is a purely instrumental engine — no vocals or lyrics; multi-part orchestral generation takes time; the UI feels dated and has a learning curve; the free plan is restrictive and commercial use requires payment.

❓ FAQ

Can I use music generated by AIVA commercially?The free plan is for non-commercial use only; music generated under Standard and above includes a commercial license.

Can AIVA generate songs with lyrics?No — AIVA is a purely instrumental engine. For vocals and lyrics, check out tools like Suno or Udio.

Can generated files be edited in a DAW?Yes — paid plans export MIDI that can be imported into major digital audio workstations for further editing.

Is the free plan enough?The free plan includes 3 downloads per month — enough for evaluation and learning. For ongoing creation, upgrading to a paid plan is recommended.
