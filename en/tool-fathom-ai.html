AI note-taking assistant for Zoom meetings with auto-highlighting and summaries

📖 Overview

Fathom is an AI note-taking tool focused on Zoom meetings. Its design philosophy differs from Fireflies — Fathom prioritizes "immediate value" over "comprehensive archiving". During a Zoom meeting, Fathom displays live transcription in a sidebar and automatically uses AI to highlight "key moments": important decisions, action items, questions, and interesting insights. After the meeting ends, Fathom generates a concise structured summary with key timestamps — clicking a timestamp jumps directly to the corresponding segment of the meeting recording.

Fathom's differentiator is its "lightweight, immediate" experience — it appears as a sidebar in Zoom without disrupting the meeting flow, yet you can click the "Mark" button at any time to flag moments you consider important. This dual mechanism of "AI auto-annotation + manual proactive marking" makes meeting records more accurate and usable than pure AI solutions. Fathom also offers seamless integrations with CRMs (Salesforce, HubSpot) and project management tools (Notion, ClickUp). The basic plan is free (unlimited recordings), and Pro costs $19/month.

✨ Pros & Cons Analysis

👍 Pros

  • Best Zoom-specific optimization: as a Zoom-focused tool, the integration is smoother and more stable than general-purpose solutions
  • Key moment highlighting: AI auto-marks decisions, action items and questions, paired with manual marking for efficient meeting review
  • Generous free plan: unlimited recording and transcription, AI summaries, CRM integration and other core features are free

👎 Cons

  • Less feature depth than Fireflies: analysis and search capabilities are relatively basic, not ideal for enterprises needing deep meeting analysis

🎯 Best Use Cases

  • Heavy Zoom users — teams that use Zoom as their primary meeting platform; Fathom's seamless integration and free plan make it the best choice
  • Sales demo review — sales reps review customer questions and concerns flagged by Fathom after product demos to prepare follow-ups
  • Interview recording and review — HR uses Fathom to record interviews and flag key candidate answers for quick reference during later evaluation

💡 Usage Tips

  • Manually mark key moments: don't rely only on AI auto-annotation — click the mark button when you hear something important so you can quickly locate it later
  • Push summaries to your CRM: enable the Salesforce/HubSpot integration so summaries from every customer meeting are automatically recorded to the contact's activity timeline
  • Share with absentees: share the meeting summary and recording link generated by Fathom with colleagues who missed the meeting — far more accurate than relaying verbally

Core Features

Real-time Zoom transcription and highlighting: Fathom's core feature is displaying live conversation transcription in a sidebar during Zoom meetings, with AI auto-marking "key moments" — including important decisions, action items, questions raised, and interesting insights. Marked moments are saved with timestamps; clicking one jumps to the corresponding position in the recording for playback. This design lets users quickly find key segments without rewatching the entire meeting recording.

Structured AI summaries: After the meeting, Fathom automatically generates a concise summary containing: a meeting topic overview, participant list, discussion points arranged by timeline, assigned action items (with owners and due dates), and key decisions recorded. Summaries can be pushed with one click to CRMs (Salesforce, HubSpot), project management tools (Notion, ClickUp, Asana) and Slack channels — no manual copy-paste needed.

Dual mechanism: manual marking + AI auto-annotation: Fathom provides a one-click "Mark" button — when users hear something important during a meeting, clicking it flags that moment as high priority. This combination of "AI auto-annotation + manual proactive marking" makes meeting records both comprehensive and precise: AI covers all potentially important content, while manual marking ensures the most critical points are never missed.

AI search (Ask Fathom): Users can search all historical meeting records using natural language — for example, "Who mentioned performance optimization in the last product review meeting?" or "What pricing issues did we discuss in my customer meeting three months ago?" The system searches transcription content across meetings and returns relevant segments, saving the time of manually browsing meeting notes.

Deep CRM integration: Fathom achieves two-way synchronization with Salesforce and HubSpot. After every customer meeting, the summary is automatically appended to the corresponding contact's activity timeline; product interests and objections mentioned in meetings are also written into the CRM as structured tags, helping sales teams with follow-up.

Getting Started

Fathom is extremely easy to install and use. Users register an account at fathom.video (Google one-click sign-in supported), install the Fathom integration plugin from the Zoom App Marketplace, and from then on, whenever you start or join a meeting in Zoom, Fathom automatically appears as a sidebar in the meeting window. No need to manually click "Start Recording" before meetings — Fathom automatically kicks in when it detects a Zoom meeting has started. During the meeting, Fathom's sidebar quietly sits at the side of the screen, showing scrolling live transcription and a list of flagged key moments. Clicking the "Mark" button causes no UI disruption — it's just a lightweight recording action. Within minutes after the meeting ends, a summary email is sent to your registered inbox, and the summary is also available for viewing and sharing in the Fathom web console. The design philosophy of the entire experience is "you don't need to learn Fathom — it embeds itself into your Zoom workflow".

FAQ

Q: Does Fathom support platforms other than Zoom?
A: Fathom is currently deeply optimized primarily for Zoom. Support for Google Meet and Microsoft Teams is gradually expanding, but the experience and feature completeness of the current version are not on par with the Zoom version.

Q: Can other participants see that Fathom is recording?
A: When Fathom joins a meeting, other participants will see a "Fathom Notetaker" joined notification in the participant list and chat — this approach ensures transparency. For compliance-sensitive scenarios, Fathom's features also conform to legal requirements for meeting recording.

Q: Is the free plan really unlimited?
A: The free plan is indeed very generous for individual users — unlimited recording and transcription, AI summaries, and CRM integration are all included. However, team collaboration features (such as cross-member search and team dashboards) and advanced AI features (such as AI search) have usage limits and require upgrading to a paid plan.

Q: How well does it support Chinese meetings?
A: Fathom's transcription and summary models are primarily optimized for English. Transcription accuracy and summary quality for Chinese meetings will be lower than for English meetings. If your meetings are mostly in Chinese, Fireflies or Otter may perform better.
