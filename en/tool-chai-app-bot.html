Tool Overview

Chai is a social chat platform focused on AI character interactions, developed by Chai Research Corp. and officially launched in 2021. Unlike general-purpose assistants such as ChatGPT, Chai positions itself as a "social AI platform" — it lets users create, share, and chat with thousands of AI characters (called "Bots" in Chai). Each Bot has its own personality settings, speaking style, and knowledge background. Chai's core differentiator is its relatively loose content filtering, which gives AI characters more room for personality expression; at the same time, it adopts a "discover + recommend" product model — users see a leaderboard of popular AI characters on the homepage and can also search for interesting characters created by other users in the community. As of 2026, Chai has accumulated over 10 million downloads on iOS and Android, with millions of daily active users, forming a duopoly with Character.AI in the AI character chat space.

Core Features

Character creation & customization: users can create any number of AI Bots on Chai. The creation flow includes filling in the character name, avatar, bio (Personality Prompt), opening line, and conversation examples. Chai's character engine lets users define personality traits in natural language — for example, "You are a detective from the 1920s, slightly sarcastic, and fond of quoting literature" — and the AI generates a distinctive conversational style based on that description. Advanced users can further adjust parameters such as reply length preference and the creativity/accuracy balance.

Character discovery & social: Chai's homepage showcases community AI characters by popularity, category, and tag. Users can browse categories such as "Popular," "New," "Roleplay," "Emotional Companionship," and "Creative Writing," or search for Bots on specific topics by keyword. Each character page displays a bio, conversation snippet previews, and user ratings. Users can like their favorite Bots or share them with friends.

Multi-turn deep conversations: Chai's conversation model is optimized for character consistency — across dozens of turns, the AI character remembers previous interactions and maintains consistent personality and narrative, without suddenly breaking character. Conversations support text and emoji, and some versions support voice input.

Creator community: Chai provides a set of analytics tools for Bot creators — showing data on conversation volume, user retention, and ratings for their characters. Creators of popular Bots gain a degree of recognition within the community, forming a creator ecosystem similar to "AI character designers."

Getting Started

Chai is a mobile-first product — the iOS and Android apps are the primary entry points, while the web experience is relatively limited. After downloading the app, users can register with an email or phone number. On first entering the homepage, you'll see a list of recommended characters and can tap any of them to start chatting immediately. To create your own Bot, tap the "Create" button to enter the editor: fill in the character name, write a personality description in natural language, choose or upload an avatar image, and write an opening line as the character's first words to new users. The whole process takes just 2–3 minutes. Once created, the Bot appears in your personal list — you can test the conversation yourself first and publish it publicly to the community once you're satisfied. The chat interface is a standard bubble-style conversation with infinite scroll to review history.

Pros and Limitations

Pros: Chai's biggest advantage is its relatively loose content filtering — compared with Character.AI's strict NSFW filtering, Chai gives creators more room for character personality expression within reasonable bounds. The mobile experience is smooth, with good app load speeds and conversation latency. The popular character leaderboard and community discovery mechanisms make it easy for users to find interesting AI characters, avoiding the cold-start problem of "not knowing who to chat with." The creator analytics dashboard is also practical, letting people who take character design seriously see data and feedback.

Limitations: the free version has many ads and noticeably slower response speeds than the paid version, which somewhat affects heavy users' experience. The web version lags far behind the app in functionality and can't satisfy users who prefer working on a computer. Although character creation has a low barrier to entry, making a truly interesting, distinctive Bot still requires solid prompt design skills — the quality of characters in the community is mixed. Chinese language support exists, but characters that mix Chinese and English can sometimes become logically confused.

FAQ

Q: What's the difference between Chai and Character.AI?
A: Chai's filtering policy is looser, allowing freer personality expression for characters; Character.AI offers stronger character immersion, and its models are better at maintaining persona consistency than Chai. Chai's community discovery mechanism also feels more like an "AI social network."

Q: Does Chai have a web version?
A: Yes, but it's less complete than the app. If you need the full creation and management features, we recommend using the iOS or Android app.

Q: How many messages can I send per day on the free plan?
A: Chai's free plan has no hard daily message limit, but it includes more ads and slower response speeds. The official website hasn't published specific technical limits; actual experience prevails.

Q: Does Chai save my chat history with AI characters?
A: Yes. Chai saves conversation history to maintain the characters' context memory. Users can manage or delete chat history in settings. We recommend not sharing sensitive personal information in conversations.
